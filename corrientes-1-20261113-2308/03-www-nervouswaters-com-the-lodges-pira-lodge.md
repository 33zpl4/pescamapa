The Kautapen Group
Esteros del Iberá Reserve—from the Guaraní meaning “Bright Waters”—is the second-largest freshwater wetland in South America, after Brazil’s Pantanal. Spanning more than 3 million acres, this vast ecosystem is a maze of gin-clear creeks, shallow flats, lakes, swamps, and lagoons.
Set in northern Argentina’s Corrientes Province, Pirá Lodge places anglers at the southern edge of this immense wetland, where the marsh and Corriente River ecosystems converge. It is a rich, dynamic bottleneck of habitat—and here, the dorado is king.
VIEW BROCHURE
Why Choose Pirá Lodge?
In the search of gold.
The world’s first lodge (since 1999) built exclusively for fly fishing for golden dorado.
Direct access to the Iberá Marshlands Provincial Reserve—nearly 3 million acres of protected, clear-water wilderness.
The only dorado destination in the world where anglers fish from state of the-art Hell’s Bay skiffs, just as on a saltwater flat.
True poling and wading opportunities—rare in the freshwater dorado world.
⁠A dock just steps from your room, allowing guests to walk to the boats each day.
THE FISHERY
⁠Dorado in the 5–20+ lb range, with both resident and migratory fish throughout the season.
Crystal-clear water that enables both blind and true sight-casting.
Three distinct environments: the creeks and lagoons of the Marsh, the fast structure of the Headwaters, and the broad Corriente River flowing 150 miles to the Paraná.
Multiple techniques: streamers, mice, skaters, floating/intermediate/sinking lines, swing presentations—and the option to spin fish when desired.
A fishery alive with 350+ bird species, mammals, and constant movement— life surrounds every session on the water.
THE LODGE EXPERIENCE — WHERE PIRA SETS THE STANDARD
Best-in-class accommodations for a dorado lodge, purpose-built by leading architects and designed for comfort, privacy, and space.
Exceptional service delivered by a highly trained, attentive team that elevates every aspect of the stay.
Refined cuisine with an à la carte menu, blending regional flavors with fresh ingredients and paired with an excellent selection of Argentine wines.
A massage room/spa area for post-fishing recovery and relaxation.
Beautiful saltwater swimming pool overlooking the wetlands.
An elevated lookout overlooking the Marsh, the perfect place to light a fire and share a sunset toast after the evening fishing session—one of Pira’s most memorable rituals.
ADDITIONAL ADVANTAGES
Private 5,000-ft grass airstrip for direct charter access.
Local bilingual guides born and raised in Iberá, with unmatched knowledge of the Marsh and its dorado.
Top-tier skiffs and curated loaner equipment available at no extra cost.
Excellent non-angling activities: horseback riding, birdwatching, night wildlife outings, and stargazing in one of Argentina’s clearest skies.
A destination ideal for anglers, couples, and anyone seeking an elevated dorado experience in Argentina’s most dynamic freshwater environment.
Accommodations
Built with exquisite Corrientes style and pride, Pirá Lodge enjoys a spectacular panoramic view of the vast wetlands. It accommodates up to 12 guests in seven deluxe rooms, each with a private entrance, private bath, two double beds, ceiling fans, and air conditioning.
A separate building, attached by covered walkways and open-air patios, accommodates the spacious living room, bar, and dining room — each with tall doors that open to the wide veranda. Pirá Lodge was designed by awardwinning architects to respect traditional regional elegance—offering all of the comforts and luxuries of a contemporary hotel. Relax and enjoy a cocktail at any hour, cool off in the outdoor saltwater swimming pool after a rewarding day of fishing, or unwind in the dedicated massage room available for guests.
Season
What time of the year should I come?
Pirá Lodge is located on the shores of the Iberá Marshland Nature Reserve, one of the largest wetlands on Earth, with more than 3.2 million acres of aquatic wilderness. Here, clear waters filter and meander through ever-changing channels, islands, lagoons, and streams.
One of the most remarkable characteristics of the Iberá is its stability—despite seasonal fluctuations, the water typically remains remarkably clear through both high and low water periods. Our fishing season is year-round, and our tactics adapt to changing conditions and fish behavior, ensuring excellent opportunities to target dorado in every season.
SPRING (Sept. – late Dec.)
Spring begins as warm fronts heat the waters of the marsh. The ecosystem comes alive—vegetation flourishes, wildlife is abundant, and dorado become increasingly active.
