What people ask about Río Bravo
What does a week at Río Bravo cost, and what does it include?
USD 3,600 per angler on double occupancy, for four full days of fishing and five nights. It covers airport meet and assist, all ground transport, a deluxe double rooms with private bathroom, all gourmet meals, all beverages including Argentine wines and beers, professional guides, fishing permits, WiFi, swimming pool and jacuzzi.
What is not included?
International and domestic airfare, fishing tackle rental at USD 35 / USD 50 a day, gratuities to house staff and guides.
How do you get to Río Bravo Lodge?
You fly into Santa Fe or Paraná airport, an hour's flight from Buenos Aires, and transfer  by road to the lodge. You are met at the airport, and all ground transportation is included in the week.
When is the season, and how is the week structured?
The season runs August to April. You arrive in the afternoon and settle in, fish four full days on the Paraná for golden dorado on bait or fly with a gourmet lunch on one of the islands, and check out at 11 am on the sixth day.
Do you fish from boats?
Yes. The boats are moored at the lodge's own dock on the bank of the Paraná, so the fishing starts a few minutes after breakfast. Our guides were born on this river!
