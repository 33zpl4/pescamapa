The Lodge
A Wilderness Retreat in Argentina's Last Great Wild Landscape.
More than just a fishing lodge, Uguay is a place where every sunrise, every boat ride, and every wildlife encounter becomes part of an unforgettable adventure. Surrounded by more than 150 acres of protected native forest, grasslands, and marshes within the private Guasú-kua Nature Reserve, the lodge sits on the edge of one of South America's largest freshwater ecosystems and serves as one of the gateways to the Iberá Wetlands.
The lodge accommodates a maximum of four guests across six comfortable rooms with private bathrooms. A professional chef prepares regional cuisine and fine Argentine wines every evening. After a full day on the water guests gather in the dining room, sitting room, and bar for a relaxed evening surrounded by one of Argentina's most extraordinary natural landscapes.

The Fishing
Exclusive Access to Fernández Lagoon. Golden Dorado in the Heart of the Iberá Wetlands.
Uguay Lodge provides exclusive access to Fernández Lagoon through the lodge’s private channel, reaching untouched waters where Golden Dorado and wildlife exist in complete harmony. This is one of the most pristine Golden Dorado environments accessible through the Pointer Fly Fishing program and the private channel access ensures that guests are fishing water that has not been pressured by other operations.
The program is beginner-friendly and can be adapted for guests interested in learning or combining fly fishing and spin casting techniques. Whether you are an experienced fly fisher targeting the Dorado on large streamers or a first-time visitor discovering this extraordinary fish for the first time, the guides at Uguay have the knowledge and patience to make every session on the water genuinely productive.
The Golden Dorado is one of the most remarkable freshwater game fish in the world. A large head with powerful jaws filled with sharp teeth, extraordinary golden coloration, and a fighting character defined by explosive runs, high jumps, and tarpon-like acrobatics. Average size ranges from 3 to 10 kilograms with the largest recorded specimens reaching 31 kilograms. The Dorado lives in warm freshwater habitats and feeds aggressively on schooling prey fish, particularly the sabalo. Its aggressive nature, stamina, and visual spectacle in the air have made it one of the most sought-after freshwater fly fishing targets in South America.
Wire Tippet — Mandatory on Every Single Fly Without Exception
Wire tippet is required on every fly at Uguay Lodge without exception. Stainless steel knottable wire, minimum 8 inches in length and 15 to 30 pound test strength. The Golden Dorado's teeth cut monofilament on the first contact every time. This applies to every fly, every session, every day of the program. See the Gear and Equipment section below for full specifications.

Our professional chef works passionately every day to delight guests with Argentine and international dishes alongside fine Argentine wines. The Iberá Wetlands
One of South America's Great Wild Places. The Fishing Is Extraordinary. The Wildlife Is Unforgettable.
The Iberá Wetlands cover approximately 13,000 square kilometers in Corrientes Province making them one of the largest freshwater wetlands in the world and one of the most biodiverse ecosystems in South America. Uguay Lodge sits within the private Guasú-kua Nature Reserve on the edge of this extraordinary landscape, surrounded by more than 150 acres of protected native forest, grasslands, and marshes.
The wildlife encountered from the lodge and from the fishing boats is genuinely remarkable. Yacaré caimans on the banks of the private channel. Capybaras in the marsh grass. Marsh deer moving through the native forest. An extraordinary diversity of bird species including jabiru storks, roseate spoonbills, and the full range of wetland birds that make Corrientes one of the most celebrated birdwatching destinations in South America.
This is not background scenery. The wildlife of the Iberá Wetlands is one of the defining elements of the Uguay experience and anglers who arrive focused entirely on the fishing consistently leave equally moved by the natural world surrounding them on every boat ride to and from the fishing grounds.
Gear and Equipment
The Same Requirements as Any Golden Dorado Program. Wire on Every Fly Without Exception.
The gear requirements at Uguay Lodge are identical to those at San Javier Lodge and Posada Uguay. The full gear guide for Golden Dorado fishing is available on the Fly Fishing Gear and Equipment page. Key requirements are summarized here.
Rods
Six, seven, eight, or nine weight fast action rods recommended so that large streamers with lead eyes do not hinder casting. Bring a minimum of two rods. No replacements available at the lodge.
Reels
Large arbor reel with a reliable smooth drag. Minimum 150 yards of 20 pound backing. Large arbor preferred.
Lines
Tropical fly lines required. One floating line and one clear or sinking tip line. Standard trout lines will not perform adequately in the subtropical conditions of the Iberá Wetlands.
Leaders and Wire
Nine-foot leaders finishing at minimum 20 to 30 pound test. Stainless steel knottable wire on every fly, minimum 8 inches, 15 to 30 pound test. Mandatory without exception. See the wire tippet callout box in The Fishing section above.
Flies
Two dozen Dorado streamers in various patterns. Sizes mostly 3/0 with a few 4/0 and a few smaller 2s. Heavy lead eyes and bead chain eyes. Dark patterns and combinations with black throughout, plus lighter or white patterns for sand flat situations. Flies are available through the lodge. Tackle rental is not included. Contact Jazmin before travel to discuss fly supply options.

Getting Here
Esteros del Iberá, Corrientes Province | Corrientes Airport CNQ or Posadas
Most international flights to Argentina land in Buenos Aires Ezeiza Airport EZE. Upon arrival at Ezeiza you will need to clear customs and transfer to Jorge Newbery Airfield AEP for your domestic connection. Allow at least three to four hours between your international arrival and domestic departure. The transfer between the two airports typically takes 30 to 50 minutes but can take up to one hour in traffic. Our team organizes private ground transfers and hotel reservations in Buenos Aires for guests who need them.
