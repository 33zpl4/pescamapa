Why Patagonia Outfitters

Since 1978, we have been the first to bring fishermen from abroad to experience the thrill of Patagonia fly fishing. Trust our expertise and join us for an unforgettable angling adventure.

About Us

Our own living legend

Jorge Trucco, a native of Argentina, is a true pioneer of fly fishing in Argentina and Patagonia. He has guided and formed deep personal relationships with fly fishing legends such as Lefty Kreh, Mel Kreiger, Art Lee, and Billy Pate, making him an industry leader in South America.
