Patagonia Argentina Fishing License 2026/27: Cost and Validity
Official rates for the Patagonian fishing permit for the 2026/27 season, for foreigners and residents, where it is valid, how long it lasts, and what is charged separately.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
Non-resident foreigners: ARS 50,000 per day, ARS 140,000 for 10 days, and ARS 280,000 for the season (2026/27 rates, in pesos).
Residents in Argentina: ARS 12,500 per day, ARS 25,000 for 10 days, and ARS 50,000 for the season.
The same permit is valid in Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, and the Southern National Parks; Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego have their own.
The fishing season runs from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027 inclusive; the season permit is valid until 31 October 2027.
No charge: people over 65, retirees, pensioners, children up to 12 years old, and people with disabilities with official accreditation.

Short answer. To fish in Argentine Patagonia in the 2026/27 season, a non-resident foreigner pays ARS 50,000 for one day, ARS 140,000 for 10 days, or ARS 280,000 for the season, and a resident pays ARS 12,500, ARS 25,000, or ARS 50,000. The permit is the same in Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, and the Southern National Parks.

Who does not pay for the permit?
No permit fee for people over 65, retirees and pensioners, children up to 12 years old, and people with disabilities with official accreditation (resident category). Residents aged 13 to 17 pay ARS 25,000 for the season.
Where is the Patagonian permit valid?
Permits issued by Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, and the National Parks Administration, with these categories and rates, are the only ones valid for fishing in all waters of those four jurisdictions. Each province also has its own annexes with exceptions by river and lake: check them before fishing a specific water. Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego do not use this permit: they have their own, with their own rates (see guides for https://pescamapa.com/en/patagonia/santa-cruz/fishing-in-rio-gallegos/ and https://pescamapa.com/en/patagonia/tierra-del-fuego/fishing-in-rio-grande/).
How long is the permit valid and when is the season?
Fishing season: from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027 inclusive. Each province’s annexes list exceptions (waters that open or close on other dates).
Season permit: valid from the date of issue until 31 October 2027.
One-day and 10-day permits: valid for the dates indicated on the permit.
Hours: fishing is only allowed during daylight hours.
What do I need to bring?
The permit is personal and non-transferable, must have all fields completed, and must be carried and shown whenever requested by authorities, along with an identity document. In Chubut, the digital permit managed through the DINO platform can be re-downloaded for free if lost.
How much is the permit for a one-week trip?
For a foreign angler fishing 6 to 7 days, the 10-day permit (ARS 140,000) is cheaper than six daily permits (ARS 300,000) and less than the season permit (ARS 280,000) if not returning.
