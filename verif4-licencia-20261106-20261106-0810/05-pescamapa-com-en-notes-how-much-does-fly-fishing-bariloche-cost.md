How much does a day of fly fishing cost in Bariloche and the Limay River (2026/27)
Price for a guided fishing day on the Limay River: in US dollars and Argentine pesos, what's included, license cost, and what changes if there are two anglers.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
A full day of guided fly fishing on the Limay with boat costs between US$ 500 and US$ 545 (one angler), depending on the outfitter.
For two anglers in the same boat, one outfitter lists US$ 520: the second angler adds only US$ 20.
In pesos, a guide on a booking platform lists $ 666,000 per day for up to two people (March 2027).
The fishing license is usually not included. Official rates 2026/27, in pesos: foreigners $ 50,000 per day, $ 140,000 for 10 days, and $ 280,000 for the season; residents $ 12,500, $ 25,000, and $ 50,000.
The best season according to one outfitter is from November to May.

Short answer. A full day of guided fly fishing on the Limay River, about 45 minutes from Bariloche, costs between US$ 500 and US$ 545 per boat, and $ 666,000 in pesos on a booking platform (up to two people). The fishing license is almost always paid separately.

How much for two anglers?
With the outfitter that lists both rates, one angler pays US$ 500 and two anglers in the same boat pay US$ 520 total: the second angler adds only US$ 20. Per person, going as a pair is almost half price (our calculation: US$ 260 per person).
How much is the fishing license?
The Patagonian permit is valid in Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, and National Parks, and the 2026/27 rates are official, in pesos: non-resident foreigners, $ 50,000 per day, $ 140,000 for 10 days, and $ 280,000 for the season; residents, $ 12,500, $ 25,000, and $ 50,000 (regulations updated 26 October 2026).
