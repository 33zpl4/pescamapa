WE ARE JURASSIC LAKE ®
Jurassic Lake® is the original world-class fly fishing lodge located on Strobel Lake, one of the most remote and wild places of Southern Argentine Patagonia. Famous for its incredible trophy rainbow trout fishing in pristine waters, the lodge is strategically located on a protected bay within walking distance of the mouth of the Barrancoso River, Strobel Lake’s only significant tributary. This inlet is home to the most productive waters of the entire lake and is easily one of the very best trophy rainbow trout fisheries in the world.

SEASONS AT JURASSIC LAKE
When is the best time to visit Jurassic Lake Lodge?
SPRING/EARLY SEASON
October to Early January
The Spring arrives quickly in Patagonia and during runoff the huge trout of Lago Strobel head to the Rio Barrancoso and Moro Creek to feed and spawn. The sheer volume of fish around at this time can be astounding.
Learn More
SUMMER/MID SEASON
Mid January to mid March
Fishing during the austral summer can be amazing. Anglers sight cast to massive fish cruising the shoreline of Lago Strobel and near the mouth of the Rio Barrancoso.
Learn more
FALL/LATE SEASON
Mid March to May
As Fall sets in rain and cool temperatures return to Patagonia. Fish begin to feed heavily in anticipation of the coming winter. For anglers willing to wait, the end of our season can provide incredible fishing. It's a favorite time for guests to visit looking to hook a chrome bright monster.
