ESTANCIA LAGUNA VERDE
The world’s largest rainbow trout. Do we have your attention? Located on the rugged, southern coast of Lago Strobel (Jurassic Lake), Estancia Laguna Verde is a remote Estancia and fly fishing Lodge of southern patagonia, providing access to the world’s most distinguished rainbow trout fishery—knowledgeable guides, an abundance of enormous fish, comfortable accommodations, and extraordinary cuisine and wines—this is the destination for anglers looking to catch the biggest rainbow trout of their life while experiencing a one of a kind fly fishing adventure like no other.
