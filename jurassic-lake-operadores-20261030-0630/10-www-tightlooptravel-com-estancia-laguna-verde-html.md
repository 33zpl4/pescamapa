Estancia Laguna Verde
​Lago Strobel - AKA Jurassic Lake
TLT Signature Destination - Unparalled fly fishing for rainbow trout.
The most complete and flexible fly fishing program on Lago Strobel (aka Jurassic Lake) and the Rio Barrancoso. Owners Beto and Lucho Alba have created one of the most spectacular remote fly fishing experiences in the world. Fish miles of private water with top professional guides, stay in comfort in the well-appointed lodge, and enjoy delicious meals with an extensive selection of fine Argentine wines.
Fishing
The Lodge
Getting Here
Exclusive access to more than 20 kilometers of Strobel Lake, more than 10 kilometers of the best fishing area on Barrancoso River, plus Lago Verde and some smaller creeks and lagoons.
Sight fishing in glass clear water along the shorelines to cruising fish using floating lines, slow stripping, small to medium size streamers and nymphs, skating big foam flies and mouse patterns, or even hanging scuds and small nymphs from big dries. This is adrenaline-rush fishing, knowing you can hook a monster on any cast. (Fish here are measured in kilos, not inches.)
And the Barrancoso River is ridiculous, better than Alaska for stalking and sight-casting to huge bows in skinny water. And when you hook up, hold on amigo, that fish is going ballistic.
​
The guides are top professionals who know each area of the lake and river perfectly. They are patient instructors and good companions who make great fishing an even better overall experience.
This is truly a unique and unforgettable fly fishing journey.​
The hospitality at the lodge is second to none. The owners, Beto and Lucho, are often at the lodge to host their guests, and our friends Nico Botting and lodge hostess Karina and their staff are always on hand to greet you and make you feel right at home. You can relax in the large living area and bar with wood-burning fireplace, or out on the deck overlooking Lago Verde.
Most meals are served in the connected dining area, though an asado at the owners home on the property happens once a week and is a special occasion. The Argentine cuisine is delicious, everything home-made, and accompanied by a fine selection of wines from Mendoza and Salta.
Large spacious guest rooms (with king bed or two queens) are accessed via a railed deck that runs from the wader and fishing gear room, to each guest room.
Single rooms can be booked at no extra cost.
All bedrooms have a private bathroom with shower and tub and plenty of room for all your gear.
By Plane
Commercial flight from Buenos Aires to El Calafate (3 hours 15 minutes). You will be greeted on arrival and driven to your hotel for overnight. We will suggest hotels and some great restaurants in the charming town of El Calafate to kick off your visit to Patagonia. And we can make all the reservations for you.
Land Transportation
The next morning, after an early breakfast, our driver will pick you up at the hotel and drive you to Estancia Laguna Verde. The scenic 5 hour drive takes you past the glaciers of Mt. Fitzroy and through the vast open plains of Patagonia, filled with guanaco and laced with rivers and lakes.
Travel Notes: If you are visiting Torres del Paine National Park in Chile, we can arrange a private vehicle and driver to bring you to El Calafate and Estancia Laguna Verde.

Plan Your Trip
Upon arrival at the lodge, you will be greeted with a welcome drink and snacks and settled into your room. Then get ready for a hearty lunch with wine, beer, or your beverage of choice. Want to get out fishing? No problem, Lago Verde, just below the lodge, awaits.
Normal daily routine - breakfast is served starting around 8 am (coffee is ready earlier). There is a breakfast special and a buffet of cereal, fruit and fresh breads, or you can order something special. Your guides will be ready to take you to Lago Strobel or the Barrancoso River whenever you're ready. You'll have a chance to fish all areas of the lake and river during your stay. Lunches are usually serviced at the lake, in a comfortable wind-protected quincho where you can relax and share fishing stories.
​Each day when you return from fishing, apre appetizers will be ready in the living room, or out on the deck. Enjoy with your favorite beer, wine, or cocktail, followed by dinner.
In terms of gear - The lodge can provide waders, wading shoes, rods, reel, lines, flies, leaders and tippets, but feel free to take your own gear. We will talk this over with you before your trip, and will send you detailed pre-arrival information with our gear, clothing and other packing recommendations.
