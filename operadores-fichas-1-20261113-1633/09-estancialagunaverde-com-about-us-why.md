Why choose
ESTANCIA LAGUNA VERDE LODGE
Choosing the right fishing operator at Strobel lake (aka Jurassic lake) is
essential, especially when you consider the enormity of the area, the
characteristics of the fishing and the type of experience you want!
◦ Since 2008, offering the world a one of a kind fishing adventure.
◦ When you book a trip with Estancia Laguna Verde, either directly or through our selected list of professional angling travel agencies that represent us, you are booking with the OWNERS of the Lodge and Estancia. No leases or doubtful exploitation permits. No third parties or risky permissions needed.
◦ Run and hosted by its owners, who are passionate fly fishermen that love and preserve this magnificent and pristine fishery.
◦ By fly fishermen for fly fishermen!
◦ The most flexible and varied fly fishing program in all southern patagonia!!!
◦ No combat fishing at ELV! Unlike other operators at Jurassic lake, at Estancia Laguna Verde there´s NO fishing elbow to elbow with 12/14 other guys by your side, throwing huge jigs and gear next to your fly.
◦ Strategically located on the south, but also east and north coasts of Strobel Lake, with exclusive access and fishing of 45.000 acres property, plus leasing to its neighbor to the east for massive Strobel lake shoreline access and roads.
◦ 10 km of Barrancoso River, in its best fishing part, with 3 sections, and 4 beats each one, two (2) hanging bridges, adding even more adventure and fishing.
◦ 12 km of Moro Creek, a slow-flowing, meandering stream, with spring creek-like style and fishing (runs only in our spring), favourite among early season visitors
◦ 12 smaller lakes and lagoons chained by Moro creek.
◦ Fly fishing only lodge.
◦ Top-notch hardcore guides addicted to sight fishing.
◦ One guide per two anglers ratio (single guiding available).
◦ World-class lodging, with single rooms available at no extra cost.
◦ Personal attention of our friendly and professional staff.◦
◦ Improved and agile Road System, of over 50 km, guarantees you will be in the water in no time, as well as unlimited combinations of programs in each day of fishing.
◦ Excellent cuisine provided by our exclusive chef and sous chef. Carefully planned menu of typical Argentinian meals, great “asados” (bbq´s), famous Patagonian lamb, the classic “empanada night» (a true Argentina classic) and the finest international dishes cooked with vegetables from our organic vegetable gardening.
◦ Outstanding wine cellar with renowned wines from Mendoza and other parts of Argentina, personally selected by us. Once a week, we do special tastings in our Cava of more than 4000 bottles paired with delicious aged cheese at the Estancia.
◦ Obsession for details!! We share the same passion and enthusiasm that moved you to travel all the way from home to southern Patagonia for the experience of a lifetime, and we want to make sure every detail of your stay is well taken care of so that you have a truly unforgettable fly fishing experience.
