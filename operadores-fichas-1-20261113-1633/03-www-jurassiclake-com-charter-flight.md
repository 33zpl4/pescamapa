CHARTER FLIGHT INFORMATION
Getting to Jurassic Lake Lodge has never been easier. Our charter flight from El Calafate lands at a private airstrip just 15 minutes from the lodge, offering the fastest and most convenient way to reach one of the world’s premier fly fishing destinations. In just one hour, you'll arrive, ready to experience some of the best fly fishing in the world.
Operated by a trusted third-party company, this scenic flight provides breathtaking aerial views of Patagonia and ensures a smooth, hassle-free transfer.
For those who prefer to soak in Patagonia’s breathtaking scenery by road, every package includes a ground transfer along the legendary Route 40—a spectacular 6-hour journey through remote and stunning landscapes.
Regular charter flights are scheduled every Saturday at a fixed rate*, but private flights can be arranged upon request on other days or to alternative destinations. Whether you choose to fly or take the scenic drive, getting to Jurassic Lake Lodge is part of the adventure!
​
*The charter flight is not included in the standard package. Depending on the date, a fixed rate per passenger may be available. Otherwise, the total cost of the flight will be determined based on the number of passengers taking the flight.
For more details on availability and pricing, please contact us.
​
Aircraft & Capacity
The flight is operated on a Britten-Norman Islander aircraft.
The plane has an 8-passenger capacity without luggage, but we typically fly with 6 passengers to allow for baggage.
Usually, the aircraft makes two flights each transfer day.
Boarding priority for the first flight is based on booking date (first booked, first assigned).
Luggage Weight Restrictions
The recommended weight limit per passenger is 15 kg (33 lbs) for checked baggage and 7 kg (15 lbs) for carry-on. We encourage guests to stay within this limit to ensure a safe and efficient flight.
However, if the aircraft's safety weight limit is exceeded or guests bring more weight than the allowed limit, we will ask them to take only what they need for the day, and the rest of their luggage will be transported separately by land. Extra baggage or luggage exceeding the limit will be driven to the lodge and will arrive later the same day.
We recommend packing efficiently and bringing only essential items on the flight. (See Pre-Trip Information for more details.)
Flight Schedule & Departure Time
Flights are available at a fixed rate on certain Saturdays.
The plane is also available upon request on other days and for flights to destinations beyond El Calafate.
Departure is typically early in the morning, but the exact schedule is subject to weather conditions.
We confirm hotel pick-up times for the airport shuttle the day before departure.
Return Flight & Connection Considerations
We strongly advise not booking any departing flights from El Calafate to Buenos Aires before 3:00 PM on your return day.
While guests may choose to book earlier flights, there is a risk of missing the connection due to possible weather-related delays.
Important Notice
This charter flight service is provided by an independent third-party company.
Jurassic Lake Lodge assumes no responsibility for changes, delays, or cancellations related to this service.
