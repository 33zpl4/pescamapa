Set's Golden Dorado Experience:
Unleash the Thrill of the Hunt!
Welcome to Set’s Golden Dorado Experience, where fishing dreams come to life in the heart of Argentina! Immerse yourself in the adventure of a lifetime as you pursue the legendary Golden Dorado, guided by our seasoned experts. With three distinctive lodges and a promise of unmatched experiences, Set’s is your gateway to the ultimate angling escapade.
Sunset Dreams and Trophy Streams: The Allure of Set's Golden Dorado Experience
Picture this: Golden Dorado dancing on the end of your line, the untamed beauty of Argentina as your backdrop, and the thrill of adventure pulsating through your veins. Set’s Golden Dorado Experience isn’t just a fishing trip; it’s a journey into the heart of angling paradise. Join us and discover why our lodges are the go-to destination for anglers seeking more than just a catch – they seek an unforgettable experience.
Three lodges, each a different gateway to this specie.
jetu’u Lodge
Unleash the Action!
Located in the Ysoro wetland, Jetu’u Lodge offers unparalleled fly fishing action. Experience two unique environments, free from boat traffic, with marshes teeming with 2-7 pound Dorado. Cast surface flies for non-stop action or head to the Parana River for a chance at a massive trophy. Ideal for beginners, Jetu’u combines comfort with unlimited fishing excitement.
Book Now for Action-Packed Adventures!
Alondra'i
Discover the Wild!
Immerse yourself in the vast Ibera wetlands, South America’s second-largest natural water reservoir. Fish the “gin clear” waters amidst capybaras, jaguars, and over 200 bird species. La Alondra’i Lodge in Conception offers stability and comfort, allowing you to explore different waters every day. Get ready for action on surface patterns and encounters with 7-15 pound Dorado in a truly wild environment.
Embark on a Wild Fishing Expedition!
Itati Lodge
Pursue the Trophy!
Set on the upper Parana River, Itati Lodge provides a chance for the ultimate trophy Dorado experience. Fish the clear waters near the Yacyreta dam, targeting the largest Golden Dorado in South America. Secluded and private, Itati is perfect for anglers seeking sizes over quantity. This is no place for beginners, but if you’re up for the challenge, the rewards are grand.
Book Your Trophy Pursuit Today!

About Set Fly Fishing
At SET fly fishing, we embody our passion as anglers, infusing every journey with our energy. Our goal is simple – to create extraordinary experiences through our unique lodges, our dedicated team, and our commitment to nature conservation.
With roots in both Argentina and the USA, we’ve been on this journey for over 20 years. Our guides, deeply connected to local culture, are our greatest assets. Alongside them, we manage distinct fly fishing lodges, each a gateway to a different experience.
Yet, above all, it’s about people. Join us in celebrating Patagonia’s essence, and the enigma of the golden dorado. We leave only footprints and take memories, ensuring these treasures endure.
Welcome to SET: Where passion sparks adventure, and people shape our journey.
