Choosing the Right Lodge for Your Dream Adventure.
August 9, 2024
Remember when you were young and you entered a huge toy shop? Maybe if you showed how much you truly want it, they would leave you have just one toy. What a tough decision when everything showed so much potential. Which one should you choose, and which one should you leave behind? Why couldn’t you just take all of them? Choosing your next SET Fly Fishing lodge can feel a bit like that, fortunately just a bit. The awesome feeling that you can get the thing that you want the most, making you the happiest man alive, that much is similar, but luckily there is no wrong pick and there is no mandatory pick only one!
Every Set lodge offers unique experiences and stunning environments, making it hard to choose just one. Hey! You can combine or go trying one after an other through out the years. Join the family!
Here at Set fly fishing we take pride in making you feel at home, top-notch amenities, expert guides, comfortable accommodations, and exceptional fishing opportunities. Designed to provide personalized experiences, whether you’re a novice angler or a seasoned veteran.
Lodges and staff have this in common, fisheries are absolutely unique. In Northern Patagonia, world-class trout fishing. Spring Creek Lodge stands out for its vast complementary activities for non-fishing partners, including hiking, birdwatching, and endless ways to experience and enjoy true Patagonia! This makes it an ideal destination for anglers traveling with family or friends who might not fish. The other lodges in Patagonia are equally impressive, providing access to diverse fishing locations and experiences, each offering something special.
For those seeking the thrill of Golden Dorado fishing, our lodges in the Corrientes province provide unparalleled experiences. Action in Jetu’u at the Ysoro Wetlands, wilderness in La Alondra’i at the Iberá Wetlands national park, and trophies in Itatí Lodge on the upper Paraná River. Perfect for anglers looking to test their skills against one of the most aggressive freshwater species on the planet. Set’s golden dorado experience offers you the chance to combine 2 out of 3 fisheries for 1 unbelievable week for the ultimate fishing program.
Feel free to reach out to the Set team! We will personally find the perfect match for you.
When do you want to travel? When can you? Fishing buddies, family? Introducing grand children to fly fishing? Outdoorsy adventure? Wonderfull lodging? Small intimate creeks, extraordinary floats, matching the hatch, fiercely stripping streamers, numbers? Size? You name it!
Meet me halfway honey! What about some leisure travel through Argentina and some fishing?
Set team adds up more than 20 years of passionate and adventurous people. Dedicated to hospitality, fishing, scouting rowing, camping, cooking hosting and yes… some office work as well.
We all love what we do and are enthusiastic as hell about sharing with anyone that trust us with their next fishing adventure.
You are most welcomed, tell us what you are looking for, we will deliver, because at Set fly fishing it’s all about the people!
