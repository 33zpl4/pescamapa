How much does a dorado fishing trip in Argentina cost: all-inclusive lodges by number of nights (2026/27)
Per person rates for dorado lodges in Corrientes, Iberá, the Paraná in Santa Fe, and the Delta, with what's included and the cost per fishing day.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
An all-inclusive lodge on the Upper Paraná in Corrientes costs from US$ 4,050 (3 nights, 2 fishing days) to US$ 7,950 (7 nights, 6 days) per person in double occupancy.
Each extra fishing day lowers the price per day: from US$ 2,025 with 2 days to US$ 1,325 with 6 days at the same lodge.
Iberá Wetlands: one lodge lists 7 nights and 6 days from US$ 6,400 per person, in a single room.
Paraná in Santa Fe: 4 fishing days from US$ 3,600; a special of 3 days and 4 nights at US$ 2,500 on selected dates from December 2026 to February 2027.
Fishing licenses and transfers are usually not included: add them in.

Short answer. An all-inclusive dorado trip at a lodge in Argentina costs, per person in a double room, from US$ 4,050 for 3 nights and 2 fishing days to US$ 7,950 for 7 nights and 6 days on the Upper Paraná (Corrientes). In Iberá and the Paraná in Santa Fe there are options from US$ 2,500 to US$ 6,400. For a cheaper trip, see the friends’ trip at an inn
(from US$ 605).

How many days are best?
The price per fishing day drops with each extra day: in the ladder above it goes from US$ 2,025 with 2 days to US$ 1,325 with 6. If budget is tight, 3 nights and 2 days is the minimum worth considering; if you’re coming from far away, 5 nights and 4 days already covers a bad weather day without losing your week.
What is usually not included in the price?
The fishing license: in Corrientes you get it online; some lodges include it, others don’t.
Flights and transfers to the lodge, unless listed as included.
Tips for guides and staff.
Single supplement: if you travel alone and want your own room and boat, the surcharge can be up to 60%.
