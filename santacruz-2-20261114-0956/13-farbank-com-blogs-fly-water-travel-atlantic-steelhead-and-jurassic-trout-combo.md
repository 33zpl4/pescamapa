Atlantic Steelhead and Jurassic Trout Combo
Jake Crawford February 21, 2024
Combine two of the best trophy trout fisheries in southern Patagonia with Atlantic Steelhead at Los Plateados Lodge on the mighty Rio Santa Cruz with trophy Rainbow trout at incomparable Jurassic Lake Lodge, both easily accessible in Argentina’s Santa Cruz Province.

Atlantic Steelhead and Jurassic Trout
Los Plateados Lodge: April 6 - 12, 2024
Jurassic Lake Lodge: April 13 - 16, 2024
Two spots available.
Sample Itinerary:
April 5: Fly from Buenos Aires to Rio Gallegos to overnight at hotel.
April 6: Get picked up and transferred to Los Plateados Lodge. Fish half day upon arrival.
April 7 - 11: Full days of guided fishing for Atlantic Steelhead at Los Plateados Lodge.
April 12: Fish half day. Ground transfer to El Calafate. Overnight in El Calafate.
April 13: Get picked up and ground transfer to Jurassic Lake Lodge. Fish upon arrival.
April 14 - 16: Full days of guided fishing for trophy rainbow trout at Jurassic Lake Lodge.
April 17: Depart lodge for El Calafate.
Los Plateados Lodge for Atlantic Steelhead:
Rate: $4,990 per person for a 6 night/6 day package
Included: Accommodations, meals and beverages including beer and wine at the lodge, transfer to/from Rio Gallegos, guided fishing.
Not Included: International or domestic airfare, hotels/meals/transfer in Buenos Aires, hotels/meals in Rio Gallegos, transfer to El Calafate ($150 pp), fishing equipment, alcohol, staff and guide gratuities.
Jurassic Lake Lodge:
Rate: $5,200 per person for a 4 night/3.5 day package *longer stay available
Included: Accommodations, meals and beverages including wine, beer and alcohol at the lodge, guided fishing, round-trip transfers to/from the lodge and El Calafate, and fishing license.
Not Included: International and domestic airfare, hotels/meals/transfers in Buenos Aires and El Calafate, fishing equipment, laundry and staff and guide gratuities.
