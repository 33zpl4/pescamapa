Santa Cruz River: Steelhead Fly Fishing, Argentina
In the wilds of Patagonia, the Santa Cruz River holds the world’s only run of Atlantic Steelhead, making it one of the rarest and most exciting fisheries on the planet. Flowing 386 kilometres from Lake Argentino to the Atlantic, this vast river offers anglers the chance to target chrome-bright fish that arrive from late February through May. Average weights run nine to ten pounds, with specimens exceeding twenty pounds landed each season. Powerful, aggressive, and acrobatic, these fish offer a test of skill and stamina few rivers can match.
Fishing is based at Los Plateados River Cabin, perched on the riverbank overlooking some of the most productive pools. With access to more than thirty named pools and only four rods hosted each week, exclusivity is assured. Anglers rotate between boat and truck access, fishing in pairs with professional guides ensuring instruction and local knowledge throughout. Slow swung flies, stripped occasionally, are often the trigger for the savage takes that make this river famous.
The Santa Cruz is broad and powerful but often fished tight to the banks. Double-handed rods in eight or nine weights matched with Skagit lines and a range of sinking tips are essential tools for covering water effectively. The team that pioneered this fishery remains dedicated to maximising every guest’s chances of connecting with these remarkable fish.
Back at the cabin, comfort comes in simplicity. A veranda overlooks the river, open fires warm the dining room, and meals feature Argentina’s finest meats paired with vegetables grown on-site.
The Santa Cruz River offers something no other destination can: the chance to pursue the planet’s only Atlantic Steelhead in the raw beauty of Patagonia. It is a holiday defined by rarity, power, and one of the most exclusive fisheries in the world.

Day 1:
Depart Any international Airport (See Details)
Arrive Buenos Aires
On arrival take a taxi to Jorge Newbery Airport where you will take a flight to Santa Cruz. A member of the team will be waiting to transfer you to your overnight hotel.
Overnight Accommodation: Rio Gallegos Hotel (Twin Rooms) B/B
Day 2: Fishing Day
Around 8:30 am you will be picked up and driven to Santa Cruz (3.5 hours). On arrival, there’s a short briefing about the coming weeks of fishing. Once everyone is ready, it will be time to hit the home pools with your guides for an afternoon fishing session.
Overnight Accommodation: Los Plateados River Cabin (Twin Rooms) B/B
Days 3 to 7: Fishing Day
For each day’s fishing, we suggest you bring double-handed rods – fly rods in 8 or 9 wt. We also recommend you bring Skagit lines with an assortment of tips for the different types of water and flies in black, blue, fuchsia and red as these make a great contrast with this milky glacier water. You will fish slowly whilst working your way downstream along these massive runs and pools. Swinging flies work very well, but don’t hesitate in stripping, as it can trigger some fierce takes.
Overnight Accommodation: Los Plateados River Cabin (Twin Rooms) B/B
Day 8: Fishing Day
On your last day, you will fish until lunchtime after which our staff will transport you to the domestic airport.
Depart Rio Gallegos, Arrive Jorge Newbery
On arrival, you will take a taxi to your hotel.
Overnight Accommodation: Buenos Aires Hotel (Twin Rooms) B/B
