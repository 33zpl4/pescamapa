Karku Fly Fishing
Fly-fishing camp on a century-old estancia 50 minutes from Río Gallegos airport, on the Río Gallegos. It takes 4 anglers a week. According to its website it has more than 40 km of exclusive water and more than 40 named pools. It sleeps guests in a cabin with two single rooms and one double, shared bathroom, solar power and Wi-Fi. It includes airport transfers, guides, breakfast, lunch and dinner, and takes care of permits; it does not include laundry, equipment, alcoholic drinks or tips. It asks for a 50% non-refundable deposit. The normal programme runs Saturday to Saturday, and the operator also arranges longer or shorter stays, subject to availability (it confirmed this to us on 13/11/2026). It asked that its rate not be published: it gives it to anglers who ask for a quote. Open rods as of 13/11/2026 (2027): 2 to 9 January (2 rods); 4 to 13 February (4 rods); 6 to 13 March (4 rods); 13 to 20 March (4 rods); 27 March to 3 April (2 rods). Ask about other dates or shorter stays.

Estancia Glencross (Solid Adventures)
Working estancia of 85,000 hectares (cattle and sheep), high in the Andes of Santa Cruz and on the border with Chile, where Solid Adventures offers fly fishing for resident brown trout and sea-run brown trout. According to its page, it has private access to the upper Río Gallegos and its tributaries Rubens and Penitente, three rivers with different scenery; in the tributaries a single-hand rod is usually used. Guests stay in the main house of the estancia. Besides fishing, the operator mentions horseback riding, hiking, condor watching and other excursions on request. Its season runs from late January to early April and it is booked as a half week or a full week. Solid Adventures offers it as an extension to a week at Las Buitreras, about a two-hour drive away according to its page. Its page shows no rate.

Lodging · Packages and trips
Estancia Las Buitreras (Solid Adventures)
Lodge on the Río Gallegos run by Solid Adventures, about a ten-minute walk from the river. According to its 2027 programme it has five double rooms and four single rooms, all with private bathrooms, and takes a maximum of 12 anglers a week. The operator describes more than 40 km of water and more than 50 named pools. It sells full weeks from Saturday to Saturday (seven nights, six guided fishing days), and on 13/11/2026 it confirmed to us that it does not run shorter stays and that January 2027 was already fully booked. You fish wading, with a single- or double-hand rod (it recommends a 7 wt 12 ft double-hand); it does not provide tackle, and the lodge shop sells flies and tackle. The guides speak English. The return transfer leaves from El Calafate domestic airport (3 to 4 hours by minibus); outside the recommended schedule it costs about US$ 300 per car. It includes the seven nights, six guided fishing days, all meals, snacks, soft drinks, wine and beer, and the fishing licence; it does not include flights, tackle, spirits or tips. It offers Glencross, another of its programmes, as an extension to the week.

How to choose a guide: what to ask before booking
Style: does the guide fish bait, spinning/baitcasting or fly? Pick one who specialises in what you want to do.
Boat and gear: what boat, how many anglers it takes, which engine (horsepower, bow electric motor)? Casting platform, fish finder? Is tackle provided?
What is included: bait, meals, fuel, lodging, transfers. Get it in writing, with the price.
Timing rules: the month decides the fishing. Ask what works on your dates, at what river level and water temperature.
The place: where the lodging is, boat ramp, distance to the fishing areas and the overall setting.
References: photos of recent trips and anglers who have been. If you can, talk to the owner or operator.
