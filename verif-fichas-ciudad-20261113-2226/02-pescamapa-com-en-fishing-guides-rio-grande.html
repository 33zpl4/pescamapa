Kau Tapen Lodge

Lodge on the Río Grande, operating since 1984 according to its website. It sleeps up to 10 anglers (sometimes 12) in single rooms with a bathroom. Its website publishes, for the season from 26 December 2026 to 17 April 2027, between USD 12,950 and USD 14,950 per person per week depending on the date, in a single room with a shared guide (two anglers per guide). It includes the transfer from Río Grande airport, meals and drinks, a guide and loaner equipment; it does not include flights, the fishing licence, laundry or tips. It asks for a 50% non-refundable deposit.

Lodging

Villa María Lodge

Lodge at Estancia José Menéndez (founded in 1894), on the lower Río Grande, a few miles above the tidal influence, according to its page on Nervous Waters. It takes six anglers in six single en-suite rooms, with two anglers per guide; the operator describes 20 km of private water and a rotation through new beats every morning and evening. You fish wading, with two-handed rods (it recommends 7 to 9 weight, 12 to 14 feet) or single-handed rods, and all fishing is catch and release. It lends fishing gear. Its weeks run Friday to Friday; according to the operator, the Friday-to-Friday weeks include one night at its Delta Eco Lodge (shared rooms) with one day of guided dorado fishing, subject to availability and first come, first served, with lunch, dinner, breakfast and open bar; it does not include the transfer from Ezeiza to Marina or from Marina to Aeroparque. The rate includes lodging in a single room (subject to availability), transfer from Río Grande airport, meals, wines, beer and spirits, a guide and loaner gear (rods, reels and lines). The operator focuses its season on January, February and March.

How to choose a guide: what to ask before booking

  1. Style: does the guide fish bait, spinning/baitcasting or fly? Pick one who specialises in what you want to do.
  2. Boat and gear: what boat, how many anglers it takes, which engine (horsepower, bow electric motor)? Casting platform, fish finder? Is tackle provided?
  3. What is included: bait, meals, fuel, lodging, transfers. Get it in writing, with the price.
  4. Timing rules: the month decides the fishing. Ask what works on your dates, at what river level and water temperature.
  5. The place: where the lodging is, boat ramp, distance to the fishing areas and the overall setting.
  6. References: photos of recent trips and anglers who have been. If you can, talk to the owner or operator.

Frequently asked questions about fishing guides in Río Grande (Tierra del Fuego)

How much does a fishing guide cost in Río Grande (Tierra del Fuego)?

It depends on the boat, the length of the day and how many go: almost all charge per boat and per day, not per person. We publish only the rates the provider itself shows, with the date we read them; the guide confirms the final price for your dates.

What does a guided trip include?

Usually the boat, fuel and the guide; bait, tackle, meals and the licence vary. Each listing says what is included according to the provider; ask about anything not listed before booking.

Do I need a fishing licence to go out with a guide?

Yes: the licence is personal and the angler pays for it unless the trip includes it. The Río Grande (Tierra del Fuego) guide says which one you need, what it costs and where to get it.

How do I book a guide in Río Grande (Tierra del Fuego)?

Pick a listing and tap "Check availability", or fill in the form with your group and dates. We pass the inquiry to the provider and they reply with a proposal; no fee, no commitment.

When is the sea-run brown trout season on the Río Grande?

The official season runs from 30 October 2026 to 18 April 2027. Dates for the Río Grande and its tributaries; the rest of the province closes on 2 May 2027. Dates according to Secretaría de Ambiente de Tierra del Fuego (Ministerio de Producción y Ambiente). Waters with their own rules may have other dates, and the rules change every season: read this guide's regulations. Regulations checked on 4 November 2026.
