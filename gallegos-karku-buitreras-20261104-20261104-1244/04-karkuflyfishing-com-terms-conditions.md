1. Booking and Payment Policy
Reservation and Deposit
To secure your reservation, a non-refundable deposit of 50% of the total trip cost is required at the time of booking. This deposit confirms your place and allows us to make the necessary arrangements for your stay.
Final Payment
The remaining 50% balance must be paid no later than 90 days prior to the start of your scheduled trip. Failure to make the final payment by this deadline may result in the cancellation of your booking, and your deposit will be forfeited.
Accepted Payment Methods
Payments can be made via bank transfer or any other method previously agreed upon with Karku Fly Fishing. All bank fees or transfer costs are the responsibility of the client.
2. Cancellation Policy
All cancellations or changes must be submitted in writing as soon as possible to be considered for any exceptions.
Deposits are non-refundable under any circumstances.
If you cancel your trip within 90 days or less before your arrival date, no refunds will be issued for any amount paid.
Karku Fly Fishing reserves the right to cancel any booking due to unforeseen circumstances (such as extreme weather conditions or safety concerns). In such cases, we will make every effort to reschedule your trip or issue a partial refund at our discretion.
We highly recommend purchasing travel insurance that covers trip cancellations, flight delays, medical emergencies, and weather-related interruptions.
3. Health and Special Requests
Please inform us in advance of any medical conditions, allergies, or dietary restrictions so we can do our best to accommodate your needs. While we make every effort to fulfill special requests, they cannot be guaranteed and are subject to availability and logistical feasibility.

During your stay and fishing activities, photographs and videos may be taken of you and your group. These materials may be used for promotional purposes on our website, social media, or printed materials.

Karku Fly Fishing is not responsible for any delays, cancellations, or additional costs resulting from airline issues, weather, illness, strikes, or other circumstances beyond our control.
We strongly recommend all guests obtain comprehensive travel and medical insurance that includes coverage for fishing-related activities, evacuation, and repatriation.
