Privacy policy
Last updated: 13 November 2026
Who handles your data
Pescamapa (pescamapa.com), a project of Cheyenne Justiniano and Alberto Ayala. Contact: [email protected]
.
What we collect and why
If you ask for a trip or a quote: your name, WhatsApp or email, country, dates, number of people, preferences (species, style, experience, lodging) and the message you write. We use them to reply to you. We ask the operators that fit your request without your name or contact, and give them to an operator only when you tell us to go ahead with that one. With the request we also keep which page you entered the site on and which site you came from (a search engine or an assistant, for example), to learn which content is useful.
If you are an outfitter and ask to be listed: name, contact, town and what you offer. We use them to contact you and build your listing; nothing is published without your permission.
If you send a bite-zone report: the zone (a square of about 1,000 m), the species, the day and the result. We never receive or store your exact position or your name: your exact position does not leave your phone.
To stop spam and abuse: for one hour we keep a code derived from your IP address to limit submissions, and with each bite-zone report an irreversible code derived from your IP to avoid duplicates. We do not store your IP in the clear.
What we do not do
We use no tracking cookies, advertising or analytics tools, and we do not sell your data. Your browser stores two things from the site: that you closed the language notice and, for the length of the visit, which page you entered on and which site you came from; that last part reaches us only if you send a request.
Who we share it with
With an operator, only when you tell us to go ahead with that one: your name and contact, so they can answer you. The operators we ask beforehand get your request without your name or contact.
With the services that run the site: the server it is hosted on, Cloudflare (network and security; it processes visits and IP addresses) and the email service we receive requests through.
When you view a map, your browser requests the images from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP under its own policy.
We share it with no one else, unless an authority requires it.
How long we keep it
Requests, for as long as needed to help you and follow your trip; you can ask us to delete them any time. Bite-zone reports hold no personal data and stay as information about the zone.
Your rights
You can ask us for access to, correction or deletion of your data by writing to [email protected]
. If you live elsewhere, you also have the rights your local law gives you (for example in the EU, the UK or Brazil); write to us anyway.
Under Argentine law (Law 25,326), data subjects may exercise their right of access free of charge at intervals of no less than six months, unless a legitimate interest is shown (art. 14, para. 3). The Agency for Access to Public Information (AAIP), the supervisory body under that law, handles complaints about breaches of the personal-data rules.
Changes
If we change something important, we update it here and change the date above. The rules for using the site are in the terms of use
.
