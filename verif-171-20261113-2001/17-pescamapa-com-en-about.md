About us
Who makes Pescamapa
We are two people with roots in Goya, Corrientes. We research each destination, show our sources and answer the inquiries ourselves.
Why it exists
Looking at how fishing is sold in Argentina, we kept finding the same things: prices scattered across many sites or out of date, regulations that are hard to find, and contacts that are somebody's personal WhatsApp. Pescamapa puts that information in one place, with a date and a source, and keeps it current.
The people
Alberto Ayala
Founder of MOMUTO and of other online businesses over the past 15 years. He works in digital marketing, search and AI-assistant visibility, and web development. He has family in Goya, where he spends more and more time. At Pescamapa he looks after the site and the data.
Cheyenne Justiniano
A tourism technician and tour guide. Co-founder of Bitácora de Viajes, an inbound travel agency in Goya, and a technician at the city's tourism office. She guides nature and birdwatching outings on Isla de las Damas and in the Isoró Nature Reserve. She is not a fishing guide: at Pescamapa she brings local knowledge and the relationships with the area's fishing guides and lodging.
How we work
We read the regulation in force and the operator's own page, and note the date we did. Every guide shows its sources.
What we could not confirm, we say so. A price is published only with its date.
Listings for guides and lodging built from public information are marked as unverified, and are corrected or removed when the provider asks.
In Goya
In Goya we know fishing guides and lodging in person. If your inquiry is for that area, we sort it out with local people we know.
How the site pays its way
How it works: you send one request; Pescamapa asks the operators that fit your trip and replies within 48 hours with a side-by-side comparison of the options. Reading Pescamapa is free, and so is asking us for options. If you book with one of the operators we asked, that operator pays us a commission and you pay the same as booking direct. We compare the operators that fit your trip whether or not they have an agreement with us: when they do, their listing says so, and being listed on the site does not depend on an agreement. The nature outings we link to in Goya are run by Bitácora de Viajes, Cheyenne's agency.
