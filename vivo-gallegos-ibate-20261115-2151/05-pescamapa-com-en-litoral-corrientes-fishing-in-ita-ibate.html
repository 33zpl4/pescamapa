Golden dorado fishing in Itá Ibaté, Corrientes: what is there, rules and licence

Itá Ibaté, on the Upper Paraná, lives off recreational fishing and receives mostly anglers from Brazil. What the COMIP–UNNE report says, what the one lodge with a page of its own that we found publishes, and which rules apply.

Itá Ibaté is on the Upper Paraná, on the Argentine bank of the river that separates Argentina from Paraguay. According to the report “Evaluación de la pesca recreativa en el río Paraná en tramos compartidos entre Argentina y Paraguay” (Acta Complementaria N.º 3, final report for February 2019 to January 2020; COMIP-UNNE agreement, Instituto de Ictiología del Nordeste; in Spanish), which places it at km 1375 of the river, recreational fishing was one of the municipality’s main sources of income: there were 20 mid-sized to large complexes along the shore and about 120 guides, most of them tied to those businesses. It received anglers from several provinces and from abroad, mainly from Brazil (some of the businessmen were of Brazilian origin and the Argentine ones offered their services on websites in Spanish and Portuguese), and in June the Mercosur Boga and Pacú Catch-and-Release Contest was held. That is data from 2017 to 2020: it may have changed.

This guide is short on purpose: the only lodge with a page of its own that we found is Dorados on the Fly (Pinti’s), and its page publishes no rate, season or directions. What follows is what has a source.

Key facts

What you can keep: the basic rules of Corrientes

Disposición 853/22 of the Natural Resources Directorate (Corrientes Official Gazette, 26 December 2022), as we transcribe it in our note on size and bag limits and closed seasons, applies until another act changes it. For the most sought-after species it says:

  • Dorado and surubí (spotted and striped): catch and release only. They cannot be kept.
  • Pacú: minimum size 45 cm; bag limit 1 per valid licence and a single fish per boat, no matter how many anglers are aboard.
  • Patí: minimum size 70 cm; bag limit 2.
  • Boga: minimum size 45 cm; bag limit 2 per angler with a valid licence and up to 6 per boat (if there are more anglers aboard, those 6 are shared among them).
  • Tararira (trahira): minimum size 30 cm; bag limit 5.

Species in the annex may only be moved within the town where they were caught: taking them to another destination, domestic or international, is forbidden.

Fishing licence

You need the Corrientes sport fishing licence: it is bought online on the Natural Resources portal and carried with your identity document. For people whose home is outside Argentina there is the foreign tourist licence, which is set in litres of “súper” petrol: 16 litres for one day, 20 for two, 24 for three, 28 for four and 36 for six (that is how it appears in our note on fishing in Corrientes from Brazil, which also gives the approximate value in pesos). The regional licence-recognition agreement (Santa Fe, Entre Ríos and Chaco) is for residents of those provinces and does not cover stretches under exclusively Corrientes jurisdiction. We found no rule recognising licences from other countries: visitors from abroad need the Corrientes licence.

The Dorados on the Fly page does not mention the licence: ask before you book.

Closed seasons: what happened in 2026

Recursos Naturales of Corrientes can order extraordinary closures on stretches of the Paraná, and in 2026 it did so several times. This is the timeline and who reported it:

  • 29 May 2026. Disposición 618/26 banned all fishing (sport, commercial and subsistence) between km 1232 and 1238, from the Paraná–Paraguay confluence to Los Cables, because of a concentration of surubí (according to El Litoral and the notice from the Corrientes Ministry of Tourism, both in Spanish).
  • 3 June 2026, 12:00. Disposición 639/26 lifted that closure and the rules of Disposición 853/22 applied again (according to the Corrientes Ministry of Tourism notice).
  • 9 September 2026, 18:00. Extraordinary closure between km 1109 and 1150 (both inclusive) because of a concentration of dorado: all fishing banned, with a fine of 500 litres of super petrol and a minimum six-month disqualification, and the ban followed the shoal if it moved (according to the Corrientes Ministry of Tourism notice). It is Disposición 931, according to La Tribuna TV (11/9/2026).
  • September 2026. According to El Litoral (14/9), the closure reached the stretch between km 1140 (Puerto Empedrado) and 1208 (Punta San Sebastián, in the capital); according to Perfil (16/9), another between km 1180 and 1239, from the INTA creek to the Confluencia area, under Paso de la Patria jurisdiction.
  • 29 September 2026, 13:00. Disposición 1054/26 lifted the closure and Disposición 853/22 applied again (according to Corrientes al Día, Elonce and Perfil).
  • 13 November 2026, 12:00. According to Impacto Corrientes (13/11/2026), Disposición 1249/26 banned all fishing off the Costanera of Corrientes city, between Punta San Sebastián and Punta Tacurú, because of a shoal of manduré cuchara; it covers all species.

According to the COMIP–UNNE report, Itá Ibaté is at km 1375, upstream of every stretch reported by the Ministry of Tourism and the press (between km 1109 and 1239). The Ministry’s notices carry a moving clause: the ban follows the shoal if it moves.

Extraordinary closures are ordered and lifted from one day to the next: before you go, check the current ones with Recursos Naturales or your guide. The same timeline is in our note on size and bag limits and closed seasons.

Getting there

The operator’s page gives no routes, distances or airport, and we found none in an official source. All we can say is its position on the river: according to the COMIP–UNNE report, Itá Ibaté is at km 1375 of the Paraná, upstream of Itatí (km 1279) and downstream of Ituzaingó (km 1467) and Posadas (km 1530). Licence, closures and regulations

Fishing in Corrientes requires a sport fishing licence, bought online on the Natural Resources portal. Disposición 853/22 (Official Gazette, 26/12/2022, in force until another act replaces it) sets dorado and surubí as catch and release only, and gives minimum sizes and bag limits for pacú (45 cm, 1 per licence and a single fish per boat), patí (70 cm, 2), boga (45 cm, 2 per angler and up to 6 per boat) and tararira (30 cm, 5). Fish you keep may only be moved within the town where they were caught. The province orders extraordinary closures on stretches of the Paraná and did so several times in 2026 (the latest, according to the press, off the Costanera of Corrientes city from 13 November). They are ordered and lifted from one day to the next: before you go, check the current ones with Natural Resources.

Lodging · Packages and trips

Dorados on the Fly (Pinti’s)

Fly-fishing lodge on the Upper Paraná, in Itá Ibaté, according to its page. It says that Pinti, its owner, is recognised by locals as a pioneer of fly fishing on the Upper Paraná and that he placed the lodge on the most productive stretch. It has eight single bedrooms with private bathrooms in two buildings of four; each angler has a single room unless they ask otherwise. Meals are served in the main lodge, with a weekly asado, wines and an open bar of domestic liquors, wines and beers. Its page says it is not a great option for non-anglers. It says the dorado of the area average 6 to 8 pounds, with real chances of fish of about 30 pounds and a few in the 40-pound class, and that dorado, pirá pitá and pacú are sight-fished. Its page shows no rate.

How to choose a guide: what to ask before booking

  1. Style: does the guide fish bait, spinning/baitcasting or fly? Pick one who specialises in what you want to do.
  2. Boat and gear: what boat, how many anglers it takes, which engine (horsepower, bow electric motor)? Casting platform, fish finder? Is tackle provided?
  3. What is included: bait, meals, fuel, lodging, transfers. Get it in writing, with the price.
  4. Timing rules: the month decides the fishing. Ask what works on your dates, at what river level and water temperature.
  5. The place: where the lodging is, boat ramp, distance to the fishing areas and the overall setting.
  6. References: photos of recent trips and anglers who have been. If you can, talk to the owner or operator.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to fish dorado in Itá Ibaté?

The page of Dorados on the Fly, the only lodge with a page of its own that we found, publishes no season: ask the operator. For dorado on the Paraná in general the sources disagree; it is explained in our note “When to fish golden dorado on the Paraná”.

How much does a trip to Dorados on the Fly cost?

Its page publishes no rate: ask for it. In our note on how much a dorado fishing trip costs we compare rates of Upper Paraná lodges, some from agencies and marked as such; they are not the operator’s.

What is the accommodation at Dorados on the Fly like?

According to its page: two simple buildings with four bedrooms each, eight single bedrooms with private bathrooms, and each angler has a single room unless they ask otherwise. Meals are served in the main lodge, with a weekly asado, wines and an open bar of domestic liquors, wines and beers.

Is it suitable if I travel with someone who does not fish?

The page itself says Dorados on the Fly is not a great option for non-anglers.

Do I need a fishing licence?

Yes: the Corrientes licence (for people who live outside Argentina, the foreign tourist licence, which is calculated in litres of súper petrol). The lodge’s page does not say whether it includes it: ask before you book.

Can I keep the dorado I catch?

No. Under Disposición 853/22 of Recursos Naturales (Official Gazette, 26/12/2022), dorado and surubí are catch and release only in Corrientes.

Is it fly fishing or spinning?

The operator’s pages talk about fly fishing: sight fishing for dorado and dry-fly fishing for pirá pitá and pacú. They say nothing about spinning: ask.

How do I get to Itá Ibaté?

The operator’s page gives no routes or distances. According to the COMIP–UNNE report, Itá Ibaté is at km 1375 of the Paraná, upstream of Itatí (km 1279) and downstream of Ituzaingó (km 1467) and Posadas (km 1530). Ask the operator how to get there.

Who fishes in Itá Ibaté?

According to the COMIP–UNNE report (data from 2017 to 2020), tourist anglers from several provinces and from abroad, mainly from Brazil, plus local anglers; there were 20 complexes and about 120 guides.

Were there closures in 2026 near Itá Ibaté?

Yes, on the Paraná. According to the Corrientes Ministry of Tourism and the press: from 29/5 to 3/6 (km 1232 to 1238, surubí), from 9/9 to 29/9 (stretches between km 1109 and 1239, dorado) and, according to Impacto Corrientes, from 13/11 off the Costanera of Corrientes city. Itá Ibaté is at km 1375 according to the COMIP–UNNE report, upstream of all those stretches. Closures are ordered and lifted from one day to the next: check the current ones with Recursos Naturales before you go.
