What is Rio Manso Lodge?

Rio Manso Lodge is a premier fly fishing destination nestled in the pristine landscapes of Argentine Patagonia. We offer an unparalleled experience for anglers of all levels, combining world-class fishing with exceptional hospitality.

Our main lodge sits on the banks of the legendary Rio Manso, where crystal-clear waters teem with trophy trout. Whether you’re a seasoned angler or new to the sport, our expert guides will ensure an unforgettable adventure.

  • Private access to exclusive stretches of river
  • Expert bilingual guides
  • Full-board accommodation and gourmet cuisine
  • Conservation-focused practices

### A Patagonia Paradise: My Unforgettable Stay at Rio Manso Lodge Nestled deep within the pristine wilderness of Nahuel Huapi National Park in Northern Patagonia, Rio Manso Lodge is nothing short of a dream destination for any fly fisherman—or anyone seeking pure natural beauty. From the moment I arrived, overlooking the crystal-clear waters of Lago Hess with the majestic snow-capped peak of Monte Tronador looming in the distance, I knew this would be an extraordinary experience. The location is simply stunning: surrounded by ancient forests, glacial rivers, and untouched lakes, it feels like a secluded haven far from the world, yet remarkably accessible from Bariloche. The **fishing** here is truly incredible—one of the best I've ever encountered. The diverse waters, from the riffles and runs of the Rio Manso to intimate spring creeks and productive lakes like Lago Fonck, teem with healthy, wild rainbow, brown, and brook trout.often in the 2- to 10-pound range. With low fishing pressure and a strict catch-and-release policy, every cast feels full of possibility. Days on the water stretched long and rewarding, with sight fishing to cruising giants in gin-clear conditions that left me breathless. What elevated the adventure were the **guides**—exceptional, knowledgeable, and passionate professionals who speak fluent English and know these waters intimately. They expertly positioned the boat, shared insider tips on hatches and techniques, and ensured every angler, from novice to expert, had epic days. Their enthusiasm and skill turned good fishing into legendary outings. Back at the lodge, the **food** was nothing short of amazing. Gourmet meals blended world-class cuisine with traditional Argentine flavors: fresh grilled meats, vibrant salads, homemade desserts (that tres leches cake is unforgettable), all paired with outstanding Malbecs and fine wines from an impressive cellar. Streamside lunches were equally delightful—freshly prepared asados by the river. The attentive staff made every dining experience feel warm and luxurious, with evenings gathered around the stone fireplace sharing stories over hors d'oeuvres. Rio Manso Lodge combines elegant comfort with raw Patagonian wilderness in a way few places can. The rooms offer panoramic views, the amenities (sauna, massages) provide perfect relaxation, and the overall hospitality is heartfelt.If you're dreaming of world-class trout fishing in paradise, book Rio Manso Lodge. 5/5 Stars) – Highly recommended!
