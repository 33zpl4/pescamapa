About The Lodge
Tipiliuke Lodge is a world-renowned retreat celebrated for its exceptional accommodations and its legendary fly-fishing. Featured in Taschen’s
lifestyle and travel books as one of the best fly-fishing lodges in the world, it has earned numerous prizes and international recognitions.
The Gear Patrol
, the online guide for enthusiasts selected Tipiliuke as a dream outdoor vacation thanks to its access to massive brown trout across 17 miles of nearly untouched waters, while Fishing Explora
lists it among the few true luxury fly-fishing lodges worldwide. Among its many honors, Tipiliuke has been named Lodge of the Year by Anglers Choice Awards
and has received the Award of Excellence from Sporting Classics
.
Since then it has become famous for it's incredible service, attention to smallest detail, and luxury in a breathtaking setting.
Located just minutes from the San Martin de los Andes (Chapelco) Airport, Tipiliuke Lodge is nestled at the heart of a 50,000 acre working cattle ranch.
