## Texto (primeras 12.000 caracteres)
Patagonia in the Loop is a fly fishing guide service based in San Carlos de Bariloche, working the rivers and lakes of Nahuel Huapi National Park. Led by head guide Ezequiel Speranza, our team of fly fishing guides offers half-day and full-day trips on the Limay River, the lakes of the Manso basin, and the Alicurá Reservoir.
Whether you're after a relaxed half-day on the water or a full-day adventure with a riverside lunch, we tailor every trip to your experience level — from first-time anglers to seasoned fly fishers chasing trophy trout.
Summer here is dry fly season: warm days, rising trout, and some of the best dry fly fishing in Northern Patagonia.

Half-Day Trip
Perfect for a shorter stay. We fish the Limay River, just 15–19 miles (25–30 km) from downtown Bariloche — about a 30-minute drive. Excellent trout, stunning scenery, no long drives.

Fishing Destinations
The most productive rivers, lakes, and reservoirs of Northern Patagonia — each trip planned around the season and water conditions to put you in the right spot at the right time.
Reservoir - Winter Fishing
Alicurá Reservoir
Known for its trophy rainbow trout, Alicurá Reservoir offers outstanding stillwater fly fishing in a spectacular Patagonian setting.
Rivers
Limay & Manso Rivers
From the legendary Limay to the crystal-clear Manso, these iconic rivers offer unforgettable drift boat and raft fishing for wild trout in breathtaking scenery.
Lakes
Manso Basin Lakes
Discover the pristine lakes of the Manso Basin, including Roca, Fonck, Mascardi, and Hess. These beautiful waters combine spectacular scenery with excellent opportunities to catch wild trout in the heart of Northern Patagonia.
About Us
I'm Ezequiel Speranza — owner and head guide, with over 10 years of experience across Northern and Southern Patagonia, and the Brazilian Amazon.
I'm certified by the Asociación de Guías Profesionales del Parque Nahuel Huapi y Patagonia Norte, and authorized to guide within Nahuel Huapi National Park and Neuquén Province. As a certified Wilderness First Responder (WFR) trained in river navigation, rowing, and rescue, safety is never an afterthought.
For me, a great day on the water isn't just about the fish — it's about the landscape, the wildlife, and the stories you take home.
When I'm not on the water myself, your trip is in the hands of one of the top local guides I personally work with — so the same standard of quality and safety applies, every time.
