Have another question? Get in touch and we will help you choose the right trip for your visit.
What is the difference from the half-day trip?A full-day trip gives you time on the water both morning and afternoon, along with a hearty Argentine lunch served riverside. The half-day option focuses on the morning and includes a snack instead of lunch.
Do we fish from a boat or wade?We float the river and stop at suitable wading spots when conditions allow. The mix depends on the river level, chosen stretch and your preferences.
Is this trip suitable for beginners?Yes. The guide adapts the day and provides instruction. Let us know your experience so we can recommend an appropriate plan.
When is the best time to target migratory brown trout?The fall season—especially April and May—is the prime period for targeting large migratory brown trout on the Limay River. As water temperatures drop, these fish move up from Lake Nahuel Huapi into the river, creating opportunities to encounter some truly impressive specimens.
It is a technical fishery that rewards patience, precise presentations, and the ability to read the water. Our guides adjust the approach and tactics each day to make the most of every opportunity.
Can you accommodate dietary requirements?Yes. Tell us about dietary requirements in advance so we can prepare suitable options for lunch.
