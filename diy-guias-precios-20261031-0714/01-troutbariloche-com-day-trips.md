Limay River Float Fishing Trip
The Limay River
, just 45 minutes from Bariloche, is one of Patagonia’s most renowned destinations for wild trout fishing. Flowing out of Lake Nahuel Huapi through the spectacular Patagonian steppe, its crystal-clear waters offer outstanding opportunities to target large resident brown and rainbow trout.
With over 40 years of local Bariloche fishing experience, our guides know the Limay’s best runs, pools, and seasonal fishing patterns. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced angler, our private fly fishing day trips in Bariloche are tailored to your skill level and the day’s conditions.

Float fishing is one of the most effective ways to fish the Limay River, offering access to long runs, deep pools, and productive riverbanks where large wild trout hold. Our specially equipped drift boats provide a comfortable and stable fishing platform, allowing anglers to cover extensive stretches of this renowned Patagonian river while enjoying its spectacular scenery.
Depending on the season, river levels, and fishing conditions, we use dry flies, nymphs, and streamers to target wild brown and rainbow trout. In particular, streamer fishing can be especially productive when targeting trophy brown trout, while dry flies offer exciting opportunities during insect hatches. Additionally, nymphing allows anglers to fish deeper runs and reach trout holding below the surface.
Our experienced local guides tailor each Limay River float trip to your skill level and fishing preferences. Whether you're a beginner learning the basics or an experienced angler looking for trophy trout, our guides provide personalized instruction in casting, fly presentation, line management, and reading the water. Furthermore, they select the most suitable flies and techniques throughout the day.
For anglers interested in fly fishing in Bariloche, Argentina, the Limay River offers outstanding opportunities to combine technical fishing with the excitement of targeting large wild brown trout. Our private Bariloche fishing trips are designed to make the most of each day's conditions, providing access to productive waters and a full day of guided fly fishing in Northern Patagonia.
Full-Day Limay River Float Trip
8:00 AM — Pickup in Bariloche
Your guide picks you up from your accommodation in downtown Bariloche or along Avenida Bustillo up to Km 8. From there, it's approximately a 45-minute drive to the Limay River.
Morning — Limay River Float Fishing
Once at the river, your guide prepares the drift boat and fishing equipment before starting the float. Spend the morning fishing productive runs, pools, and riverbanks while enjoying the scenery of the Patagonian steppe.
Midday — Riverside Lunch
Around noon, we'll stop along the Limay River for a delicious regional lunch featuring traditional Argentine food and wine. It's the perfect opportunity to relax before heading back onto the water.
Afternoon — More Fishing
After lunch, we continue floating and fishing new sections of the river. Your guide will adjust the techniques and fly selection according to the conditions and trout activity.
End of the Day — Return to Bariloche
At the end of the Bariloche fishing day, your guide will drive you back to your accommodation in Bariloche.
Half-Day Limay River Float Trip
We also offer half-day float fishing trips on the Limay River. The Bariloche fishing trip follows the same morning itinerary, including pickup, transportation, professional fishing equipment, and guided fishing from our drift boats.
However, the half-day trip finishes before lunch, allowing you to return to Bariloche earlier.

Manso River Float Fishing Trip
The Manso River flows from the heart of the Andes through Nahuel Huapi National Park, surrounded by lush forests, towering mountains, and crystal-clear waters. Our private Manso River float trips take anglers through remote, lightly fished stretches of the river, offering excellent opportunities to target wild brown and rainbow trout. With no need for long casts, the Manso is ideal for both beginners and experienced fly anglers.
With decades of local knowledge, our fly-fishing guides in Bariloche tailor each trip to the season, river conditions, and your skill level. Whether you prefer dry fly fishing, sight fishing, or simply a relaxing day on the water, the Manso is a great choice for a guided fly fishing day trip in Bariloche, Argentina.

Float fishing on the Manso River is one of the highlights of fly fishing in Bariloche, Argentina. Our specially equipped catarafts provide a stable and comfortable fishing platform, allowing anglers to reach remote pools, productive runs, and riverbanks surrounded by native Patagonian forest.
Depending on the season and water conditions, we use floating and sinking lines, dry flies, nymphs, and streamers to target wild brown and rainbow trout. In particular, dry fly fishing can be excellent on the Manso, where accurate presentations along the banks and quieter pools often produce exciting strikes.
Additionally, nymphing is highly effective in deeper runs, while smaller tributaries and side channels provide opportunities for sight fishing. Our experienced local guides select the best flies and techniques throughout the day, making these Bariloche fishing trips suitable for different skill levels.
For beginners, the Manso offers excellent fishing opportunities without requiring long casts. Experienced anglers, meanwhile, can focus on technical presentations, sight fishing, and targeting larger wild trout in one of Patagonia's most beautiful rivers.
Full-Day Manso River Float Trip
8:00 AM — Pickup in Bariloche
Your local fly fishing guide Bariloche will pick you up from your accommodation in downtown Bariloche or along Avenida Bustillo up to Km 8. From there, we'll drive to the beautiful Manso River region, surrounded by mountains and native Patagonian forest.
Morning — Manso River Float Fishing
Once we arrive, your guide will prepare the cataraft and professional fly fishing equipment before starting the float trip. Spend the morning fishing crystal-clear waters, targeting wild brown and rainbow trout with techniques suited to the day's conditions.
Midday — Riverside or Lodge Lunch
Around midday, we'll take a break for a delicious regional lunch with Argentine wine. Depending on your booked trip, lunch will be served either on the banks of the Manso River or at our riverside Lodge.
Afternoon — More Fly Fishing Bariloche
After lunch, we'll continue floating through beautiful stretches of the Manso, fishing new pools, runs, and riverbanks. Your guide will adjust the fly selection and fishing techniques according to water conditions and trout activity.
End of the Day — Return to Bariloche
At the end of your full-day float trip, your guide will drive you back to your accommodation in Bariloche.
