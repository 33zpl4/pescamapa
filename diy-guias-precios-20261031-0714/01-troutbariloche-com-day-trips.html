Fly Fishing Bariloche Argentina

Guided fishing day trips in North Patagonia

Fly fishing Bariloche, Argentina, gives anglers access to some of the finest wild trout waters in Northern Patagonia. At Trout Bariloche, we offer private guided fishing day trips led by experienced local guides, with full-day and half-day options. Whether you prefer float fishing, wade/hiking fishing, or a combination of fishing and outdoor activities, our trips take you to the Limay and Manso Rivers and the beautiful lakes of Nahuel Huapi National Park.

Our fly fishing guides in Bariloche choose the best fishing spots based on the season, weather, river levels, and current fishing conditions. In addition, every day trip includes transportation and professional fishing gear. There are day-trip options that include a delicious lunch by the river or a meal at our lodge, featuring traditional Argentine cuisine and local wines.

For our Bariloche fishing trips, we use fully equipped drift boats, catarafts, and motorboats, along with quality rods, reels, waders, and a carefully selected assortment of flies. Furthermore, our English-speaking guides provide personalized instruction and local knowledge throughout the day. We typically pick you up from your accommodation at 8:00 a.m. and head to the water best suited to the day’s conditions. As a result, each trip is tailored to your skill level, fishing preferences, and the opportunities available that day.

Additionally, at our Lodge by the Manso River offers horseback riding, rafting, hydrospeed, hiking, and introductory fly fishing lessons. These activities are ideal for beginners, families, couples, and non-anglers who want to enjoy Patagonia while others spend the day on the water.

Limay River Float Fishing Trip

The Limay River, just 45 minutes from Bariloche, is one of Patagonia’s most renowned destinations for wild trout fishing. Flowing out of Lake Nahuel Huapi through the spectacular Patagonian steppe, its crystal-clear waters offer outstanding opportunities to target large resident brown and rainbow trout.

With over 40 years of local Bariloche fishing experience, our guides know the Limay’s best runs, pools, and seasonal fishing patterns. Whether you’re a beginner or an experienced angler, our private fly fishing day trips in Bariloche are tailored to your skill level and the day’s conditions.

Float fishing is one of the most effective ways to fish the Limay River, offering access to long runs, deep pools, and productive riverbanks where large wild trout hold. Our specially equipped drift boats provide a comfortable and stable fishing platform, allowing anglers to cover extensive stretches of this renowned Patagonian river while enjoying its spectacular scenery.

Depending on the season, river levels, and fishing conditions, we use dry flies, nymphs, and streamers to target wild brown and rainbow trout. In particular, streamer fishing can be especially productive when targeting trophy brown trout, while dry flies offer exciting opportunities during insect hatches. Additionally, nymphing allows anglers to fish deeper runs and reach trout holding below the surface.

Our experienced local guides tailor each Limay River float trip to your skill level and fishing preferences. Whether you're a beginner learning the basics or an experienced angler looking for trophy trout, our guides provide personalized instruction in casting, fly presentation, line management, and reading the water. Furthermore, they select the most suitable flies and techniques throughout the day.

For anglers interested in fly fishing in Bariloche, Argentina, the Limay River offers outstanding opportunities to combine technical fishing with the excitement of targeting large wild brown trout. Our private Bariloche fishing trips are designed to make the most of each day's conditions, providing access to productive waters and a full day of guided fly fishing in Northern Patagonia.

Manso River Float Fishing Trip

The Manso River flows from the heart of the Andes through Nahuel Huapi National Park, surrounded by lush forests, towering mountains, and crystal-clear waters. Our private Manso River float trips take anglers through remote, lightly fished stretches of the river, offering excellent opportunities to target wild brown and rainbow trout. With no need for long casts, the Manso is ideal for both beginners and experienced fly anglers.

With decades of local knowledge, our fly-fishing guides in Bariloche tailor each trip to the season, river conditions, and your skill level. Whether you prefer dry fly fishing, sight fishing, or simply a relaxing day on the water, the Manso is a great choice for a guided fly fishing day trip in Bariloche, Argentina.

Float fishing on the Manso River is one of the highlights of fly fishing in Bariloche, Argentina. Our specially equipped catarafts provide a stable and comfortable fishing platform, allowing anglers to reach remote pools, productive runs, and riverbanks surrounded by native Patagonian forest.

Depending on the season and water conditions, we use floating and sinking lines, dry flies, nymphs, and streamers to target wild brown and rainbow trout. In particular, dry fly fishing can be excellent on the Manso, where accurate presentations along the banks and quieter pools often produce exciting strikes.

Additionally, nymphing is highly effective in deeper runs, while smaller tributaries and side channels provide opportunities for sight fishing. Our experienced local guides select the best flies and techniques throughout the day, making these Bariloche fishing trips suitable for different skill levels.

For beginners, the Manso offers excellent fishing opportunities without requiring long casts. Experienced anglers, meanwhile, can focus on technical presentations, sight fishing, and targeting larger wild trout in one of Patagonia's most beautiful rivers.

Wade fishing on the Manso River allows anglers to fish on foot, moving between shallow pools, riffles, and productive runs. Equipped with professional waders and wading boots, you'll have direct access to beautiful stretches of the river.

Depending on the season and water conditions, our guides use dry flies, nymphs, and streamers to target wild trout. In particular, the Manso's clear water provides opportunities for sight fishing and delicate dry fly presentations. Additionally, nymphing can be effective in deeper pools and faster currents.

For beginners, the day starts with an introduction to fly casting, basic techniques, and safe wading. Our patient, English-speaking guides will show you how to read the water, select the right flies, and present them naturally. Meanwhile, experienced anglers can focus on technical presentations and targeting larger trout.

Unlike a float trip, wade fishing lets you spend more time working individual pools and exploring the river on foot. Furthermore, our private riverside access provides a peaceful setting for guided fly fishing in Bariloche, away from heavily visited fishing areas.

Bariloche Lake Fishing – Moreno & Nahuel Huapi

Our private lake fishing trips in Bariloche, Argentina, offer outstanding opportunities to target large brown, rainbow, and brook trout from fully equipped motorboats. Surrounded by the Andes and Northern Patagonia’s spectacular scenery, Lake Moreno and Lake Nahuel Huapi offer excellent fishing at different times of the year.

From June through late October, we fish Lake Moreno, from October through mid-December, our trips focus on Lake Nahuel Huapi.

Whether you prefer fly fishing, spinning, or trolling, our experienced local guides select the best locations and techniques based on seasonal and weather conditions. These private Bariloche fishing trips are available as full-day or half-day excursions, with an optional lakeside lunch.

Lake Moreno — Winter Trout Fishing

June – Late October

Lake Moreno offers excellent winter fishing opportunities just outside Bariloche. Its varied underwater structure, including sandy banks, submerged vegetation, reed beds, and deep channels, provides ideal habitat for brown, rainbow, and brook trout.

Lake Nahuel Huapi — Trophy Trout Fishing

October – Mid-December
 
Lake Nahuel Huapi is one of Patagonia's iconic trout fishing destinations, known for its vast glacial waters and opportunities to target large wild brown and rainbow trout. During the spring fishing season, our professional guides use fully equipped motorboats to reach productive shorelines, bays, and feeding areas.

FAQs About Fly Fishing in Bariloche, Argentina

What should I bring on my fishing day?

Bring sunglasses, sunscreen, a sun hat, a waterproof jacket, warm layers, their own water bottle, and comfortable clothing. Patagonia’s weather can change quickly, so we recommend dressing in layers even during summer.

Can my family or non-fishing companions join the trip?

Of course! Our private fly fishing trips Bariloche are suitable for couples, families, and friends. Non-anglers can enjoy the scenery, relax by the river, or choose activities such as horseback riding, rafting, and hiking at our Manso River property.

Can I combine fly fishing with other activities in Bariloche?

Yes! At our Manso River Fishing Lodge, you can combine fishing with horseback riding, rafting, or a relaxing afternoon at our riverside lodge. We can customize your day to suit anglers and non-anglers alike.

Can I stay at your Fly Fishing Lodge in Bariloche?

Yes. Our Fly Fishing Lodge Bariloche offers riverside accommodations, homemade regional cuisine, guided fishing, and outdoor activities. You can extend your day trip into a multiday Patagonia fishing vacation with our local team.

Why should I book directly with Trout Bariloche?

Trout Bariloche is a locally owned business led by Emanuel Medina. Booking directly means personal advice from our local team, experienced guides, flexible trip planning, and local prices without agency middlemen. Read our TripAdvisor reviews to hear from previous guests.

How far in advance should I book my Bariloche fishing trip?

We recommend booking early, especially if you’re traveling during the summer fishing season or have specific dates in mind. Contact our local team with your preferred dates, group size, and fishing interests, and we’ll help you plan your Bariloche fishing trip.

Can beginners book a fly fishing trip in Bariloche?

Absolutely! Our patient, English-speaking guides welcome first-time anglers and provide personalized instruction throughout the day. You can also start with our two-hour fly casting clinic at our Manso River fishing base camp before heading out to fish.

Is fishing equipment included in your Bariloche fishing trips?

Yes. We provide professional rods, reels, lines, flies, waders, and wading boots for our guided fly fishing trips. Our guides select suitable equipment and flies for the river, lake, and fishing conditions.

Do your fly fishing guides in Bariloche speak English?

Yes. Our professional local guides speak English and have extensive knowledge of Patagonia’s rivers and lakes. They’re friendly, patient, and happy to help anglers of all skill levels improve their techniques.

Is transportation from my Bariloche hotel included?

Transportation is included on our standard guided river trips, with pickup from central Bariloche and accommodations along Avenida Bustillo up to Km 8. For lake fishing, the usual meeting point is Bahía López. Transportation for casting clinics and other activities can be arranged upon request.

Do I need an Argentine fishing license?

Yes, anglers must hold the appropriate fishing license. Our team can help you arrange the fishing license in Bariloche.
