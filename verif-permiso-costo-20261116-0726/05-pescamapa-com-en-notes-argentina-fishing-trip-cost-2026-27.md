What fishing trips in Argentina cost, by type, with the figures operators publish and the date each was read, and what changes the price.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 16 November 2026
Key facts
Day trips: from US$ 410 for half a day and US$ 500 for a full day near Bariloche, per boat for one or two anglers (read 6/11/2026); in El Calafate AR$ 440,000 and AR$ 720,000 per angler (5/11/2026).
Dorado: a multi-day package at a guesthouse in Esquina from US$ 605 per person; an all-inclusive lodge from US$ 4,050 (3 nights) to US$ 7,950 (7 nights).
A week at a trout lodge in northern Patagonia: US$ 5,800 to US$ 9,200 for 7 nights and 6 fishing days (5/11/2026).
Sea-run trout in Río Grande, Tierra del Fuego: US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 per person for a week; Jurassic Lake: US$ 8,000 per angler for 7 nights.
Flights, the license and tips are usually not in the price; each figure is the operator’s, for its own dates.

Short answer. A fishing trip to Argentina costs very different amounts depending on what you buy: from US$ 410 for half a day with a guide to US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 for a week of sea-run trout. This page is the overview: each figure comes from one of our dated notes, which link to the detail and the conditions. They are the prices operators publish, with the date we read them; they are not quotes for your dates.

What changes the cost
A full week or half a week: the price per fishing day falls with each extra day. At one Neuquén lodge it goes from US$ 1,975 with 2 fishing days to US$ 1,158 with 6; at the dorado lodges of the table, from US$ 2,025 to US$ 1,325 (our calculations, in the notes linked above).
A companion who does not fish: US$ 650 per night at the dorado lodges and at a Río Grande lodge, US$ 800 per night at a lodge on the Manso River, and AR$ 185,000 (half day) or AR$ 335,000 (full day) in El Calafate.
Single room or boat: a 60% surcharge at the dorado lodges of the price ladder and 50% for an individual boat at a lodge on the middle Paraná. The Río Grande rate is already for a single room.
The license, apart from the price: it was included in 4 of the nine packages we compared, not included in 3 and unspecified in 2 (what a package includes
). A Río Grande lodge estimates about US$ 550 per angler per week for it. How to get it: Argentina fishing license, step by step
.
Flights and transfers: the Río Grande price starts at the airport, the dorado lodges do not include flights, and the Jurassic Lake charter flight is extra.
Rented equipment: a lodge on the Manso River charges US$ 80 per angler per day for a full set (US$ 40 for rod and reel or for boots and waders); the dorado lodges of the price ladder lend equipment.
Tips: Jurassic Lake’s page suggests US$ 80 to 100 per person per day.
The date: the Río Grande week costs US$ 12,950, US$ 13,950 or US$ 14,950 depending on the dates, and the Manso River lodge raises its week from US$ 7,000 to US$ 7,700 from 1 January 2027.
What a price usually includes and what it does not
In the pages operators publish and we have read, the price usually covers lodging, meals, drinks, a guide and loan equipment. What usually stays outside: flights and transfers to the lodge, the fishing license, tips and laundry. It is not the same at every operator, which is why two numbers are only comparable when they cover the same things: see what an Argentina fishing package includes and what is extra

Frequently asked questions
How much does a fishing trip to Argentina cost?
It depends on the type. The published figures we have read go from US$ 410 for half a day with a guide near Bariloche (per boat, 6/11/2026) to US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 for a week of sea-run trout in Río Grande (4/11/2026). In between: a dorado package at a guesthouse from US$ 605 per person, a dorado lodge from US$ 4,050 for 3 nights, and a week at a trout lodge in northern Patagonia from US$ 5,800 to US$ 9,200. The table above gives the source and the date of each.
How much does a week at a fishing lodge in Patagonia cost?
For a week of 7 nights and 6 fishing days in northern Patagonia, the lodges we read publish US$ 5,800 to US$ 9,200 per person in a double room (5/11/2026), and a lodge on the Manso River US$ 7,000 for 2026/27 and US$ 7,700 from 1 January 2027 (read 10/11/2026). In Tierra del Fuego, a week of sea-run trout is US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 (4/11/2026).
How much does a day of fishing with a guide cost?
Near Bariloche, from US$ 410 for half a day and US$ 500 for a full day, per boat for one or two anglers, with the license extra (6/11/2026). In El Calafate, AR$ 440,000 for half a day and AR$ 720,000 for a full day per angler, with the permits included in that price (5/11/2026).
How much does a dorado fishing trip cost?
In Corrientes, a guesthouse in Esquina publishes packages from US$ 605 per person (group of three, 3 nights, 2 fishing days) and US$ 705 (group of two) as of 4/11/2026; all-inclusive lodges publish US$ 4,050 for 3 nights and US$ 7,950 for 7 nights per person in a double room (read 13/11/2026). Flights are not included.
How much does sea-run trout fishing in Tierra del Fuego cost?
A Río Grande lodge publishes US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 per person for a week, depending on the dates, in a single room and with a shared guide (read 4/11/2026); another lodge publishes the same range for 2027 (read 13/11/2026). The price does not include flights, the license or tips.
Is the fishing license included in the price?
It depends on the operator: it was included in 4 of the nine packages we compared, not included in 3 and unspecified in 2. Ask in writing. How to get the permit and what it costs: Argentina fishing license, step by step
.
Are flights included?
In the lodges we read, no: the Río Grande price starts at the airport, the dorado lodges leave out flights and transfers, and the Jurassic Lake charter flight is extra. A few operators include charter flights or transfers; the detail is in each regional note.
Are the prices in dollars or in pesos?
In the currency each operator publishes: lodges in dollars, day trips in El Calafate in pesos, and Bariloche in both, from different operators. We do not convert one into the other because each operator sets its own exchange rate when you book.
Does a companion who does not fish pay the same?
Not usually: the companion pays less than an angler, but still pays. US$ 650 per night at the dorado lodges and at a Río Grande lodge, US$ 800 per night at a lodge on the Manso River, and AR$ 185,000 or AR$ 335,000 for a day in El Calafate.
