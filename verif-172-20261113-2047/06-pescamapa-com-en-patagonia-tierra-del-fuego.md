Updated 13 November 2026
The sea-run brown trout season on the Río Grande runs from 30 October 2026 to 18 April 2027 on the river and its tributaries, with catch and release of all salmonids and a prior booking in the SAPTDF access system; the rest of the province closes on 2 May 2027 (Resolución S.A. 259/2026, read on 4 November 2026). The ordinary permit for a non-resident foreigner costs $ 78,000 a day, plus an additional permit on the Grande and Menéndez rivers.
