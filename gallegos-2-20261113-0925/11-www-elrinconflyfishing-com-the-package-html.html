The El Rincon Fly Fishing Package
The fishing package at Rincon consists of 6 full days fishing/ 7 nights accommodation based on single occupancy. Inclusive of transfers to and from the local airport.
Two anglers per guide.
All meals, drinks (including alcoholic) are included within the price.
(International and Internal flights not included).
Please contact us for current rates.

The Fish

Fishing is for migratory Sea Trout and Brown Trout during the season of December 15 - April 15.

For more info see our Fly Fishing page

Accommodation

All guests bedroom accommodation is based on single occupancy unless couples wish to share. All meals and drinks, including alcoholic beverages, are included in the price.

The Area

Remote location in Southern Patagonia Argentina on rivers Gallegos and Penitente. Some fishing also on Rio Rubens.
For more info see our wildlife page

The Lodge

Spacious historic house set in 57,000 acres, surrounded by spectacular Patagonian landscape.

For more info visit our Lodge page

The Food

We have a professional Argentinian Chef specialising in traditional locally sourced produce.


The Price

Please contact us for current prices.   
We aim to deliver best value in Patagonia.
Due to recent volatility of prices within Argentina we ask that you make contact for latest package rates.
