Get to Know The Camp
A cozy, eco-friendly lodge in the heart of Patagonia — your home by the river.
Just 45 minutes from Río Gallegos Airport, Karku Camp welcomes anglers to experience Patagonia’s wild beauty, home-cooked meals, and exclusive access.
What’s Included at Karku Camp
Everything you need for an unforgettable fly fishing week in Patagonia — comfort, nature, and authentic local flavor.
Transfer
Round-trip transportation from Río Gallegos Airport (RGL) to the lodge.
Guided Fishing
Expert local guides and exlusive access.
Room
Cozy refurbished cabin with 2 single bedrooms and 1 double room (shared bathroom).
Meal
Continental breakfast, homemade lunch and dinner — using local and fresh ingredients.
Wi-Fi
Internet access available (STARLINK). Perfect for staying connected when needed.
Sustainability
Powered entirely by solar energy, Karku Camp is a genuine eco-friendly lodge committed to preserving Patagonia’s wild beauty.
Our StoryThe Estancia
Located in the vast plains of Santa Cruz, Argentina, Karku Camp sits within a century-old estancia founded in 1914 — a place where Patagonia’s history and wilderness coexist.
Once a traditional sheep farm, today it offers guests a warm cabin and a glimpse into Argentina’s pastoral life.
The lodge blends rustic charm and comfort, surrounded by open skies and unforgettable landscapes.
Today, Karku Camp blends the heritage of the Patagonian estancia with the comfort and warmth of a modern fly fishing lodge.
A Fisherman’s ParadiseFishing Paradise
Our camp lies near the Río Gallegos estuary — one of the most iconic fly fishing destinations in Patagonia. As the first lodge located near Güer Aike and the estuary, we have the advantage of catching the first runs of the sea-run brown trouts. The lodge is just a five-minute drive from the river, where you’ll spot guanacos, flamingos, foxes, and rheas along the way.
With exclusive access, Karku guests enjoy some of the most rewarding fishing in the southern hemisphere. Whether you’re chasing trophy sea-run brown trout or simply soaking in Patagonia’s silence, Karku Camp offers one of South America’s most exclusive fly-fishing experiences.
For those with an eye for discovery, the surrounding area is rich in natural history. Keen observers might find ancient arrowheads or, if lucky, some boleadoras—stone tools tied with leather used for hunting. Please remember, if you discover any objects of archaeological or paleontological interest, you are welcome to take photos, but collecting or taking objects home is not permitted.
Life at the lodgeLife at Karku Camp
After a day on the river, relax by the fire and enjoy a home-cooked meal.
Beer and wine are available at fair prices, and the highlight of the week is our traditional Argentine asado — grilled outdoors over stone and flame.
At Karku Camp, comfort meets simplicity — the perfect base for a week of fishing, good food, and new friendships.
Here, simplicity becomes luxury — good food, good stories, and good company by the river.
What’s no Included at Karku Camp
Laundry service, fishing gear, flies, alcoholic beverages, and gratuities are not included.
However, we’re happy to offer laundry services for your convenience, available for a small fee. We also have a selection of our favorite flies for the Gallegos River, suitable for all water conditions. These flies are crafted with excellent hooks and high-quality materials to ensure a successful fishing experience.
Additionally, enjoy a variety of local alcoholic beverages, carefully selected to enhance your stay.
