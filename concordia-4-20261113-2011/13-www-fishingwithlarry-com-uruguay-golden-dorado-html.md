Uruguay Golden Dorado
Spin, Bait, or Fly Fishing in World-Record Tailwaters
Think La Zona—the world-famous golden dorado fishery. With River Plate, you’ll fish the very same tailwaters of the Uruguay River, just below the Salto Grande Dam. The difference? You’ll stay on the Uruguay side at the elegant Quiroga Thermal Spa & Resort, only 500 yards from the dam.
River Plate fishes the exclusive 1,000-meter Restricted Zone below the dam—known simply as The Zone—where countless IGFA world records have been set. Access is strictly limited: River Plate can fish a maximum of two boats and four anglers at a time.
Enjoy single-occupancy lodging, luxury amenities, and some of the best golden dorado fishing in the world. Book early—space is limited.
2027 Rates start at $5,600 per person for 5 nights / 4 days guided fishing.
River Plate's Tail Waters
Art Weston with World Record
Golden Dorado
The Uruguay River flows from the Salto Grande Dam and is a natural border between Argentina and Uruguay. River Plate (on the Uruguay side) and La Zona Lodge (on the Argentina side) share the exclusive 1,000-meter fishing zone located just below the dam.
Salto Grande Dam
Salto Grande Dam:  Giant dorado thrive here, feeding on baitfish flushed from the dam's hydroelectric turbines. The dam turbines flush large amounts of food and supply a superabundance of whirling oxygen, creating a uniquely rich, Jurassic-like environment.
In these catch-and-release waters, golden dorado grow larger and larger and in staggering numbers. Water levels fluctuate frequently in response to hydroelectric demand, creating an added challenge for anglers.
Uruguay River World Records
The golden dorado pictured above was landed in 2013 by Andrei Bylchynski on a deep-diving Rapala X-Rap, and when weighed, it bottomed out a 60-pound boga grip.
At least 23 IFGA baitcasting and fly-fishing world records have been set here in various categories. The All-Tackle World Record was broken twice; the latest has stood for over a decade!
Fishing Session:  Multiple passes are made in the Exclusive Zone during a three-hour fishing session. You'll begin fishing upriver near the dam, making multiple casts through the drift while you back down with the current. Once you reach the downriver side of the Zone, your guide will motor upriver to begin another drift.
Season:  The fishery is open year-round. The fishery has a two-boat limit and is only fished Monday through Thursday. Peak fishing is during the summer from mid-November through mid-March. Dorado spawning season is from late October through early January.
Your Host:  Your stay will be overseen by Pancho Cardoso, one of River Plate’s founding partners, with the club for over 25 years.
The Fishing Program:
Group Size:  Four to eight anglers per week, depending on the season. You'll fish two anglers per boat and guide. River Plate can fish a maximum of four anglers at a time inside the Restricted Zone.
Boats:  Wide, stable 21-ft. shallow-draft fiberglass bass boats with 90 HP outboard engines. Boats have two large casting platforms, so two anglers can fish simultaneously. All fishing is from the boat.
Guides:  Outstanding and reputable Spanish-speaking guides.
Equipment:  Spinning and baitcasting rods and reels are provided. Conventional lures are available for purchase for $8–$15 each. Fly anglers must bring their own fly-fishing gear. A comprehensive gear and tackle list is included in our confirmation packet.
Fishing License:  A fishing license and a permit to fish the Restricted tailwater are included in the package.
Travel Time:  The fishery is 500 yards from the hotel, about an 8-minute walk to the docks.
Physical Condition:  The hotel and fishing program are wheelchair-friendly.

Today is our last day of fishing, but I’ve got a few minutes before we go out, so I figured I’d drop you a line with some feedback and observations.

Biggest piece of advice for future anglers: absolutely no reason to bring any of your own gear unless you really want to. Patrick and the guides have absolutely everything you need. I haven’t used 95% of the stuff I brought with me. Curly tails are the one thing someone may want to bring.

Quiroga Thermal Spa and Resort
Anglers will stay in the exclusive VIP Suites section of the Quiroga Thermal Spa and Resort in Salto, Uruguay. This section has twelve bedrooms and a private dining room with an open bar overlooking the lake. The Resort is 500 meters from the Salto Grande Dam.
Hotel Horacio Quiroga YouTube Video
The large vein of the Guarani Aquifer flows along the Uruguay River directly beneath the resort. The resort taps into these hot, mineral-rich waters. After a day of landing big fish, you'll enjoy your room's private ensuite whirlpool bath and a thermal massage shower. The package provides access to the facilities and services at Quiroga Thermal Spa, including thermal whirlpools.
Angler Suites in the VIP Section
Spacious single-occupancy rooms. Each angler will have their own room with a view of the reservoir and a partial view of the 590-acre park surrounding the Resort.
Communications:  Wi-Fi access is available at the resort.
Non-Angler Activities:  The largest thermal-water aquatic park in Latin America is nearby. There are hiking trails, catamaran rides around the lake, electric train rides, and horses available for hire.
Private Dining Area and Open Bar
Meals:  Anglers are assigned a private dining room. Continental-style breakfast with eggs cooked-to-order, bacon/sausage, yogurt, fruit, juices, coffee, and tea. For lunches and dinners, you'll order from a full menu.
Alcohol:  The package includes a 24-hour open bar with liquor, wine, beer, and soft drinks.

Getting Here:
The fishery is reachable from Buenos Aires, Argentina, or Montevideo, Uruguay, with direct flights from most U.S. gateways.
Upon arrival at either airport, you'll be met by a River Plate staff member who will drive you to the Resort, a 5-hour drive. The package now includes the roundtrip ground transfers between Buenos Aires or Montevideo and the Resort.
A one-hour private charter flight may be available from Buenos Aires or Montevideo. Please ask for current options and pricing.
Departure Day:  After breakfast, you'll be driven back to the international airport, a 5-hour drive.
