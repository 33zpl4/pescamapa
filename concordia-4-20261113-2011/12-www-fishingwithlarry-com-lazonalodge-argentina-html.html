La Zona Lodge - Argentina

Flies or Lures for World Record Golden Dorado

Our client, Mike Sadar, broke a world record here.

La Zona, "The Zone," is on the Uruguay River in the province of Entre Ríos, about 270 miles from Buenos Aires. La Zona is the exclusive and Restricted Zone on the Uruguay River, downstream from the Salto Grande Dam. La Zona is a very restricted fishery. The area is only open four days per week, and only 12 anglers from Argentina and Uruguay can fish at a time. Our client, Mike Sadar, landed a world-record fly-caught 50-pound golden dorado at La Zona in April 2012.

La Zona freshwater golden dorado fishing is unrivaled and is home to over 12 world records. The shallow lake, created by the nearby dam, supports an abundant and complex food chain. Here, many species, including the dorado, achieve unprecedented growth rates.
More than 300 fish of over 40 pounds were landed at La Zona in the past three seasons. The Uruguay River is a tailwater below the large Salto Grande Reservoir. La Zona Lodge, which has exclusive rights to the Argentine side of the river, can fish a maximum of six anglers at a time inside the Restricted Zone.
La Zona is a unique fishery where dorado reach incredible sizes and numbers due to abundant food and very low fishing pressure. Anglers usually average 15 to 25 strikes per day. Fish average about 14 pounds, with a chance at dorado over 50 pounds. Twenty-pounders are commonplace.

"...I feel that starting the drift 500 meters away from the dam walls puts the angler in a better position to catch true giants, as evidenced by our results. - In my opinion, La Zona is the world's greatest freshwater fishery." - Bob Daly - Peacock Bass e-Zine


La Zona Fishing Program:

La Zona Season:  Peak season runs from the last week of December to late April when the Uruguay River is at its lowest. La Zona is a year-round fishery. During the Mid-Season or spring season - September and October - the river is at its normal flow, and huge schools of big dorado migrate to the dam to spawn. Rapala Super Shad Raps and similar crankbaits are hot during this time.
Fishing is only allowed with flies or artificial lures. Barbless hooks are required.
The Guides:  Professional local guides speak fishing-English.
Boats:  Very comfortable 21-foot fiberglass center-console boats with 70-HP, four-stroke Suzuki motors, an 8-HP auxiliary motor, and spacious casting platforms in the bow and stern. Each boat can accommodate two or three anglers, but for the best fishing experience, we recommend the two-angler package for greater comfort and casting room.
Fishing Equipment:  High-quality spinning and baitcasting gear is provided; a breakage or loss fee applies for replacement. Lures are also provided and are not subject to a loss or breakage fee. Fly fishing gear and flies are not included. A detailed list of what to bring is in our Confirmation Packet.
A Fishing License is included in the package.

A Typical 6-Hour Peak Season Fishing Day:

Each guided fishing session includes three hours of fishing time, not including travel to and from the fishing grounds. Travel consists of approximately a five-minute drive to the boats and a 15-minute boat ride to The Zone.
5:00 a.m. – Cooked-to-order breakfast is available for anglers fishing the early session.
5:30 a.m. – Transfer to the boats, followed by a 15-minute boat ride to The Zone.
6:00 a.m. – Morning fishing session begins.
9:00 a.m. – Return to the Lodge. Relax by the pool, enjoy a game of pool, or rest before your afternoon session.
Noon – Enjoy lunch at the Lodge. If you're fishing the 12:00 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. session, you may have lunch before departing, take a packed lunch on the boat, or eat after returning to the Lodge.
3:00 p.m. – Afternoon fishing session begins.
6:00 p.m. – Return to the moorage and transfer back to the Lodge. Freshen up, grab a beverage, and relax by the pool.
8:00 p.m. – Three-course dinner is served.

Beverages on the Boat:  Bottled water, Coca-Cola (regular and sugar-free), sports drinks (regular and sugar-free), and beer are provided.

Sunset: From December through February, sunset is typically around 8:00 p.m. In March, sunset is around 7:00 p.m., and by late April, around 6:30 p.m.

Full La Zona Program:  Your 6.5- to 8-hour guided fishing day includes a morning and afternoon session with a midday break. You’ll return to the Lodge for lunch and a siesta before heading back out for your afternoon fishing session.

La Zona's Lodge

La Zona Lodge is situated on the banks of Ayuí Creek, a scenic tributary of the Uruguay River, on a 37-acre property surrounded by native vegetation and abundant wildlife. Downstream from the Lodge, a dense forest provides opportunities to observe birds, capybaras, deer, and other native species. The Lodge's peaceful setting combines comfort and privacy with direct access to world-record Golden Dorado fishing.
The Lodge provides free daily laundry service, a pool table, a swimming pool, and a fully equipped tackle shop. The Lodge is wheelchair accessible.

Rates and Details

Getting to La Zona Lodge:

1.) You'll fly to Buenos Aires, Argentina.

2.) On Thursday morning, a lodge representative will pick the group up from the airport or your hotel in Buenos Aires if you choose to arrive a day early.

3.) You'll be driven in an executive van to La Zona Lodge in Concordia, a five-hour, 270-mile trip on an excellent highway. Upon arrival at the Lodge, guests will check in, assemble fishing equipment, settle into their rooms, and enjoy dinner.
Please note:  Airfare, hotels, and meals in Buenos Aires are not included in the package.
Departure Day – Friday
After breakfast, guests will check out and be transferred back to Buenos Aires. Departure times can be adjusted to accommodate guests' flight schedules. Late-morning departures from the lodge can be arranged without difficulty.
