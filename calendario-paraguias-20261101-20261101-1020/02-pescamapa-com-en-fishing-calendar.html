Goya corrientes

WhenWhat to expect
October – AprilGolden dorado: most active in warmer water, in rapids, lagoon mouths and sandbanks. Lures and trolling.
March – AugustSurubí: deep holes and drop-offs of the Paraná, with live bait, anchored or drifting.
December – MarchPacú and boga: near floating vegetation and tree-lined banks.
28 April – 2 May 202750th National Surubí Festival. Peak demand for lodging and transport: book early.
Before every tripCheck the Paraná level at Goya on the INA site, the forecast and current closures. Seasons are indicative and shift with the river.

What the regulations say

Fishing in Corrientes requires a sport fishing licence, issued online through the provincial Natural Resources portal, with categories for bank and boat fishing and by residence (Corrientes, rest of Argentina, foreign visitors). Carry the receipt and your ID. Residents of Santa Fe, Entre Ríos or Chaco with a valid licence can fish in Corrientes under the regional mutual-recognition agreement, following local rules; check its scope before you travel. Golden dorado is a Provincial Natural Monument (Law 6730) and is catch and release. In the Ysoró creek, harvesting fish is banned: catch and release only (Law 6655). For surubí, the national reference minimum size is 85 cm; check the current Corrientes rule. The province sets spawning closures and, when needed, extraordinary closures on stretches of the Paraná (the 2026 ones were upstream, in the Alto Paraná, far from Goya). Always check the current ones for the stretch you plan to fish.

Authority: Dirección de Recursos Naturales de Corrientes (provincial Natural Resources office). Regulations read by Pescamapa; not yet formally checked with the authority.

Esquina corrientes

WhenWhat to expect
October – AprilDorado: most active in warm water. Fly fishing on the Corriente River and its side channels; lures and trolling on the Paraná. Always catch-and-release.
March – AugustSurubí: regional reference, in deep holes and drop-offs of the Paraná. Large fish are also reported outside those months.
December – MarchPacú: summer season, near floating vegetation and wooded banks.
FebruaryNational Pacú Festival (in 2026, 8–15 February). Peak demand for lodging: book early.
Before every tripCheck the Paraná level, the forecast and the closures in force. The periods are indicative and change with the river.

What the regulations say

Fishing in Corrientes requires a sport fishing licence. You get it online on the Recursos Naturales portal: fill in the form with your ID (DNI), pay through the digital gateway and receive the credential with a QR code, which you can show on your phone together with your ID. There are shore and boat categories, and rates by residence (Corrientes residents, rest of the country, foreigners); retirees, pensioners and children under 12 apply for an exemption at no cost. On the Corriente River and the side channels of its delta only sport fishing with a rod or hand line is allowed: no nets or longlines. The dorado is a Provincial Natural Monument (Law 6730) and is fished catch-and-release. The pacú has a minimum size of 45 cm, and local operators recommend releasing it in streams and marshes. Spawning closures (or the regime of fixed days per week) are set every year by Recursos Naturales: if you travel between November and January, check the current provision before you leave.

Authority: Dirección de Recursos Naturales de Corrientes. Regulations verified on 20 October 2026.

Paso de la Patria corrientes

WhenWhat to expect
AprilTorneo Apertura of the dorado season (in 2026, 2 to 5 April, catch and release).
AugustNational Dorado Festival (61st edition in 2026, 14 to 17 August, catch and release). Peak demand for lodging: book early.
Before every tripCheck the Paraná level at the Paso de la Patria station (INA), the forecast and the closures in force.

What the regulations say

Fishing in Corrientes requires a sport fishing licence, bought online on the Natural Resources portal. Disposición 853/22 (Official Gazette, 26/12/2022, in force until another act replaces it) sets dorado and surubí as catch and release only, and gives minimum sizes and bag limits for pacú (45 cm, 1 per licence and a single fish per boat), patí (70 cm, 2), boga (45 cm, 2 per angler and up to 6 per boat) and tararira (30 cm, 5). Fish you keep may only be moved within the town where they were caught. The province can order extraordinary closures on stretches; they change every year, so check the current ones with Natural Resources before you go.

Authority: Dirección de Recursos Naturales de Corrientes. Regulations read by Pescamapa; not yet formally checked with the authority.

Frequently asked questions about the fishing calendar

When is the best time to fish in Argentina?

It depends on the species and the place. On the Litoral, dorado and surubí follow the Paraná level and operators repeat two windows, October–November and March–May; lagoon pejerrey is an autumn and winter fish; Patagonian trout have a regulated season, generally November to April or May. Each destination on this page says its own.

Is there a closed season for dorado in Argentina?

Each province sets its own. In Corrientes dorado is catch-and-release only all year (Disposición 853/22); other provinces set closures by dates or bag limits. Check the destination's regulations before travelling: extraordinary closures change every year.

Where do I confirm a closed season?

With each province's authority, linked in every destination. Our guides say when we read it; if your trip is later, check the source again or ask your guide.
