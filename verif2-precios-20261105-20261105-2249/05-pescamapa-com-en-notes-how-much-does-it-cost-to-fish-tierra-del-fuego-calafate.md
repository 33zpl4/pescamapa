How much does it cost to fish in Tierra del Fuego and El Calafate: sea-run trout and day trips (2026/27)
The price of a week of sea-run trout at a Río Grande lodge and a half-day or full-day trip in El Calafate, with what each includes.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
A week at a sea-run trout lodge in Río Grande (Tierra del Fuego) costs from US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 per person, depending on the date, in the 2026/27 season.
That lodge rate is for a single room and shared guide, and includes meals, drinks, and transfer from the airport.
In El Calafate, a half-day trip (5 hours) costs $ 440,000 per angler and a full-day trip (9 hours) $ 720,000.
In El Calafate, fishing is from the shore, wading; non-fishing companions pay $ 185,000 for half-day and $ 335,000 for full-day.

Short answer. In southern Patagonia there are two published price points. A week of sea-run trout at a Río Grande lodge (Tierra del Fuego) costs from US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 per person. A day trip in El Calafate costs $ 440,000 (half-day) or $ 720,000 (full-day) per angler, in pesos.

Does not include flights, fishing license (the lodge estimates about US$ 550 per angler), laundry, or tips. A 50% deposit is required and is non-refundable.
