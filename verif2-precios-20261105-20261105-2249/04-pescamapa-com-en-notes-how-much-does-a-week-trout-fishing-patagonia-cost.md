How much does a week of trout fishing cost in Neuquén and Chubut: all-inclusive lodges (2026/27)
Rates per person for trout lodges in Junín de los Andes, Cholila, Futaleufú, and Trevelin: from 3 to 7 nights, with what each one includes.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
A lodge on the Chimehuín River (Junín de los Andes, Neuquén) costs from US$ 3,950 (3 nights, 2 fishing days) to US$ 6,950 (7 nights, 6 days) per person, from December 2026 to April 2027.
A lodge chain in Chubut lists 7 nights and 6 fishing days from US$ 6,545 (Trevelin) up to US$ 9,200 (Cholila), in high season.
A hosted week in Trevelin, from 6 to 13 March 2027, costs US$ 5,800 per angler and includes licenses and transfers from Esquel.
These prices are per person in double occupancy with shared guide.

Short answer. A week of fly fishing at an all-inclusive lodge in Argentine Patagonia costs, per person and in double occupancy, from US$ 5,800 to US$ 9,200 for 7 nights and 6 fishing days. In Neuquén, a Chimehuín lodge lists the full range, from US$ 3,950 for 3 nights to US$ 6,950 for 7.

How many days are best?
At the Neuquén lodge with the full range, each extra fishing day lowers the price per day: from US$ 1,975 with 2 days to US$ 1,158 with 6. A stay of 4 nights and 3 days is the shortest that justifies the trip; a full week makes the most of the transfer.
