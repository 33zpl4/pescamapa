Fishing in Corrientes from Brazil: license, documents, routes & costs (2026/27)

Which license an angler coming from Brazil needs, approximate cost, what documents to bring, and travel times to Paso de la Patria, Esquina, and Goya.

By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026

Key facts

  • The foreign tourist license (residence outside Argentina) is set in liters of “súper” gasoline (the standard grade, not premium): 16 liters for one day, 20 for two, 24 for three, 28 for four, and 36 for six.
  • With “súper” gasoline at about $ 2,177 per liter, that's approximately $ 35,000 for one day, $ 44,000 for two, $ 52,000 for three, $ 61,000 for four, and $ 78,000 for six.
  • The license is obtained online, 24 hours a day, and the portal is available in Spanish, English, and Portuguese; the registration accepts the Brazilian CPF as a document.
  • To enter Argentina from Brazil you need a physical RG in good condition or a valid passport; digital versions and driver's license (CNH) are not accepted.
  • From Uruguaiana to Esquina is 310 km (4 h 31 min) and from São Borja to Paso de la Patria, 386 km (4 h 45 min), according to Google Maps.

Short answer. To fish in Corrientes, a Brazilian needs the foreign tourist license, which is obtained online and paid in pesos based on the price of “súper” gasoline. Today it costs about $ 35,000 for one day, $ 52,000 for three days, and $ 78,000 for six. To enter, a physical RG in good condition or a passport is enough. From Uruguaiana to Esquina is about 310 km and 4 h 30 min driving.

How much does a fishing license in Corrientes cost for a foreigner?

The liters are from Annex I of Disposición 1144/2024 of the Dirección de Recursos Naturales de Corrientes. The value is updated only with the price of “súper” gasoline sold at the Automóvil Club Argentino in Corrientes Capital: the annex used $ 1,135 per liter in October 2024. We calculated today’s approximate value with $ 2,177 per liter, which is the average at 14 Corrientes stations according to pump prices from the Secretaría de Energía (updated 21 October 2026). It’s not the exact ACA price: the portal shows the current amount at purchase.

Is it worth getting a multi-day license?

Yes. Each extra day costs less: the 3-day license (24 liters) is half the price of three one-day licenses (48 liters, about $ 104,000), and the 6-day license (36 liters) is just over a third of six one-day licenses (96 liters). This is our calculation using the table’s liters.

How do you get the license online?

  • On the portal of the Dirección de Recursos Naturales de Corrientes. It’s available 24 hours and in Spanish, English, and Portuguese, according to the provincial Ministry of Tourism (2019 note, made for Brazilian tourism).
  • Registration requires type and ID number, email, first and last name, phone, location, profile photo, and password.
  • Document: the registration dropdown offers DNI, Brazilian CPF, Uruguayan CI, Argentine passport, and “Others” (screenshot from 6 November 2026).
  • The license has a QR code that the inspector checks with a phone. Bring it, printed or on your phone, on the boat.

We couldn’t confirm which payment methods the portal accepts for foreigners: ask the Dirección de Recursos Naturales ([email protected] or +54 379 4909039). The exemption benefits in the regulation (retirees, people with disabilities, under 15) apply only to those with residence in Corrientes.

What documents do I need to enter Argentina from Brazil?

  • Physical RG in good condition or valid passport. Digital versions are not accepted, including digital RG.
  • CNH is not valid as an entry document.
  • Under 13 years old: need a special authorization; see the process at argentina.gob.ar (“entering the country with a minor”).
  • Some guides and lodges recommend an RG less than 10 years old: this is practical advice, not an official requirement.

According to Argentina’s official tourism site, checked on 28 October 2026. Confirm with Immigration before traveling.

How long does it take from the border?

Google Maps times checked on 6 November 2026; do not include border wait. The Santo Tomé – São Borja crossing, via the International Integration Bridge, is open 24 hours (National Security Ministry; phone (3756) 420035).
