This adventure begins by fishing the river during the 2005 season and being impressed by the quantity and quality of trout. It is from that moment and when we saw that there was no place to stay, we started looking for land with a river coast to buy and build. After a long wait in 2013, we were able to find an incredible place, ideally located since it is in the middle of the 90 km of river that the limay has from the dam to the source of the lake.

The following year construction began in a place away from everything, luckily the team worked hard to be able to be operational in 2015, which was our first season. From that moment it is that our guides have worked tirelessly to know each secret of the river, that is why today the LRL guides, after many years and hundreds of days on the river, know it better than anyone.
