The Lodge
Voted one of the Top 72 boutique hotels in the world by Taschen's, World's top fishing Lodge for 2011 by Sporting Classics & Lodge of the Year by Anglers Choice Awards in 2014
Tipiliuke was first discovered as one of the world’s premier fly fishing operations in Patagonia over 20 years ago.
Since then it has become famous for its incredible service, attention to the smallest detail, and luxury in a breathtaking setting.
The lodge / The bar / Cocktail time ready!
Located just minutes from the airport of San Martín De Los Andes (Chapelco), Tipiliuke Lodge is nestled at the heart of a 50,000 acre working cattle ranch.
The lodge itself is designed to harmonize with its natural surroundings, constructed with locally sourced materials and featuring rustic charm. Its cozy atmosphere offers a peaceful sanctuary, allowing guests to connect with nature while enjoying modern comforts.
Tipiliuke has just 9 guest rooms (all of which are en suite), allowing the team to focus on providing every guest with highly personalised service and exceptional attention to detail.
Wi-Fi is available throughout the lodge as well as at our variety of outdoor seating areas where guests can take in a range of dramatic Patagonian views.
As the day ends, the evening sky transforms into a canvas of stars, illuminating the vastness of the Patagonian night. In the evenings, while enjoying one of our famous Argentinian malbecs, guests can relax outside around the fire.
Guests also have access to our wood fired sauna, massage room, yoga studio and hot tub
