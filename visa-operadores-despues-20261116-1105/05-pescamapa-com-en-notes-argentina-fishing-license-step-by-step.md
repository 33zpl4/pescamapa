Argentina fishing license: how to get it, step by step
Which permit you need depending on where you fish in Argentina, where each one is issued, what the official portals ask for and how they are paid: read on 16 November 2026.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 16 November 2026
Key facts
There is no national fishing license in Argentina: you need the permit of the region where you fish, and each region has its own portal.
Patagonia (Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and the National Parks) shares one permit, sold online on the Neuquén portal and on Río Negro’s; Santa Cruz, Tierra del Fuego and Corrientes each issue their own.
Santa Cruz: online request, with passport accepted as ID; the portal says payment is by bank transfer or MercadoPago in pesos and that it does not accept transfers in foreign currency.
Tierra del Fuego: online registration on siped.ar, then the permit is issued at a point of sale with your ID, paid on the spot.
No official source fixes a lead time; according to one lodge, the permit is arranged a few days before arrival. Read on 16/11/2026.

Which permit: the one of the region. Patagonia (Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, National Parks) shares one; Santa Cruz, Tierra del Fuego and Corrientes have their own (table below).
Where: online in Patagonia, Santa Cruz and Corrientes; in Tierra del Fuego you register on siped.ar and the permit is issued at a point of sale.
Can a foreigner pay online? Santa Cruz says it takes bank transfers in pesos and MercadoPago, not foreign-currency transfers. The other portals we read do not say which payment methods they take.
Documents: an ID document; Santa Cruz and Tierra del Fuego list the passport as an option on their forms.
How early: no official source gives a lead time; according to one lodge, the permit is arranged a few days before arrival.

Patagonia: Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and National Parks
Two official sites sell the Patagonian permit with the same values: the Neuquén Wildlife Directorate portal and the Río Negro Mesa Directiva portal. We read both; the 2026/2027 regulation is published on the interprovincial regulation site
. According to Río Negro’s page, its electronic permit is valid in all fishing waters of Patagonia except Tierra del Fuego and Santa Cruz.
Choose where to buy: Neuquén’s “Comprar permiso”
page, which lists the non-resident categories and says non-resident foreigners pay, or Río Negro’s portal
.
Choose the non-resident category: 1 day, 10 days or full season. A 10-day permit for non-residents from neighbouring countries appears on both portals.
Fill in the form and select the payment method (Río Negro’s “how to buy”
). The pages we read do not list the payment methods.
You receive the credential by e-mail. Río Negro says to print it and carry it, or show it on your phone, and to check the spam folder if it does not arrive (its FAQ
).
Trolling, Neuquén’s preferential zone and Laguna Blanca need an extra permit, listed on the Neuquén portal; the trolling permit must be bought together with an ordinary one.
Values, validity and what changes by province are in our Patagonian permit note
, including Chubut’s digital permit. Neuquén’s portal also lets you request its trolling or preferential-zone permit by attaching a permit from Río Negro or Chubut.
Santa Cruz
The Santa Cruz portal says the 2026-2027 permits are available for the season that runs from 01/10/2026 to 30/09/2027 and that the procedure is done online (read 16/11/2026).
Go to pesca.santacruz.gob.ar
and choose “Solicitar Permiso”.
Have your documents ready: the portal’s list names the front of the ID document and the proof of payment. The form offers passport as a document type and a box “No soy residente del país” (I am not a resident of the country).
Choose the permit type; the non-resident categories are daily, weekly and full season (values page
).
Pay by bank transfer or MercadoPago, in pesos, using the data on the values page, and attach the proof of payment. The page says it does not accept transfers in foreign currency, and that a MercadoPago payment must carry the holder’s full name.
The portal says the permit is valid to fish from the moment it is created and that the request stays pending administrative validation.
The Santa Cruz River steelhead, Jurassic Lake, the Río Gallegos and El Calafate are all in Santa Cruz: see the guides linked in the table.
Tierra del Fuego
The 2026-2027 season runs from 30 October 2026 to 2 May 2027 (Resolución 259/26 of the Secretaría de Ambiente, page read on 16/11/2026
).
Register on siped.ar
. The “No residente país” form asks for name, surname, document type (ID card or passport), document number, date of birth and local address or lodging, among other data (form
). SIPED says you can load your data, including a photo, before the permit is issued.
Go to a point of sale with your ID document. SIPED says the registration is completed there, the virtual credential is generated and the permit is issued, paid at that moment. The points of sale are listed on siped.ar
(Ushuaia, Río Grande and Tolhuin).
You get a proof of issue, which you must show with your ID document to the inspector.
The plastic credential is collected at the point of sale; SIPED says it is valid for two years, but each season’s permit is paid again (SIPED, “Acerca”
).
The preferential zones (Grande and Menéndez rivers, Ewan Sur, Irigoyen and Lake Fagnano) have an additional permit: see the table in fishing license for foreigners in Argentina 2026/27
. The Secretaría de Ambiente also runs an access system for some fishing grounds (SAPTDF); the page we read describes it for Tierra del Fuego residents, so ask your guide or the Dirección General de Recursos Hídricos what applies to you.
Corrientes
The Corrientes Tourism Ministry lists fishing licenses under “Trámites y permisos” and sends you to the Dirección de Recursos Naturales portal (read 16/11/2026).
Open the Recursos Naturales portal
and choose “Registrarme”. The registration asks for ID type and number, e-mail, name, phone, location, a profile photo and a password (registration
).
Buy the tourist license for foreigners, which is set in liters of gasoline and runs from 1 to 6 days: the table and the calculation are in fishing in Corrientes from Brazil
.
The license carries a QR code that the inspector checks; carry it printed or on your phone.
The pages we read do not detail the payment methods for a foreigner; the Dirección de Recursos Naturales is the one to ask (contact details on its portal).
Entre Ríos
Entre Ríos publishes the procedure “Obtener licencia y permiso de caza o pesca deportiva” (last updated 02/10/2026 on the portal, read 16/11/2026).
Document the portal lists: the ID document of the holder.
Generate the license or permit and make the payment.
It is a personal procedure, done before fishing, at one of the offices the portal lists (Paraná, La Paz, Concordia, Victoria and Villa Paranacito, among others).

What to carry and what to keep
Your passport or the identity document you used: Río Negro says to show the permit and an identity document at every inspection.
The permit, printed and on your phone: Río Negro says either is valid; in Tierra del Fuego you show the proof of issue with your ID document.
The proof of payment and the confirmation e-mail: Santa Cruz asks for the proof when you apply, and Río Negro asks you to send your ID number if the credential does not arrive.
A copy that works without signal: a screenshot or a PDF saved on your phone; many fishing waters have no coverage.

Frequently asked questions
Do I need a fishing permit in Argentina as a tourist?
Yes. There is no national license: you need the permit of the region where you fish, from the portals listed in the table above (read on 16/11/2026). Santa Cruz’s portal states that the permit is personal, nominative and non-transferable.
Can the lodge get the permit for me?
It depends on the operator. In the nine packages we compared (November 2026) the license was included in 4, not included in 3 and unspecified in 2; one Bariloche operator arranges it and charges it separately, at US$ 40 per person per day. According to one lodge, the permit is arranged a few days before arrival. Ask in writing who buys it and in whose name.
