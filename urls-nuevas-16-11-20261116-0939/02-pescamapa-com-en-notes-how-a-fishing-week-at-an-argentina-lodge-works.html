How a fishing week at an Argentina lodge works

How a fishing week at a lodge is put together: formats (full week, half week and packages by nights), arrival and departure days, anglers per guide, rooms, a companion who does not fish and what is usually included. Each fact with the page that says it and the date we read it.

By Equipo Pescamapa · 16 November 2026

Key facts

  • The usual pattern on the pages we read: 7 nights and 6 fishing days (6.5 on some), arriving and leaving on a Saturday; there are half weeks and packages of 3 to 6 nights (read between 30/10 and 16/11/2026).
  • Anglers per guide or boat: two at the dorado and sea-run trout places we read; one guide for two or three anglers at Jurassic Lake.
  • A double room is the base of the prices; a single room costs extra: 50% according to the page of a place at Itá Ibaté and 60% at one in Corrientes according to our note.
  • A companion who does not fish is possible and paid separately: US$ 450 a night at one place, US$ 650 at two others and US$ 800 at a third (pages read on 10 and 13/11/2026).
  • No page says how many months ahead to book; one of them puts the flight and trip insurance “90+ days out” from departure.

Short answer. On almost every page we read, a fishing week at a lodge is a fixed program of 7 nights and 6 fishing days (6.5 on some), Saturday to Saturday, in a double room, with meals and a guide shared by two anglers; depending on the place it also includes gear, transfers and the license. There are also half weeks and packages of 3 to 6 nights. This page gathers what the pages of several places say, each fact with its source and the date we read it. It is not a general rule and not a quote for your dates.

To put the whole trip in order, from the species to asking for options, in eight steps: How to plan a fishing trip to Argentina, step by step.

  • Formats: a full week of 7 nights, a half week (3 or 4 nights) and packages of 3 to 6 nights.
  • Arrival and departure: Saturday to Saturday at most places; there are Friday-to-Friday and Saturday-to-Friday weeks, and half weeks from Tuesday to Saturday or Saturday to Tuesday.
  • Fishing: two sessions a day, morning and afternoon, with lunch and a break in between; two anglers per guide at almost all of them.
  • Room: double by default; a single room and a companion who does not fish are charged extra.
  • It changes from place to place: license, flights, transfers, gear and tips.
  • Availability: we confirm it when you ask us for options.

The formats: full week, half week and packages by nights

What each place’s page publishes, read between 30/10 and 16/11/2026. Some pages count an extra half fishing day (6.5 days) in the 7-night week; the column copies what each one says.

For a trout week in northern Patagonia, our price note says that the places of one chain publish Saturday-to-Saturday weeks and that some accept stays of another length (5/11/2026).

What a fishing day looks like

According to the pages, the day is split into two sessions, one in the morning and one in the afternoon, with lunch and a break in between. What they publish:

  • Pirá Lodge: you leave at 8:00 or earlier, fish until about 12:30, return for lunch and head out again around 16:30 until dusk; dinner is around 21:00 (Fly Water Travel, 13/11/2026).
  • Pinti’s Dorado on the Fly: the morning runs from 7:30 to 12:00 and the afternoon from 15:45 until 21:00 or 22:00, with lunch and a siesta in between (Yellow Dog, 13/11/2026).
  • Itatí Lodge: early fishing, lunch at the lodge and back out after a short break (Set Fly Fishing, 15/11/2026).
  • Las Buitreras: two sessions of 4 to 5 hours with a long midday break; each guide works the same beat all week and anglers rotate beats and guides each session (Fly Water Travel, 13/11/2026).
  • Jurassic Lake: you fish wading or from the bank, not from a boat (jurassiclake.com, 10/11/2026).
  • A day off: depending on the place and the length of the trip, Set Fly Fishing recommends considering a rest day in the middle of the trip to look around (its travel guide, 15/11/2026).

How the week is put together, end to end

  1. Buenos Aires. The itineraries we read go through Buenos Aires: international flights land at Ezeiza (EZE) and domestic flights leave from Aeroparque (AEP), about an hour apart according to Set Fly Fishing (15/11/2026). Nervous Waters recommends spending a night in the city before the flight to the place (15/11/2026).
  2. Flight and transfer. For dorado in Corrientes, Set Fly Fishing names Corrientes airport (CNQ) as the preferred one and Resistencia (RES) as the alternative; in Santa Cruz, Las Buitreras asks for a flight to El Calafate and a transfer, and Jurassic Lake runs a charter from El Calafate on Saturdays.
  3. Arrival day. Río Manso Lodge says in its FAQ that arrival day is for settling in, meeting the guides and a welcome dinner, and that fishing starts the next morning (read 30/10/2026). The other pages do not say.
  4. The fishing days. Six in a row at Pirá Lodge, according to the Fly Water Travel itinerary, and Sunday to Friday at Las Buitreras.
  5. Departure. At Pirá Lodge it is day 9 of the itinerary; at Las Buitreras, Saturday, with a flight to El Calafate and a connection to Buenos Aires.

How many anglers per guide or boat

Two is the usual number. Pirá Lodge: two per guide (Fly Water Travel); Itatí Lodge: two at a time in the boat (Fly Water Travel), both pages read on 13/11/2026. Pinti’s: two anglers per boat (Yellow Dog, 13/11/2026). Las Buitreras and Glencross: two per guide (Fly Water Travel, 13/11/2026). At Jurassic Lake, one guide takes two or three anglers depending on the beat (jurassiclake.com, 10/11/2026). At the Nervous Waters places we read, the base is a double room and a shared guide, and a single room and boat depend on availability and can cost more (their FAQ, 15/11/2026).

Double room and the single-room surcharge

The per-person prices on these pages are for a double room. If you travel alone and want your own room and boat, the surcharge varies a lot: 50% for a single room and boat according to the Pinti’s page on Yellow Dog (13/11/2026); 60% of the package at one place in Corrientes and US$ 200 a night at one in Chubut according to our note on what a package includes; a single-guide supplement of US$ 250 per angler per day at Río Manso Lodge (its rates page, 10/11/2026). Kau Tapen and Villa María come with a single room by default (Nervous Waters, 15/11/2026).

If someone who does not fish comes along

It is possible and paid separately. Pinti’s Dorado on the Fly publishes US$ 450 a night for a non-angler and warns that it is not a great option for companions, because it offers no guided non-fishing activities (Yellow Dog, 13/11/2026). Villa María and Kau Tapen: US$ 650 a night (our note, Nervous Waters, 13/11/2026). Río Manso Lodge: US$ 800 a night or US$ 5,400 a week, with guided non-fishing activities included (its rates page, 10/11/2026). Pirá Lodge mentions horseback riding, birdwatching and night wildlife outings (Nervous Waters, 13/11/2026), and Set Fly Fishing lists horseback rides, wine tasting, birdwatching and spa (its travel guide, 15/11/2026). The Los Plateados page mentions no non-fishing activities. If someone who does not fish is coming, it is one of the first things to tell us.

What is usually included and what is not

It changes from place to place, which is why two prices are only comparable when they cover the same things: what an Argentina fishing package includes and what is extra. What these pages say:

  • License: included at Itatí Lodge and not included at Pirá Lodge, according to Fly Water Travel (13/11/2026); Nervous Waters says it asks for your details to arrange it before arrival (15/11/2026). How to get it: Argentina fishing license, step by step.
  • Flights and transfers: Itatí Lodge includes the charter flights between Buenos Aires and the lodge; international flights are left out where they are mentioned (Itatí, Pirá). Nights in Buenos Aires are not included either.
  • Gear: loan rods, reels and lines are included at Pirá Lodge, at Pinti’s and at Suindá Lodge (“at no additional cost”); Río Manso Lodge charges US$ 80 per angler per day for a full set. For the trip, Set Fly Fishing warns that Argentine airport security does not allow rods or reels in the cabin on domestic flights: they go in checked baggage (15/11/2026).
  • Laundry: included at Pinti’s (Yellow Dog) and not included at Itatí Lodge (Fly Water Travel).
  • Tips: not included. Set Fly Fishing suggests 12% to 20% of the cost of the trip in a shared pool split between guides, kitchen and housekeeping staff (15/11/2026); Jurassic Lake suggests US$ 80 to 100 per person per day (our note).

Weather, wind and why many offers are by the week

The pages do not explain why offers are by the week. What they do show: fixed departures around one day (Jurassic Lake flies a charter every Saturday morning, according to its location page), long journeys to the place (Fly Water Travel warns that Pirá Lodge calls for being ready for a long travel program) and few spots per week, 6 to 12 anglers in the table above.

  • Dorado and heat: according to Set Fly Fishing (15/11/2026), from December to March days average 27 to 32 °C and can reach about 38 °C, though that is rare; in April and May and from September to November they run 16 to 25 °C. Its Itatí Lodge page says “September–May (avoid Jan–Feb for heat)”. Two sessions a day avoid the midday heat (Fly Water Travel).
  • Trout in Patagonia: Río Manso Lodge says that from November to April days run 13 to 24 °C and that it can rain at any time (its FAQ, 30/10/2026).
  • Wind: Fly Water Travel says of Las Buitreras that wind is a constant and should be expected; Tres Amigos Outfitters recommends a fly line built for the often windy conditions in its Santa Cruz guide (14/11/2026).
  • If the weather or the water stops the fishing: at Pirá Lodge you can go back to the pool or go out to watch wildlife or ride horses (Fly Water Travel, 13/11/2026). No page we read says what happens to the price in that case: ask in writing.

The dates of each season and the closed seasons are in the fishing calendar.

What if you want fewer days

Some pages publish shorter programs with their own price: 3 to 6 nights at Pirá Lodge and Suindá Lodge (Nervous Waters, 13/11/2026), 4 nights and 3 days at Itatí Lodge, a half week at Glencross and at Los Plateados, and 3 and 4 nights at Jurassic Lake. Others say it in a sentence: Yellow Dog writes that at Pinti’s shorter stays are also welcome; Fly Water Travel, that Pirá Lodge accepts stays of varying length; Tres Amigos Outfitters, that it can customize the length of the stay (16/11/2026); and Jurassic Lake offers tailor-made itineraries, arriving from another city or staying longer (10/11/2026). How the price changes with fewer days is in each place’s rate: ask in writing.

When to book

None of the pages we read says how far ahead to book the week. What one of them, Set Fly Fishing, does publish is a preparation checklist: 90 days or more before departure, the final payment, the flights and the trip insurance; 60 days or more, the flight details and the gear gathered; 7 days out, re-confirm the flights (15/11/2026). It is the timeline of one place, not a rule. Since each week has few spots (6 to 12 anglers in the table), asking early is the prudent thing.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a fishing week at a lodge?

On the pages we read, 7 nights and 6 fishing days; Jurassic Lake and Villa María count 6.5 fishing days in the 7-night week (read between 10 and 13/11/2026). Los Plateados counts 6 fishing days from Saturday to Friday (16/11/2026).

Which day do you arrive and which day do you leave?

Most weeks run Saturday to Saturday (Las Buitreras, Glencross, Jurassic Lake and, according to Yellow Dog, most Pinti’s packages). Los Plateados runs Saturday to Friday and the Villa María week, Friday to Friday. Half weeks run Tuesday to Saturday or Saturday to Tuesday at Jurassic Lake, and Wednesday to Saturday or Saturday to Wednesday at Glencross.

Is there a half week or are there shorter packages?

Yes, at several: 3 to 6 nights at Pirá Lodge and Suindá Lodge, 4 nights and 3 days at Itatí Lodge, a half week at Glencross and at Los Plateados, and 3- and 4-night programs at Jurassic Lake (January to April). What each costs is on each place’s page; some of our notes gather the prices with their date: how much a dorado fishing trip costs.

How many anglers go per guide or boat?

Two at Pirá Lodge, Itatí Lodge, Pinti’s, Las Buitreras and Glencross; one guide for two or three anglers at Jurassic Lake, depending on the beat. Sources and dates above.

Do I stay in a double room? How much is a single room?

The prices on these pages are per person in a double room. A single room is extra: 50% for room and boat at Pinti’s (Yellow Dog, 13/11/2026), 60% of the package at one place in Corrientes and US$ 200 a night at one in Chubut (our note on what a package includes), and US$ 250 per person per day for a single guide at Río Manso Lodge (10/11/2026).

Can a companion who does not fish come?

Yes, and it is paid separately: US$ 450 a night at Pinti’s (which warns it offers no guided non-fishing activities), US$ 650 at Villa María and Kau Tapen, and US$ 800 a night at Río Manso Lodge, with guided non-fishing activities included. Sources and dates above.

What does a week include and what does it not?

It depends on the place. On the pages we read, lodging, meals, the guide and loan gear are usually in; the license, flights, nights in Buenos Aires, laundry and tips change from place to place. Itatí Lodge includes the license and charter flights; Pirá Lodge includes neither the license nor flights (Fly Water Travel, 13/11/2026).

What happens if the weather or the river stops the fishing?

No page we read explains what happens to the price. They do say what is done that day: at Pirá Lodge you can go back to the pool, watch wildlife or ride horses (Fly Water Travel, 13/11/2026). Ask in writing, along with the payment and date-change terms.

How far ahead should I book?

No page we read says. Set Fly Fishing publishes a checklist: 90 days or more before departure, the final payment, flights and insurance; 60 days or more, flight details (15/11/2026). Since weeks have 6 to 12 spots, asking early is the prudent thing.

How is availability for a week confirmed?

The place itself confirms it. When you ask us for options, we ask the places that fit and reply within 48 hours, side by side. It is free for you and the price is the place’s own.

Everything on this page comes from public pages read between 30 October and 16 November 2026, each with its date, and from our dated notes. They change every season. We have not stayed at these places: confirm the dates, what is included and the conditions with the place before booking.
