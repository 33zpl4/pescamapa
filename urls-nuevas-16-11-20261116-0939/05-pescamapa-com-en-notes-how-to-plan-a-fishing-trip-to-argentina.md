Step 5. Understand how the week works
On the pages we read, a week at a lodge is usually 7 nights and 6 fishing days, Saturday to Saturday, in a double room with two anglers per guide; there are also half weeks and packages by nights. How a fishing week at a lodge works
gives the formats, the arrival and departure days, what happens with a companion who does not fish and what is included.
Step 6. Flights, transfers and luggage
Everything below is what the pages we read say, and none is an official source: confirm with the airline and the consulate.
Airports: international flights land at Ezeiza (EZE) and domestic flights leave from Aeroparque (AEP), an hour apart according to Set Fly Fishing (15/11/2026). For dorado in Corrientes, Set Fly Fishing names Corrientes airport (CNQ) as the preferred one and Resistencia (RES) as the alternative; for Santa Cruz, Tres Amigos Outfitters says you fly to El Calafate or Río Gallegos (its Los Plateados page
, 16/11/2026).
A night in Buenos Aires: Nervous Waters recommends spending a night in the city before the flight to the place (its FAQ
, 15/11/2026); Set Fly Fishing advises arriving with a margin so a delay does not cost fishing days (its travel guide
, 15/11/2026).
Rods and reels: Set Fly Fishing warns that Argentine airport security does not allow rods or reels in the cabin on domestic flights: they go in checked baggage. It also recommends a change of clothes in your carry-on in case your luggage is delayed.
Documents: Set Fly Fishing asks for a passport valid for at least 6 months after the trip, and Nervous Waters and Set Fly Fishing say citizens of the US, Canada and the European Union do not need a visa for tourism. It is not an official source: confirm with the Argentine consulate in your country. The fishing permit is separate: step 3.
Trip insurance: Set Fly Fishing recommends it and puts it on its checklist at 90 days or more before departure (15/11/2026).
Flight prices: we do not publish them; they change by date and airline. Some places include charter flights between Buenos Aires and the place and others do not: each offer says so.
Step 7. What to pack
Gear depends on the species. For dorado, the gear section of the dorado pillar

How do I plan a fishing trip to Argentina?

What do I choose first: the species, the area or the date?
The species, because it almost always decides the area and the season. Then check the fishing calendar

Do I need a permit to fish?
Yes. There is no national license: you need the permit of the region where you fish, and each region has its own portal. It is explained in Argentina fishing license, step by step
