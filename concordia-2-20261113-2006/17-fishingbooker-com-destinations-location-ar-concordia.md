Fishing in Concordia
Concordia, Argentina, stands as a premier destination for anglers seeking world-class Golden Dorado fishing. Nestled on the Uruguay River below the Salto Grande Dam, this region offers exclusive access to 'La Zona'—a legendary 1.5-mile stretch where only four boats fish at a time. Here, record-setting Dorado exceeding 55 pounds thrive in the nutrient-rich currents, providing year-round opportunities for both fly fishing and conventional tackle enthusiasts.
Seasonal variations create distinct experiences: from May to October, the cooler waters during spawning season yield fewer but exceptionally large specimens ideal for trophy hunters. November through April brings warmer temperatures and aggressive surface strikes, with fish actively attacking poppers, minnow imitations, and flies amidst longer daylight hours. Beyond Dorado, the Uruguay River teems with hard-fighting Surubí and Pacú, adding diversity to your adventure.
Accessibility is streamlined through Buenos Aires airports, with convenient transfers to Concordia's specialized lodges. These remote accommodations prioritize angler comfort—offering private rooms, curated culinary experiences featuring local catches, and direct river access. With its unique blend of seclusion, record-breaking potential, and meticulously managed fisheries, Concordia delivers an unparalleled freshwater angling frontier where every cast could yield the catch of a lifetime.
