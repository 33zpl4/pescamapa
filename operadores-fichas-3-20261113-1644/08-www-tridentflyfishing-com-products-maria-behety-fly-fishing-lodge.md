Trip Summary
Estancia Maria Behety Lodge offers dedicated anglers extensive private access to the middle and lower Rio Grande, one of the world’s most established fisheries for large sea-run brown trout. Located on a historic working estancia in Tierra del Fuego, the program accommodates just 12 anglers and rotates pairs through more than 100 named pools with experienced, English-speaking guides. Long morning and evening fishing sessions are complemented by comfortable accommodations, traditional Argentine cuisine, and a relaxed lodge atmosphere overlooking the surrounding pampas.

Private
access to the middle and lower Rio Grande
Maria Behety gives anglers access to an extensive stretch of the Rio Grande containing more than 100 named pools. The lodge’s location provides convenient reach to varied water throughout the middle and lower river, with private beat rotations designed to prevent crowding.

The Rio Grande is one of the world’s most established fisheries for large sea-run brown trout. Anglers encounter a broad range of fish throughout the season, including powerful trout in the teens and the legitimate possibility of fish exceeding 20 pounds.

The 12-angler lodge combines a serious fishing program with comfortable en suite rooms, hearty Argentine cuisine, and inviting common spaces overlooking the pampas. A heated wader room, central fireplace, and open bar make it easy to relax and prepare between long sessions on the river.

Seasonal Fishing
Calendar
January
45-60 °F
Sea Run Brown Trout
January opens the season with long daylight hours and a strong presence of bright, recently arrived sea-run brown trout. River levels and weather can vary, but fish are often active throughout the system. Floating and intermediate lines may be used when conditions allow, while Skagit heads, sink tips, nymphs, and streamers provide additional flexibility.
February
45-60 °F
Sea Run Brown Trout
By February, increasing numbers of sea-run brown trout are typically distributed throughout the river’s pools. Water can become lower and clearer, favoring smaller flies, longer leaders, and lighter presentations, although changing weather may require heavier sink tips. February remains windy, but favorable conditions can create opportunities to fish floating lines and surface-oriented presentations.
March - Early April
35-55 °F
Sea Run Brown Trout
Late season brings cooler temperatures, shorter days, and a mixture of bright fish and trout that have spent more time in freshwater. Conditions can range from low and clear to high and discolored following rainfall, making versatility especially important. Anglers may use small nymphs and lighter tips in clear water or larger streamers and heavier sinking lines when flows rise.
Lodging &
Accommodations
Accommodations
Estancia Maria Behety Lodge accommodates up to 12 anglers in eight comfortable, en suite guest rooms configured for double occupancy. The additional rooms allow a limited number of guests to reserve private accommodations for an added fee. The lodge’s spacious great room features high ceilings, a central fireplace, an open bar, and broad views across the surrounding pampas. A heated wader room provides space for drying gear between fishing sessions, while the main lounge and dining areas offer a relaxed setting for unwinding after long days on the river.
Food
Meals are built around the lodge’s split fishing schedule and highlight traditional Argentine ingredients and hospitality. Breakfast is served before the morning session, followed by a substantial midday meal with wine and dessert before the afternoon siesta. Traditional grilled meats and asados are an important part of the experience, alongside fresh vegetables, regional dishes, and Argentine wines. After the evening session, guests return to the lodge for hors d’oeuvres and a late dinner.
Connectivity
Complimentary WiFi is available at the lodge and is generally sufficient for email and basic communication. Because Maria Behety is located in a remote area, service may slow or become unavailable during adverse weather. Cellular coverage is limited and should not be relied upon during the trip.
Target Species
Sea Run Brown Trout
Sea-run brown trout are migratory brown trout that spend part of their lives feeding in saltwater before returning to freshwater rivers to spawn. Their ocean-based diet allows them to grow larger, heavier, and more powerful than many resident trout, with broad shoulders, silvery flanks, and remarkable endurance. Anglers typically target them by swinging streamers with single- or two-handed rods, often in demanding conditions where presentation, line control, and persistence are essential. Their size, strength, and unpredictable nature have made sea-run brown trout one of the most sought-after gamefish in the world.

11-Day Itinerary
Each day is carefully planned to maximize your fishing success while experiencing the best of what this destination has to offer. Itineraries are flexible based on conditions and guest preferences.
GET IN TOUCH
Day 1-2
From Home to Buenos Aires, Argentina
A week at Estancia Maria Behety Lodge runs from Saturday to Saturday. Most travelers depart home on Thursday evening and arrive at Ezeiza International Airport (EZE) on Friday morning. After transferring to a pre-arranged hotel in Buenos Aires, you will have the remainder of the day to rest or explore the city. Some guests choose to arrive earlier and spend additional time in Buenos Aires before continuing south.
Day 3
From Buenos Aires to Maria Behety Lodge
On Saturday, you will transfer from your hotel to the appropriate Buenos Aires airport, most commonly Jorge Newbery Airport (AEP), for your flight to Rio Grande (RGA). Upon arrival, a lodge representative will meet you and provide the approximately 45-minute transfer to Maria Behety Lodge. After settling in, you will attend an orientation, meet the guide team, and prepare your equipment for the week. Fishing begins the following morning.
Day 4-9
The Fishing Days
Each fishing day includes separate morning and evening sessions. After breakfast, pairs of anglers depart with their guide to fish a private rotation of pools before returning to the lodge for the main midday meal, Argentine wines, and an afternoon siesta. Anglers head back to the river later in the day and often fish until sunset. Following the evening session, you will return to the lodge for hors d’oeuvres, dinner, and time to relax at the bar. The days are long and fishing-focused, but the split schedule provides time to rest between sessions.
Day 10
From Maria Behety Lodge to Buenos Aires
After breakfast on Saturday morning, you will transfer approximately 45 minutes to Rio Grande Airport (RGA) for your flight to Buenos Aires. Depending on your domestic and international flight schedules, you may connect home that evening or transfer to a pre-arranged hotel for one final night in the city. An overnight is often the more comfortable option and provides additional protection against schedule changes.
Day 11
From Buenos Aires to Home
After breakfast, you will transfer to Ezeiza International Airport (EZE) for your return flight home. Travelers departing on an overnight flight may instead arrive home on the following morning.
Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Estancia Maria Behety Lodge a leading sea-run brown trout destination?
Estancia Maria Behety combines extensive private access, more than 100 named pools, an experienced guide team, and a fishing schedule built around the productive morning and evening periods. Its location on the middle and lower Rio Grande gives anglers access to varied water and one of the world’s most established populations of large sea-run brown trout.
Where is Estancia Maria Behety Lodge located?
Estancia Maria Behety Lodge is located on a historic working estancia near Río Grande in Tierra del Fuego, Argentina. The lodge overlooks the Rio Grande and is positioned near the center of the estancia’s extensive river frontage, providing convenient access to both the middle and lower beats.
When is the best time to fish the Rio Grande for sea-run brown trout?
The Maria Behety fishing season generally runs from early January through early April. January often brings long days and newly arriving fish, while February and early March typically find greater numbers distributed throughout the river. Late March and early April bring cooler weather and shorter days. Every period can produce exceptional fish, and weekly conditions depend heavily on river level, water clarity, temperature, and wind.
