A unique jewel in Patagonia’s Fishing crown 75 km of water; hard-fighting sea trout in every pool.
ACCOMMODATIONACCOMMODATION
About 100 km from Rio Gallegos and its international airport, the lodge retains the character as the Estancia’s family home, with five single occupancy rooms and one large double, all with en-suite bathrooms and comfortable privacy. The living room has a fireplace and sofas and armchair to sink into; there is a piano bar in the adjacent sun room bar for relaxing draught beer, wine or other drinks at the end of every memorable fishing day.
Much of the produce you will eat comes from our own garden, fresh and nutritious; we make our own bread and marmalades. Eggs are the the centrepiece of our breakfast served any way you like; we offer steak, lamb or venison for lunch but can cater to vegetarians too. If there is anything you don’t eat or something you’d like to see on the menu, just tell us. Our chefs’ pleasure is your pleasure. In the evenings we offer a lighter meal of chicken, fish or seafood with soup or salad starters and a choice of wines we have selected ourself to showcase the mastery of Argentina’s winemakers.
There’s comfortable wading room in which to get in and out of your gear. Since its reopening, we have used solar energy for power to cut down on fossil fuel use. We are the first and currently the only lodge in South America to use a wood-pellet furnace to heat our guests and staff. We live and work in an astonishing natural environment and are committed to eco-friendly sustainability.
