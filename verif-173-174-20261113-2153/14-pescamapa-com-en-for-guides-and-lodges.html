For guides and lodges

Add your listing to Pescamapa

Anglers from Argentina, Brazil and abroad read our guides before booking. If you guide, run a lodge or put trips together, your listing goes in your destination's guide, with no monthly fee.

What you get

  • A listing in your destination's guide and on the "Fishing guides in your town" page, in Spanish, Portuguese and English.
  • Qualified inquiries: the angler has already read the rules, the licence and the calendar; it reaches you with dates, group and method.
  • No monthly fee or subscription. If an angler who asked us for options books with you, you pay us a commission on that booking, agreed in advance; the angler pays the same as booking direct.

What we ask

  • Confirm your listing: what is included, how many per boat, what you charge and since when.
  • Three photos of your own (your boat, your trips, your catches) with your name as the author.
  • Answer the inquiries we pass on the same day, even if it is to say you are full.

How it works

  1. Write to us with this form or claim the listing that already exists.
  2. We confirm the facts together; your listing becomes "confirmed by the provider" and moves up the list.
  3. Inquiries for your area reach you by WhatsApp or email; you quote and close.

Already listed and want to correct it or leave?

Listings built from public information say "not yet verified". Ask for a correction or removal and we do it the same day. [email protected]

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to be on Pescamapa?

Nothing to be listed. No fee, no plan. If an angler who came through Pescamapa books with you, you pay us a commission on that booking, agreed in advance with each operator; the angler pays the same as booking you directly.

Why is my listing already published if I never spoke to you?

Because we built it from what you publish on your own channels, attributed and without your contact details, so anglers can find who runs trips in your area. Confirm it and it becomes yours; ask for removal and we take it down.

Do you publish my phone number?

No. Inquiries come to Pescamapa and we pass them to you. Your listing never shows WhatsApp, email or social accounts.

What about reviews?

We do not copy reviews from other sites or invent any. When we have opinions from anglers who travelled through Pescamapa, we publish them with name and date.

We only use your details to contact you and build your listing. Nothing is published without your permission. privacy policy
