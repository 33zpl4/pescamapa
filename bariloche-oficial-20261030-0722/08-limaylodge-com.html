This Lodge is a true eco lodge in the Patagonian wilderness, and the first (and only) lodge on the incredibly productive Middle Limay River in Northern Patagonia featuring world class accommodations and argentinian tradicional cuisine.

About us

This adventure begins by fishing the river during the 2005 season and being impressed by the quantity and quality of trout. It is from that moment and when we saw that there was no place to stay, we started looking for land with a river coast to buy and build.

The Fishing

Probably the richest fishing resource in Patagonia today, the Middle Limay River offers unique fly-fishing opportunities for the international angler.

This is an outstanding tail-water fishery that flows out of Pichi Picun Leufu Dam guaranteeing consistent flow, temperature, clarity and cleanness at all times.
