What it means for fishing

Here the height also rises and falls with the tide and the wind: the daily reading is a reference, not the tide. A south-easterly (sudestada) pushes water up and muddies it; north or west winds bring it down. For fishing, the wind of the coming days matters most.

Alert and evacuation levels are flood thresholds for riverside towns; they say nothing about fishing.

On the Río de la Plata and in the Delta the height also moves with the tide and the wind (the sudestada). The daily reading does not show high or low tide: it is a reference, not the peak.
