Fishing gear and rules in Patagonia: felt soles, hooks, and disinfection (2026/27)
What the Patagonian regulations say about wading footwear, gear brought into the country, hooks, lead, and fishing hours, with the article for each rule.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
Wading boots with felt soles are prohibited in all waters covered by the Patagonian regulations (art. 22).
Anglers entering the country must use new or disinfected gear (art. 27); guides and outfitters must disinfect gear, boats, and vehicles when required by authorities (art. 28).
In rivers and streams, catch and release is mandatory, with only one single barbless hook or a hook with the barb flattened, and the fish must be released alive (art. 17).
Anchors and sinkers with lead, and lures with lead bodies or heads, are prohibited (art. 22).
The season runs from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027; fishing is only allowed during daylight hours and with one rod per angler.

Short answer. In Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, and the Southern National Parks, wading boots with felt soles are not allowed, gear brought into the country must be new or disinfected, and in rivers and streams you must use only one single barbless hook and release the fish alive. Anchors and sinkers with lead are also not allowed, nor are lures with a lead body or head. These are rules from the common 2026/27 regulations; each province also has its own annexes.
Can I use boots with felt soles?
No. Article 22 bans “the use of wading boots with felt soles in all waters covered by these regulations,” meaning in all four jurisdictions (Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut, and National Parks). Bring wading boots without felt soles.
Do I need to disinfect my gear?
Yes, in two cases. Article 27 states that anglers entering the country are required to use new or disinfected gear. Article 28 requires anglers, guides, and outfitters to disinfect gear, boats, and vehicles whenever and however the authorities require. The regulations do not specify the procedure in these articles.

When and how much can you fish?
Season: from 1 November 2026 to 1 May 2027 inclusive (art. 7), with exceptions in each province’s annexes.
Hours: only during daylight (art. 6).
Lakes and lagoons: one fish per day per angler may be kept between 1 December 2026 and 31 March 2027 (art. 18).
Gravel: it is recommended not to wade fine gravel areas in rivers and streams until 1 January 2027, to avoid damaging spawning beds (art. 24).
