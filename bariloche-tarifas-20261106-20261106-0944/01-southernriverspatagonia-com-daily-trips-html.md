Day trip description
What your fishing day looks like.
01
Full Day Trip
Approximately 10–11 hours
Your full-day fishing experience begins with pickup directly from your accommodation in downtown Bariloche at around 8:00 AM. Depending on the river or lake selected for the day, the drive takes approximately 45 minutes to 1 hour and 45 minutes. You will usually be fishing by 9:30 or 10:00 AM.
At midday, we pause for a generous riverside lunch featuring appetizers, grilled Argentine steak, fresh salads and dessert, accompanied by Malbec wine. Refreshed, we return to the water and continue fishing until around 5:00 PM. You will be back in Bariloche at approximately 6:30 PM, with drop-off at your accommodation.
02
Half Day Trip
Approximately 6 hours
Our half-day trips are offered exclusively on the Limay River, allowing you to enjoy a rewarding fishing experience while keeping travel time to a minimum. Your guide will pick you up directly from your accommodation at around 8:00 AM, and you will fish from approximately 9:30 AM until 1:30 PM.
At the end of the trip, we provide a snack before returning to Bariloche. You will be back at your accommodation at around 2:30 PM. Lunch is not included on half-day trips.

Good to know
Frequently asked questions.
If there is anything else you would like to know, send us a message and we will help you plan every detail.
Do I need to know how to fly fish?
Not at all. Our guides patiently teach and assist beginners throughout the day, while adapting the experience to more advanced anglers.
Will my guide speak English?
Yes. Our guides speak fluent English and enjoy sharing their knowledge of fly fishing, Patagonia and Argentine culture.
Can a non-angler join the day trip?
Absolutely. Non-anglers are welcome to enjoy the scenery and river journey, with riverside lunch on full-day trips or a snack on half-day trips. We will help choose the most suitable destination.
Do you provide fishing gear?
Yes. We can provide rods, reels, flies, waders and wading boots. Let us know your sizes and preferences before the trip.
Can you accommodate dietary requirements?
Yes. Vegetarian meals and other dietary requirements can be arranged when we receive the information in advance.
What personal items should I bring?
Bring sunglasses, a hat, rainwear, sunscreen, lip balm and warm layers for Patagonia's changing weather.
