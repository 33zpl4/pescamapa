About Us
Full & Half Day Fly Fishing Trips
If you want to enjoy fishing in the best rivers and lakes of Northern Patagonia, we can recommend our all-inclusive full or half-day trips. We will be able to fish the Limay river basins, which take their waters to the Atlantic Ocean, or the Manso river, which takes its waters to the Pacific Ocean. These two basins are among the most famous and productive basins in the region.
Whether you are an experienced angler or you are taking your first steps in fishing and want to improve, or even if you have never fished before and want to start experiencing the wonderful world of fishing. We recommend our full day trips, where you will have more time to perfect your skills.
We will adapt the trip according to each occasion to improve your expectations and our experienced guides will accompany and advise you at all times. We fish from a Driftboat, which provides all the necessary comforts for the fisherman. We also fish from rafts when the environment in which we fish requires it.
Do you want to share the experience with someone who is not a fisherman? No problem! Take a friend or family member with you to enjoy the natural beauty, capture stunning photos, or simply relax while fishing.
Our all-inclusive packages include:
Experienced and knowledgeable guides.
Fishing gear
Delicious meals and soft drinks.
Transportation for the fishing place
Bilingual guides
Limay River
Full-Day Trips - For One or Two Anglers
Experience the Limay River's magic on a full-day float trip! Anglers have ample time to perfect their cast and target different hatches throughout the day. But fishing isn't the only draw. Invite a friend to share the adventure, whether they prefer photography, relaxation, or simply soaking up the natural beauty. Everyone's welcome to enjoy a day on the river.
Half-Day Trips - For One or Two Anglers
Short on time? Craving a quick fishing fix? Our half-day trips are perfect for you! Enjoy a morning or afternoon on the water, focusing on nearby fishing hotspots. Even if you're alone, bring a friend or loved one to soak up the natural beauty, relax, and take stunning photos while you cast your line. It's the ideal way to experience the outdoors and catch some fish without committing to a full day.
Manso River
Full-Day Trips - For One or Two Anglers
To fully immerse yourself in the beauty of the river and have enough time to explore, we exclusively offer full-day excursions. The Manso River is one of the most beautiful landscapes that you can see within the National Park. Due to the distance from the river to Bariloche, additional travel time is required.
