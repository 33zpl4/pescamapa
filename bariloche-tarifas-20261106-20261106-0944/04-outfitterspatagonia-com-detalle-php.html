San Carlos de Bariloche Fly Fishing Charters and Overnight Float Trips

Outfitters Patagonia operates private fly fishing trips in San Carlos de Bariloche and Nahuel Huapi National Park. With over 20 years of guiding experience in Northern Patagonia, the outfitter provides daily float and wade charters (half-day or full-day) as well as multi-day float camping trips (overnight float trips). Guided charters accommodate both beginner and experienced anglers targeting brown trout and rainbow trout. All trips run strictly on a private basis.


What Features and Equipment Are Included in Outfitters Patagonia Charters?

Every private fly fishing charter and multi-day expedition organized by Outfitters Patagonia includes complete equipment and premium support services at no additional cost:

  • All Fishing Gear Included: High-quality fly rods, reels, lines, terminal tackle, and flies are provided free of charge on all private daily charters and overnight expeditions.

  • Remote Wilderness Access: Multi-day overnight float trips grant exclusive access to secluded Patagonian waters far from civilization, reachable only by boat or through partner private ranches.

  • Adaptive Mobility: Rivers fluctuate across the fishing season, and trout behavior shifts accordingly. If water levels on a specific river rise or conditions change, guides dynamically relocate anglers to alternative waters offering optimal fishing conditions.

  • Accommodations for Non-Anglers: Customized wilderness programs for accompanying travel companions, including guided hiking, white-water rafting, and horseback riding.

  • All-Inclusive Wilderness Expeditions: Multi-day camping float trips feature full campsite setup, meals, and first-class lodging partnerships across partner ranches.

 

Which Rivers Are Covered by Private Charters and Overnight Expeditions?

Outfitters Patagonia operates private charters across the finest river systems in Northern Patagonia:

  • Río Limay: Internationally renowned tailwater known for floating charters targeting world-class legendary trophy brown trout.

  • Río Manso: Crystal-clear river system flowing through pristine National Park landscapes, offering technical fly fishing options.

  • Río Aluminé: Major river featuring diverse water structures, abundant insect hatches, and high trout density.

  • Río Collón Curá & Río Malleo: Classic Patagonian trout waters ideal for dry fly fishing, nymphing, and accessible wade fishing.

  • Río Pichileufu & Río Caleufu: Remote streams accessible through exclusive private ranch agreements and multi-day float camping.
