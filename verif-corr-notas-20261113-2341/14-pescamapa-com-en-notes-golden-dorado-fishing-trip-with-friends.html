Golden dorado fishing trip with friends: how many days, how much it costs, and where to stay (2026/27)

Three ways to plan a golden dorado trip with friends on the Paraná: per-person prices for each, what's included, and how many days are best.

By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026 · Updated 13 November 2026

Key facts

  • Inn in Esquina (Corrientes): 2 fishing days and 3 nights from US$ 605 per person in a group of three and from US$ 705 in a group of two, with meals, boat, guide, and license.
  • Houseboat in the Paraná Delta: 3 nights from US$ 1,260 and 4 nights from US$ 1,680 per person.
  • All-inclusive lodge in Corrientes (Upper Paraná or Iberá): 3 nights and 2 fishing days from US$ 4,050 per person; 7 nights and 6 days US$ 7,950.
  • The more days, the cheaper each fishing day: at an inn in Esquina, it drops from US$ 353 to US$ 326 per person between 2 and 4 days.
  • The extraordinary fishing ban on the Paraná in Corrientes was lifted on 29 September 2026, according to the press (Perfil).

Short answer. For a dorado trip with friends, it’s best to go for 2 to 4 fishing days. The per-person price depends more on the type of lodging than the number of days: from US$ 605 to US$ 705 at an inn (2 fishing days, 3 nights), from US$ 1,260 on a Delta houseboat, and from US$ 4,050 at an all-inclusive lodge. All prices are per person, in US dollars, not including travel.

How many days are best for a dorado trip with friends?

Our opinion, based on published numbers: 3 nights and 2 fishing days if time is short, since a windy or rainy day matters less than on a one-day trip; and 4 nights and 3 days if you’re traveling from far away, as the cost per fishing day drops and travel is already paid. At an inn in Esquina, the per-person, per-fishing-day cost goes from US$ 353 (2 days) to US$ 337 (3 days) and US$ 326 (4 days), for a group of two. These are our calculations.

Multi-day packages at an inn in Esquina

Posada Hambaré, National Route 12, km 688, Esquina. “From” prices as of 4 November 2026; the inn does not publish the season for these rates. Includes lodging; breakfast, lunch, and dinner; soft drinks, water, beer, and unlimited wine; boat, fishing guide, two dozen live baits per person, 50 liters of fuel, and fishing license. Our calculation, not including travel or gear. For 4 and 6 friends, you need to confirm the inn has two boats available on your dates and that each group keeps its price.

What if you prefer a one-day trip?

A guide in Esquina advertises a full day on the boat with guide from $ 480,000 per boat, or $ 160,000 per person if there are three (1 November 2026, in pesos). In Paso de la Patria, none of the operators we checked publish a day rate: offers say “all inclusive” or “inquire.” We do not set the exchange rate between pesos and dollars: confirm it when booking.

Is there a fishing ban on the Paraná in Corrientes now?

Source: the press (Perfil, articles of 4 June and 10, 16 and 29 September 2026), not the text of the orders: we did not read them in the Official Gazette, and where it says “number pending” the article did not give it. Corrientes Department of Natural Resources. We found no ban after 4 November 2026, but the rules allow it to be reinstated if fish concentrations reappear: ask your guide before heading out. Since Order 853/22, dorado and surubí must be released in Corrientes.

Do I need a fishing license?

Yes. In Corrientes, you get the sport fishing license online and carry it with your ID. It’s already included in Esquina inn packages; at lodges it depends: the two lodges in the nights-based price ladder do not include it, while other Corrientes lodges do, according to the operator or agencies (see the table of other lodges in how much a golden dorado fishing trip costs). Always ask.
