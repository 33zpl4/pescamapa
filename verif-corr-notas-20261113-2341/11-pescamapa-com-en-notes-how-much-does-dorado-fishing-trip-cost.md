How much does a dorado fishing trip in Argentina cost: all-inclusive lodges by number of nights (2026/27)
Per person rates for dorado lodges on the Upper Paraná, the Middle Paraná, the Iberá Wetlands, the Paraná in Santa Fe and the Delta, with what each includes and the cost per fishing day.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
· Updated 13 November 2026
Key facts
Two lodges of the same group, one on the Upper Paraná and one in the Iberá Wetlands, charge the same: from US$ 4,050 (3 nights, 2 fishing days) to US$ 7,950 (7 nights, 6 days) per person in double occupancy.
Each extra fishing day lowers the price per day: from US$ 2,025 with 2 days to US$ 1,325 with 6 days at the same lodge.
Iberá Wetlands: one lodge lists 7 nights and 6 days at US$ 6,400 per person in a single room (according to its operator's page, no year and subject to change); the other lodge in the ladder charges US$ 7,950 for the week in a double room.
Paraná in Santa Fe: 4 fishing days from US$ 3,600; a special of 3 days and 4 nights at US$ 2,500 on selected dates from December 2026 to February 2027.
The fishing license and transfers depend on the lodge: check the table before adding them in.

Short answer. An all-inclusive dorado trip at a lodge in Argentina costs, per person, from US$ 4,050 for 3 nights and 2 fishing days to US$ 7,950 for 7 nights and 6 days (in a double room) on the Upper Paraná and in the Iberá Wetlands, in Corrientes. A Middle Paraná lodge lists US$ 3,950 to US$ 7,950, and in the Paraná in Santa Fe there is a special at US$ 2,500. For a cheaper trip, see the friends’ trip at an inn
(from US$ 605).

In Iberá there are two prices for the week: US$ 6,400 in a single room (one lodge in this table) and US$ 7,950 in a double room (the lodge in the ladder). Each operator includes different things: just comparing the number can be misleading.
How many days are best?
The price per fishing day drops with each extra day: in the ladder above it goes from US$ 2,025 with 2 days to US$ 1,325 with 6. If budget is tight, 3 nights and 2 days is the minimum worth considering; if you’re coming from far away, 5 nights and 4 days already covers a bad weather day without losing your week.
What is usually not included in the price?
The fishing license: in Corrientes you get it online. According to their pages, the two lodges in the ladder and the Middle Paraná lodge do not include it (the latter charges it separately, US$ 100 per person); Itatí and the Iberá lodges in the table do include it, according to the operator or agencies, and one agency says the same of the US$ 7,000 Upper Paraná lodge.
Flights and transfers to the lodge, unless listed as included.
