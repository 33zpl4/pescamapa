Step 1. Choose species, area and season

The species almost always decides the area and the season. The fishing calendar gathers the seasons and closed seasons of all our guides. Examples of what the pages we read say: at Itatí Lodge the season is September to May and January and February are best avoided because of the heat (Set Fly Fishing, 15/11/2026); at Los Plateados the steelhead season runs March to May (Tres Amigos Outfitters, 16/11/2026); on the Río Grande, fishing is open from 30 October 2026 to 18 April 2027 (Resolución 259/26, read on 16/11/2026). To choose in more detail: the dorado pillar, when to fish dorado, the trout in Patagonia pillar and when to fish trout in Patagonia. The guides by destination are on the dorado page and the trout page.

Step 2. Choose the style you want

Size, numbers, sight fishing and wildlife change from place to place. What each place’s page says, attributed and with the date we read it:

All our trout and dorado guides, with their sources, are at San Martín and Junín de los Andes, Bariloche, Esquel and Trevelin, Jurassic Lake, Río Gallegos, Río Grande, Goya, Esquina and Paso de la Patria.

Step 3. Know which permit you need

There is no national license: each region has its own and each has a portal. How to get it, what they ask for and how it is paid, from the official pages read on 16/11/2026: Argentina fishing license, step by step. The values by region, in pesos and approximate dollars: fishing license for foreigners in Argentina 2026/27.

Step 4. Know what it costs

The figures places publish run from US$ 410 for half a day with a guide near Bariloche to US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 for a week of sea-run trout in Río Grande, according to our overview, with the source and date of each figure. Two prices are only comparable when they cover the same things: what a fishing package includes and what is extra.

Step 5. Understand how the week works

On the pages we read, a week at a lodge is usually 7 nights and 6 fishing days, Saturday to Saturday, in a double room with two anglers per guide; there are also half weeks and packages by nights. How a fishing week at a lodge works gives the formats, the arrival and departure days, what happens with a companion who does not fish and what is included.

Step 6. Flights, transfers and luggage

Everything below is what the pages we read say, and none is an official source: confirm with the airline and the consulate.

  • Airports: international flights land at Ezeiza (EZE) and domestic flights leave from Aeroparque (AEP), an hour apart according to Set Fly Fishing (15/11/2026). For dorado in Corrientes, Set Fly Fishing names Corrientes airport (CNQ) as the preferred one and Resistencia (RES) as the alternative; for Santa Cruz, Tres Amigos Outfitters says you fly to El Calafate or Río Gallegos (its Los Plateados page, 16/11/2026).
  • A night in Buenos Aires: Nervous Waters recommends spending a night in the city before the flight to the place (its FAQ, 15/11/2026); Set Fly Fishing advises arriving with a margin so a delay does not cost fishing days (its travel guide, 15/11/2026).
  • Rods and reels: Set Fly Fishing warns that Argentine airport security does not allow rods or reels in the cabin on domestic flights: they go in checked baggage. It also recommends a change of clothes in your carry-on in case your luggage is delayed.
  • Documents: Set Fly Fishing asks for a passport valid for at least 6 months after the trip, and Nervous Waters and Set Fly Fishing say citizens of the US, Canada and the European Union do not need a visa for tourism. It is not an official source: confirm with the Argentine consulate in your country. The fishing permit is separate: step 3.
  • Trip insurance: Set Fly Fishing recommends it and puts it on its checklist at 90 days or more before departure (15/11/2026).
  • Flight prices: we do not publish them; they change by date and airline. Some places include charter flights between Buenos Aires and the place and others do not: each offer says so.

Step 7. What to pack

Gear depends on the species. For dorado, the gear section of the dorado pillar gathers rods, lines, tippets and flies from what several places publish; for Patagonia, gear and rules for Patagonia says what the regulations require. In addition, according to Set Fly Fishing (15/11/2026): type C or I plugs and 220 V; insect repellent in the dorado areas, where there are mosquitoes and horseflies; copies of the first pages of your passport in different bags and a digital copy; and cash for tips, which it recommends in US dollars.

Checklist before you book

  • Species, area and season chosen, with the opening dates and closed seasons checked in the calendar.
  • Fishing permit identified by region and how to get it (step by step).
  • Price compared only between offers that cover the same things: license, transfers, flights, guide, gear and tips.
  • Length, arrival day and departure day; double or single room; a companion who does not fish.
  • Valid passport (6 months after the trip according to Set Fly Fishing) and trip insurance.
  • Flights with a margin: a night in Buenos Aires before the flight to the place.
  • Rods and reels in checked baggage; a change of clothes in your carry-on.
  • Copies of your passport and permit, printed and on your phone, and cash for tips.

Frequently asked questions

How do I plan a fishing trip to Argentina?

In eight steps: species, area and season; style; permit; cost; how the week works; flights, transfers and luggage; what to pack; and asking for options. Each step on this page leads to the detail with its source and date.

What do I choose first: the species, the area or the date?

The species, because it almost always decides the area and the season. Then check the fishing calendar: for example, Itatí Lodge publishes a September to May season (Set Fly Fishing, 15/11/2026) and Los Plateados a March to May one (Tres Amigos Outfitters, 16/11/2026).

Do I need a permit to fish?

Yes. There is no national license: you need the permit of the region where you fish, and each region has its own portal. It is explained in Argentina fishing license, step by step (official pages read on 16/11/2026).

How much does a fishing trip to Argentina cost?

It depends on the type: the figures places publish run from US$ 410 for half a day with a guide to US$ 12,950 to US$ 14,950 for a week of sea-run trout, each with its source and date in our overview.

What is a fishing week at a lodge like?

On the pages we read, 7 nights and 6 fishing days, Saturday to Saturday, in a double room with two anglers per guide; there are also half weeks and packages of 3 to 6 nights. The detail is in how a fishing week at a lodge works.

Should I write to each place myself or ask for options here?

You can do both. If you write to each place, each one answers with its own format, dates and list of what is included. Here you write once, tell us what you are looking for and within 48 hours you compare the options side by side. The place’s price is the same either way: here it is free for you and you pay the same as if you booked directly.

Can I take my rods on the plane?

Set Fly Fishing warns that Argentine airport security does not allow rods or reels in the cabin and that they go in checked baggage (15/11/2026). Confirm with your airline: each has its own baggage rules.

What documents do I need?

A valid passport (Set Fly Fishing asks for at least 6 months of validity after the trip) and the fishing permit of the region. According to Nervous Waters and Set Fly Fishing, citizens of the US, Canada and the European Union do not need a visa for tourism; it is not an official source, so confirm with the Argentine consulate in your country.

How far ahead should I book?

No page we read says how many months ahead. Set Fly Fishing publishes a checklist: 90 days or more before departure, the final payment, flights and insurance (15/11/2026), and weeks have 6 to 12 spots. Asking early is the prudent thing.

What if someone who does not fish comes along?

It is possible, and it is paid separately at the places we read. The details and published prices are in how a fishing week at a lodge works. It is one of the first things to tell us when you ask for options.
