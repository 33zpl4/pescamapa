Fishing in Corrientes from Brazil: license, documents, routes & costs (2026/27)

Which license an angler coming from Brazil needs, approximate cost, what documents to bring and how long it takes to get to Paso de la Patria, Esquina and Goya.

By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026

Key facts

  • The foreign tourist license (residence outside Argentina) is set in liters of 'súper' gasoline: 16 liters for one day, 20 for two, 24 for three, 28 for four and 36 for six.
  • With 'súper' gasoline at about $ 2,177 per liter, that's approximately $ 35,000 for one day, $ 44,000 for two, $ 52,000 for three, $ 61,000 for four and $ 78,000 for six.
  • The license is obtained online, 24 hours a day, and the portal is in Spanish, English and Portuguese; the registration accepts the Brazilian CPF as a document.
  • To enter Argentina from Brazil you need a physical RG in good condition or a valid passport; digital versions and the driver's license (CNH) are not accepted.
  • From Uruguaiana to Esquina is 310 km (4 h 31 min) and from São Borja to Paso de la Patria, 386 km (4 h 45 min), according to Google Maps.
  • A Brazilian agency lists a fishing package in the city of Corrientes at R$ 1,800 per angler per day (2 per boat) or R$ 1,600 (3 per boat), minimum 3 days, for the 2026 season.

Short answer. To fish in Corrientes, a Brazilian needs a foreign tourist license, which is obtained online and paid in pesos according to the price of ‘súper’ gasoline. Today it costs about $ 35,000 for one day, $ 52,000 for three days and $ 78,000 for six. To enter, a physical RG in good condition or a passport is enough. From Uruguaiana to Esquina is about 310 km and 4 h 30 min driving.

How much does the fishing license cost in Corrientes for a foreigner?

The liters are from Annex I of Disposición 1144/2024 of the Corrientes Directorate of Natural Resources. The value is updated only with the price of ‘súper’ gasoline sold at the Automóvil Club Argentino in Corrientes Capital: the annex used $ 1,135 per liter in October 2024. We calculated today’s approximate value with $ 2,177 per liter, which is the average of 14 stations in Corrientes according to pump prices from the Energy Secretariat (updated 21 October 2026). It is not the exact ACA price: the portal shows the current amount when you buy.

Is it better to get a license for several days?

Yes. Each extra day costs less: the 3-day license (24 liters) is half the price of three one-day licenses (48 liters, about $ 104,000), and the 6-day license (36 liters) is just over a third of six one-day licenses (96 liters). This is our calculation with the liters from the table.

How do you get the license online?

  • On the Corrientes Directorate of Natural Resources portal. It works 24 hours a day and is in Spanish, English and Portuguese, according to the provincial Ministry of Tourism (2019 note, made for Brazilian tourism).
  • The registration asks for type and number of ID, email, first name, last name, phone, location, profile photo and password.
  • Document: the registration dropdown offers DNI, Brazilian CPF, Uruguayan CI, Argentine passport and “Others” (screenshot from 6 November 2026).
  • The license has a QR code that the inspector checks with a phone. Bring it, printed or on your phone, on the boat.

We could not confirm which payment methods the portal accepts for a foreigner: ask the Directorate of Natural Resources (ventas@recnaturalesctes.gob.ar or +54 379 4909039). The exemption benefits in the regulation (retirees, people with disabilities, under 15 years old) apply only to those with residence in Corrientes.

What documents do I need to enter Argentina from Brazil?

  • Physical RG in good condition or valid passport. Digital versions are not accepted, including the digital RG.
  • The CNH is not valid as an entry document.
  • Children under 13: need a special authorization; see the procedure at argentina.gob.ar (“entering the country with a minor”).
  • Some guides and lodges recommend an RG less than 10 years old: this is practical advice, not an official requirement.

According to the official tourism site of Argentina (22 July 2026) and the Migrations page on entry documents, read on 10 November 2026. Migrations accepts a valid passport or valid Brazilian ID card and authorizes a stay of up to three months, extendable for another similar period; the tourism site specifies the RG in physical format and a maximum of 90 days, extendable for another 90. Confirm with Migrations before you travel.

What do they require to enter with a Brazilian car?

According to the ARCA (Customs) page on vehicles registered in MERCOSUR, read on 10 November 2026 (no publication date), the driver needs:

  • Valid ID to travel in MERCOSUR and the tourist document issued by the migration authority.
  • Driver’s license from a MERCOSUR country; the driver must be a resident of the country where the vehicle is registered.
  • Title or other official document proving ownership of the vehicle.
  • Proof of insurance valid in MERCOSUR.
  • If someone other than the owner is driving: a notarized authorization (with the green card proving ownership) or the blue card in their name. Spouses and relatives up to second degree can drive without express authorization, as tourists and proving the relationship.
  • If the car is a rental: the Authorization for Circulation in MERCOSUR (ACM) provided by the rental company replaces part of this documentation.

The length of stay for the car is set by Migrations. A difference between two official sources: the tourism site (22 July 2026) requires “international insurance” and the “complete Temporary Vehicle Admission process”, while ARCA mentions “insurance valid in MERCOSUR” and does not mention that admission. Neither mentions the Green Card. Confirm with your insurer and at the border customs before you travel.

How long does it take to get there from the border?

Google Maps times checked on 6 November 2026; do not include border wait. The Santo Tomé – São Borja crossing, via the International Integration Bridge, is open 24 hours a day (Ministry of National Security; phone (3756) 420035). The Goya row comes from Ruta0, a non-official route site, checked on 10 November 2026.

How much does a package cost in reais?

This is the only rate in reais with a date we found published by an agency, read on 10 November 2026; the page says “2026 season” and does not show a publication date. It is a package in a 5-star hotel with casino in Corrientes city, not in Goya or Esquina. Includes full board with drinks (beer, spirits, wines and soft drinks), 6.20 m boat with guide, fishing gear and lures. Does not include visa or entry authorization, tips, transfer to the area (quoted separately), medical assistance or travel insurance. An agency in Porto Alegre lists three Argentine lodges (Hambaré, in Esquina; Puerto Yacarey and Matute) on its 2026-2027 package page, but does not publish prices.
