About Tres Amigos

Our company was founded and is operated by guides. We know our clientele's wants and needs, and the standard of service they expect. Our goal has been and still is to develop some of the best fly-fishing destinations in South Patagonia through the exploration of remote Santa Cruz Province, while following high environmental practices and, most importantly, following our family values.

Frequently asked questions what do you usually do

How do I book a trip?

Easy, contact us through our e-mail travel@tresamigosoutfitters.com

Skype or call us: +54 9 2966 554999.

Talk to our guides. They’ll give you the best advice.

When is the best time to go?

We’ve got the best option at every part of the season:

  • Estancia Río Pelke: November-April.
  • Río Santa Cruz Steelhead: March-May.
  • King Salmon: November-March.
  • Las Buitreras: January-April.

My partner does not fish, are there other activities?

Estancia Río Pelke and Glacier King program have a variety of activities for the non-fishing ones. Hiking, visiting glaciers, horse-riding, bird watching, etc…

Can I customize my trip?

We can customize your trip combinaing 2 or more of our destinations as well as the lenght of your stay.

How to get there?

After arriving to Buenos Aires, Argentina, normally flying to El Calafate (FTE) and/or Río Gallegos (RGL) where our staff will meet you. All our programs are located in a range of 250 miles along south Santa Cruz province, in Argentine Patagonia.
