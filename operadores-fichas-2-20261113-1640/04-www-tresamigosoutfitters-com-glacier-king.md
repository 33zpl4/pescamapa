Our Glacier King Program, based out of the historic Estancia Cristina lodge, is embedded
amidst the most picturesque scenery that Andean Patagonia and Los Glaciares National Park
have to offer. We are exceptionally fortunate to have exclusive access to the sacred
headwaters of the mighty Caterina River.
This lodge offers luxury accommodation and service with a warm and rustic feel characteristic of the estancia's history. The cuisine is particularly exquisite. All dishes are crafted from traditional recipes, flavors, and locally-sourced products and are coupled with some of the finest wines from across Argentina.

TheFish
King Salmon arrived to Southern Patagonia in the early 2000s.
In this lonely place of the Andes they have developed a numerous population that
grows every year due to the really low fishing pressure and not having many predators
above them.
Every year Chinook salmon move upriver from the Atlantic Ocean, thru the Santa Cruz
river and Argentino lake, to arrive to these pristine waters.

When is the best time to go?
We’ve got the best option at every part of the season:
Estancia Río Pelke: November-April.
Río Santa Cruz Steelhead: March-May.
King Salmon: November-March.
Las Buitreras: January-April.
My partner does not fish, are there other activities?
Estancia Río Pelke and Glacier King program have a variety of activities for the non-fishing ones. Hiking, visiting glaciers, horse-riding, bird watching, etc…
Can I customize my trip?
We can customize your trip combinaing 2 or more of our destinations as well as the lenght of your stay.
How to get there?
After arriving to Buenos Aires, Argentina, normally flying to El Calafate (FTE) and/or Río Gallegos
(RGL) where our staff will meet you. All our programs are located in a range of 250 miles along
south Santa Cruz province, in Argentine Patagonia.
