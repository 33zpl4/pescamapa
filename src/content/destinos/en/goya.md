## Why Goya

Goya sits in southern Corrientes province on the Paraná and is known as the **home of the surubí**. The islands and side channels facing the town are this catfish’s habitat, which gave rise to the **National Surubí Festival**: in 2026 it drew 1,400 teams, and it has been catch and release since 2004.

But Goya is more than the tournament. The province describes it as a mosaic of channels, side channels, sandbanks, lagoons and wetlands, with half-day, full-day and multi-day trips. The **Paraná–Isoró system** adds appeal for longer trips and non-fishing companions: capybaras, caimans, waterbirds and black howler monkeys are often seen along the way.

## The water: Paraná, riacho and Isoró

The **riacho Goya** is an arm of the Paraná right next to town, and its flow depends on the river level. Further out are the **deep holes and drop-offs of the Paraná**. Inland, the **Isoró** is a protected wetland and fish breeding area: official information promotes it mainly for boating and wildlife watching. Check permits and zones before fishing there.

There is no official map of permitted fishing spots. That is why Pescamapa shows **areas**, not exact fishing marks, and never presents a spot as permitted without confirmation.

## Species and techniques

The **surubí** is the signature species. **Golden dorado** and mixed river species round out the fishing, and provincial promotion also mentions pacú, manguruyú and armado. For Corrientes as a whole, the province mentions spinning, trolling and fly fishing. The right technique in Goya depends on the day, the species and the guide: confirm it with your outfitter.

## When to go: read the river before the calendar

The level and trend of the Paraná change everything. Before you travel, check the [INA river alert system](https://alerta.ina.gob.ar/). For example, on 18 October 2026 Goya read 5.28 m and rising, above the alert level (5.20 m). That reading is only a snapshot of that day.

## Choosing a guide

Ask before you book:

- Which water and species they suggest for your dates, and how the current river level affects it.
- How long the trip lasts, where you launch and what the price includes: fuel, tackle, bait, food and transfers.
- Whether the guide is licensed, whether they help with your licence and what happens if the weather turns.
- What safety gear the boat carries (a life jacket for each person) and whether fishing is catch and release.

> **PENDIENTE:** outfitter directory. Each listing is published only with the outfitter’s consent and a verification date.

## Surubí week

The **50th National Surubí Festival** is announced for **28 April to 2 May 2027**. If you plan to compete, read the rules and follow COMUPE’s announcements. If you’re going for the festival, book lodging and transport early and check cancellation policies: it’s the busiest week of the year.

## Getting there

Goya is reached via national route **RN 12** and provincial route **RP 27**, about 220 km from the city of Corrientes and about 715 km from Buenos Aires. Check roadworks and road conditions before you set out.
