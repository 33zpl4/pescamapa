Our family has been actively hunting and fishing for decades, constantly learning and refining details to make our operation a true success. We know that making the time to undertake new adventures is not always easy, that is why we are proud to be able to provide personalized packages where our clients can meet as many goals as possible during their stay with us. To hook a Dorado is an experience in and of itself which can hardly be adequately described by words. Acrobatic leaps accompany finger burning runs as the Dorado tries to escape its fate. The ritual provides a real challenge and quite a workout for the angler.
