EXPERIENCE THE THRILL OF EPIC FISHING ADVENTURES!
Embark on a fishing journey to the Golden Dorado paradise. Whether you choose a solo expedition or share the excitement with another angler, get ready for an unforgettable experience.
We will explore various techniques like trolling, fly fishing, popping, and spinning to pursue your desired catch. We have different options for this destination: fishing day, with accommodation, with transportation - the package is tailored to your needs.
This destination is renowned by anglers worldwide, and the Golden Dorado is considered one of the most powerful freshwater sport fish. In Concordia, you'll have the opportunity to catch record-breaking specimens. The Uruguay River, flowing below the massive Salto Grande Reservoir, sets the stage for remarkable encounters.
All fishing equipment, including rods, reels, and terminal tackle, is included in the package, allowing you to focus solely on your passion for fishing. Please note that fly fishing equipment is not provided, so make sure to bring your own or inquire about rental options. Fishing licenses will be managed by our coordinator, ensuring a hassle-free experience.
In addition to the thrilling fishing action, indulge in complimentary snacks, drinks, and a delicious lunch during your adventure. However, feel free to bring your own provisions if you prefer. If you have any questions or special requests, our coordinator will be here to assist you.
Families are more than welcome to join the fishing excursion, creating lasting memories together. This is an opportunity to share the excitement of fishing with your children and introduce them to the wonders of nature.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Cabal, and Discover debit, credit, and prepaid cards issued abroad, converting transactions to your local currency using a favorable exchange rate. For cash payments in ARS, we apply the Blue Dollar exchange rate on the day of purchase.
