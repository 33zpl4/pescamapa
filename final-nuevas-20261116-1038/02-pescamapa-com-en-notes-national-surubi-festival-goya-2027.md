National Surubí Festival 2027 in Goya: dates, entry cap and prizes
The 50th edition, from 28 April to 2 May 2027: entry cap, prizes, how the contest works and what happened in 2026. With sources and dates.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 16 November 2026
Key facts
The 50th National Surubí Festival (the “Golden Anniversary”) runs from 28 April to 2 May 2027 in Goya, Corrientes (Elonce, 10/8/2026).
Cap of 1,400 teams (about 4,200 anglers): entries for 2027 filled in about two hours, with 1,413 registrations and about 300 teams on a waiting list (Elonce, 23/8/2026).
Prizes worth 1,000 million pesos, the highest in the event’s history according to Elonce (10/8/2026).
It is a boat-based contest with 12 fishing zones of 7 to 8 km each; in Goya every fish is released (the festival’s official magazine, 2025).
In 2026 (49th edition, 27/4 to 3/5) the biggest fish was a 119 cm surubí (Perfil, 4/5/2026).
In Portuguese, surubí is “surubim” or “pintado” (Embrapa, 2015).

Short answer. The 50th National Surubí Festival runs from 28 April to 2 May 2027 in Goya (Corrientes). According to Elonce (10/8/2026)
, the Municipal Fishing Commission (Comupe) opened entries with a cap of 1,400 teams and prizes worth 1,000 million pesos. According to Elonce (23/8/2026)
, the cap filled in about two hours, with 1,413 registrations and about 300 teams on a waiting list, and Comupe was considering raising it.
Dates, entry cap and prizes for 2027
Dates: 28 April to 2 May 2027, in Goya, Corrientes: the 50th edition, which Elonce calls the “Gold Edition” (Elonce, 10/8/2026
).
Entry: through a web system, paying in full or in instalments; each team must list at least two anglers, with tax ID (for collecting prizes) and the size of the official anniversary clothing (Elonce, 10/8/2026
).
Cap: 1,400 teams, which Elonce puts at about 4,200 anglers; Comupe explains the cap by river safety, officiating, logistics and the capacity of the prize dinner (Elonce, 23/8/2026
).
What happened with the cap: 1,413 teams registered in about two hours and about 300 were left out, on a waiting list; Comupe said it was considering raising the number (Elonce, 23/8/2026
).
Prizes: 1,000 million pesos shared among the categories, the highest figure in the event’s history (Elonce, 10/8/2026
).
How the contest works
Elonce describes it as “the biggest boat-fishing tournament in the world”. In the festival’s official magazine for 2025 (Municipality of Goya
, 48th edition, read on 16/11/2026), the contest’s chief official says it is a boat contest where nobody gets off “for fifteen hours”; that the 12 fishing zones of 7 to 8 kilometres each are kept; and the same magazine says “in Goya and in the Fishing World Cup there is no taking of any fish without releasing it” (our translation). That fits the Corrientes rule: surubí is catch and release only (Disposición 853/22
).
What happened in 2026
The 49th edition ran from 27 April to 3 May 2026 with the Costa Surubí grounds as its centre, with more than 1,000 boats announced (Canal 26, 15/4/2026
). According to Perfil (4/5/2026)
, about 1,400 boats took part; a team from Santa Fe won on accumulated points, with 42.40 points and 3 fish, and the biggest fish was a 119 cm surubí, officiated in Zone 11.

What the fish is called
In Argentina it is surubí. In Brazil, according to Embrapa Pesca e Aquicultura (December 2015)
, the species of the genus Pseudoplatystoma are popularly known as “surubim” or “pintado”, and the same name can refer to different species depending on the region. More on the surubí page

National Surubí Festival: frequently asked questions
When is the National Surubí Festival 2027?
From 28 April to 2 May 2027, in Goya, Corrientes: it is the 50th edition (Elonce, 10/8/2026).
How many teams take part?
The cap is 1,400 teams, about 4,200 anglers according to Elonce (23/8/2026). In 2026, Perfil (4/5/2026) counted about 1,400 boats.
How do I enter?
Through a web system, by team (at least two anglers), paying in full or in instalments (Elonce, 10/8/2026). The 2027 cap filled in about two hours and there is a waiting list (Elonce, 23/8/2026): ask Comupe about an increase.
Do you keep the surubí you catch in the contest?
No: the festival’s official magazine (2025) says that in Goya no fish is taken without releasing it, and in Corrientes surubí is catch and release only (Disposición 853/22).
How big was the biggest fish of 2026?
A 119 cm surubí, officiated in Zone 11, according to Perfil (4/5/2026).
