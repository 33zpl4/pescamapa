Key facts
Golden dorado (Salminus brasiliensis) is fished on the Paraná River; our guides cover the Upper Paraná (Paso de la Patria) and the middle Paraná (Goya, Esquina, La Paz).
The places we read on the Upper Paraná in Corrientes repeat October–November and March–May as the best months; some put the peak earlier, from June to October.
In Corrientes golden dorado and surubí are catch and release only (Disposición 853/22, Official Gazette, 26 December 2022).
Visitors from abroad need the Corrientes sport fishing licence, bought online on the provincial Natural Resources portal; we found no rule recognising licences from other countries.
Extraordinary closures change from year to year: check the current ones with the local authority or your guide before you go.
According to the pages of the places we read (13/11/2026): big fish and sight fishing on the Upper Paraná, clear water and wildlife in the Iberá Wetlands.

Short answer. Golden dorado is the fish that brings most anglers from abroad to the Paraná River in Corrientes. The months the places we read repeat most are October–November and March–May on the Upper Paraná; you need the Corrientes sport fishing licence; and in Corrientes the dorado is catch and release only. When is the best time?
The windows differ by place and by stretch, so we do not give a single answer. On the Upper Paraná in Corrientes the sources we checked repeat October to November and late March to May; some places put the peak earlier, from June to October, following the baitfish. The heat of January and February slows daytime fishing. Outside the window you can still fish, with a different technique and lower expectations.

What are the rules in Corrientes?
Golden dorado and surubí: catch and release only. You cannot keep them. Source: Disposición 853/22
of Recursos Naturales (Official Gazette of Corrientes, 26 December 2022; in force until another act replaces it).
Other species have minimum sizes and bag limits (pacú, patí, boga, tararira). They are in our table: size, bag limits and closed seasons
.
Extraordinary closures can be ordered on stretches of the river and they change every year (see the box below).

Which licence do you need?
The Corrientes sport fishing licence, bought online on the Natural Resources portal and carried with your identity document (Corrientes Tourism Ministry: procedures and permits
). The regional recognition agreement (Santa Fe, Entre Ríos and Chaco) is for residents of those provinces and does not cover stretches under exclusively Corrientes jurisdiction (agreement
). We found no rule recognising licences from other countries: visitors from abroad need the Corrientes one. The price and duration of each category are shown when you apply.
How do you plan the trip?
Pick the stretch and the month from the two sections above; ask the guide for the calendar and how far ahead you need to book.
The festival weeks fill up. Around the National Dorado Festival in August, lodging in Paso de la Patria is in high demand: book early.
Check the closures for your dates with your guide or Recursos Naturales.
Carry your licence and your identity document on the boat.
Uruguay River: Concordia and Salto Grande
The IGFA records 25.28 kg (55 lb 11 oz) as the golden dorado all-tackle world record: according to the IGFA record page (read 13 November 2026), Andre L. S. de Botton caught it on 11 January 2006 in the Uruguay River at Concordia. El País (Uruguay, 13 January 2011) places the catch at the Salto Grande dam, so the press describes the place differently (more on the golden dorado species page
).
Concordia, in Entre Ríos, sits on the Uruguay River below the Salto Grande dam and is on our list of coming destinations (Uruguay River, golden dorado): it has no guide yet. The only months we have for this stretch come from our surubí season note
: Concordia guides talk about a surubí run from late September to mid-November, peaking in October, and say that with the dam gates open the river rose to 11 metres, against the usual 5. That is about surubí, not dorado: we have no source with dorado months for the Uruguay River.
What to bring for golden dorado fishing in Argentina: rod, reel, lines and flies
We do not recommend brands or products. This section gathers what three operators with lodges or posadas in Corrientes publish on their pages, each fact with the name of who says it (pages read on 15/11/2026). The lists differ from one operator to the next: before buying gear, check with yours.
Rod
For fly fishing, according to Nervous Waters (Suindá Lodge page, in Itatí), the rods are 9 feet: an 8-weight for dorado on a floating line, a 9-weight for larger dorado (floating or sinking line) and a 6- or 7-weight for pacú and pirá pitá. Set Fly Fishing lists a 9-foot 8-weight for dorado, a 6-weight for pacú and pirá pitá, and asks you to bring a backup. For spinning and baitcasting, Nervous Waters suggests medium to medium-heavy rods of 6 to 8 feet, rated for 8 to 20 lb line. Pousada Puerto Yacarey, in Itatí, speaks of «varas de ação média a pesada» (medium to heavy action rods).
Reel, lines and backing
Nervous Waters asks for good-quality reels with an excellent drag and at least 100 yards of 30 to 50 lb backing, and tropical lines (floating 6 or 7-weight, 8-weight and 9-weight, a 9-weight intermediate and a fast-sinking 275 to 350 grain). It warns against bringing cold-water floating lines, because the heat makes them soft and hard to cast. Set Fly Fishing asks for 150 yards of 30 lb backing; for the Upper Paraná, a tropical floating line and a tropical intermediate or intermediate-tip line; for the Esteros del Iberá, two tropical floating lines, one for the surface and one for streamers. For spinning, Nervous Waters suggests a 2000 to 3000 open-face reel or a fast-retrieve baitcaster, with 30 to 40 lb braid.
