Bariloche Fishing Guides

Fly Fishing Guides and Outfitters in Bariloche Patagonia Argentina since 1999


-Fly Fishing Trips in Northern Patagonia Argentina, San Carlos de Bariloche. Day Trips 5 to 10 hours, Camps and All inclusive Packages in the best rivers this Patagonia area, Limay, Manso, Collón Curá and Chimehuin Rivers.

-Our Goal is to provide fun, relax and memorable fishing moments, discovering and enjoying unique landscapes, in mountain and steppe rivers, with crystal clear waters and wild Brown, Rainbow and Brook trout.

-Most of our trips are made by Boat, Rowing and Drifting with the chance to stop and Wade in some of the best pools. It will be fun to Fish Together and we will listen to any Special Situation to organize your trip.

-Professional Fly Fishing Guides, operating in the provinces of Rio Negro and Neuquen, and the Nahuel Huapi and Lanin National Parks.
