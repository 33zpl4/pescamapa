Patagonia Fly Fishing Lodge, All-Inclusive
Our proposition is simple: show up, bring your layers, and fish. We like to say to our experienced
clients “You can travel as heavy or as light as you would like”, we’ve got the rest.
The lodging, the meals, all beverages, the guides, the gear/equipment, the shuttle from the airport,
already arranged and part of the package. One figure covers the trip. Choose Caridad Lodge
, a working sheep ranch at the base of the Andes with over 96 miles of private water. See the full lineup of lodges on Accommodations
.
Every package is sold as N Nights / N-1 Days. Our standard package is 7 Nights / 6 Days, arriving and departing on Saturday: seven
nights of lodging and six full days on the water. Arrival ends with the casting clinic and welcome
dinner; checkout ends at the last bite of a hot, chef prepared breakfast and the bottom of a coffee
cup. Everything in between is yours on the rivers.
We guide on the
Corcovado, Carrileufu, Rivadavia, and Futaleufú rivers
, plus
Arroyo Pescado, one of the world’s great spring creeks, and a network of private lake and
estancia water most anglers will never reach. Several of these waters are shared across more than one
lodge: which one you’re on a given day is a call your guide makes based on conditions, not a
fixed itinerary. Our
lodges’ own guides
are professional, multilingual, and
native to the water they fish.
What’s Included
Lodge accommodations at one or two of our lodges
Every meal: breakfast, shore lunch, and dinner
All beverages
A full day of guided fishing, one or two anglers per boat
Orvis and Simms loaner rods, reels, waders, boots
Flies, tippet, and leader, whatever the day calls for
On-property casting time whenever you want it
Airport transfers to/from Esquel (EQS) or Bariloche (BRC), facilitated by our Argentina-based travel team
Rates below are per guest, double occupancy, shared guide. Private guide days, single rooms, and
non-angling companion rates are available as you build your trip. For a different length or
arrival day (some lodges can accommodate one), private-water days, multi-family groups, or a
custom itinerary, contact us
and our travel team will confirm what
can be arranged.
