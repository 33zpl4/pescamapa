Challhuaquen Lodge Hosted Week, March 2027
$5,800.00
Challhuaquen Lodge, Trevelin, Argentina | 06–13 March 2027
Join Isolation Outfitters for a hosted week based on the banks of the Río Futaleufú in northern Patagonia. This Saturday-to-Saturday program includes seven nights and six full guided fishing days, giving you an additional day on the water to explore one of the region’s most varied trout programs.
Depending on conditions, the week may range across the Futaleufú, the Rivadavia and Arrayanes rivers, Lago Verde, Lago Krüger and the Estrecho de los Monstruos, Arroyo Pescado, the Corintos and Percey systems, with longer-range options towards the Corcovado and Río Pico country.
Hosted dates: 06–13 March 2027
Rate: US$5,800 per angler
Package: Seven nights, six guided fishing days, meals, beverages, Esquel transfers, licences and Isolation Outfitters hosting.
Places are strictly limited. Contact us to confirm availability and receive the complete booking pack. Please contact us if you would like to visit Challhuequen Lodge on different dates and we can advise on availability.
