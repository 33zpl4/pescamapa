How much does a day of fly fishing cost in Bariloche and the Limay River (2026/27)
Price for a guided fishing day on the Limay River: in US dollars and Argentine pesos, what's included, license cost, and what changes if there are two anglers.
By Equipo Pescamapa · 5 November 2026
Key facts
Half day on the Limay (about 6 hours): US$ 410 per boat with guide, for one or two anglers.
Full day on the Limay: from US$ 500 (one angler) and US$ 520 (two) with one outfitter, US$ 530 with another and from US$ 545 with an agency.
Full day on the Manso: US$ 600, which includes a US$ 70 supplement over the Limay.
In pesos, a guide on a booking platform lists $ 666,000 per day for up to two people (March 2027).
The license is usually not included. Official 2026/27 rates: foreigners $ 50,000 per day, $ 140,000 for 10 days and $ 280,000 for the season; residents $ 12,500, $ 25,000 and $ 50,000.

Short answer. A day of guided fly fishing near Bariloche costs from US$ 410 for a half day and from US$ 500 for a full day, per boat for one or two anglers. The Manso goes up to US$ 600. In pesos, a guide on a platform lists $ 666,000 per day. These are entry prices: what changes the price is below. The license is paid separately.

Why are there so many prices?
Because published prices are entry level, not a cap. They change with the river (the Manso adds US$ 70 to the Limay), with duration (half day is US$ 410 and full day from US$ 500) and with what each operator includes. Some operators also offer multi-day programs and float trips with camping and do not publish rates: you have to ask. We also did not find a published rate above US$ 600 for a day; others may charge more, but we can’t confirm it without a published price.
How much for two anglers?
The price is per boat, not per person. With the outfitter who lists both rates, one angler pays US$ 500 and two anglers in the same boat pay US$ 520 total: the second adds only US$ 20. Per person, going as a pair is almost half (our calculation: US$ 260 per person). With the full day on the Limay at US$ 530, it’s US$ 265 per person.
How much is the fishing license?
The Patagonian permit is valid in Neuquén, Río Negro, Chubut and National Parks, and its 2026/27 rates are official, in pesos: non-resident foreigners, $ 50,000 per day, $ 140,000 for 10 days and $ 280,000 for the season; residents, $ 12,500, $ 25,000 and $ 50,000 (regulations updated 26 October 2026). Some operators handle it for you and charge it separately: one reports US$ 40 per person per day. All rates, who is exempt and the trolling permit are in Patagonia fishing license 2026/27
