National Surubí Festival 2027 in Goya: dates, entry cap and prizes

The 50th edition, from 28 April to 2 May 2027 according to the official site: entry cap, registration, prizes and the contest rules. With sources and dates.

By Equipo Pescamapa · 16 November 2026

Key facts

  • The 50th National Surubí Festival (the “Golden Anniversary”) runs from 28 April to 2 May 2027 in Goya, Corrientes; it is organised by the Municipal Fishing Commission (Co.Mu.Pe.) with the Corrientes government and the Goya municipality (official site, read on 16/11/2026).
  • The fishing contest runs from Saturday 1 May (from 17:00) to Sunday 2 May 2027 (until 08:00) (2027 rules, art. 4).
  • Cap of 1,400 teams: it filled in two hours, with 1,413 registrations (4,239 anglers) and about 300 teams on a waiting list (Co.Mu.Pe., official notice).
  • Registration: ARS 1,350,000 in instalments; paying in full, from ARS 1,100,000 (August 2026) to ARS 1,350,000 (April 2027) (Co.Mu.Pe., registration-opening notice).
  • Prizes: “$1.000.000.000 in prizes, raffles and trophies” (banner of the official site); 1st in the contest, ARS 100,000,000; 1st for biggest fish, ARS 50,000,000.
  • A fish counts from 50 cm and is released alive; the fishing ground has 14 zones (2027 rules; official notice).
  • In 2026 (49th edition, 27/4 to 3/5) the biggest fish was a 119 cm surubí (Perfil, 4/5/2026).

Dates, registration and entry cap for 2027

  • Dates: 28 April to 2 May 2027, in Goya, Corrientes: the 50th edition, which Co.Mu.Pe. calls the “Gold Edition” and the “Golden Anniversary” (official site, 16/11/2026; notice). It is organised by Co.Mu.Pe., the Corrientes government and the Goya municipality.
  • The fishing contest: from Saturday 1 May 2027 at 17:00 to Sunday 2 May at 08:00. Boats set off between 13:45 and 15:00 depending on the group, and the zone draw is that Saturday at 07:00. If the weather prevents the start, the rules allow delaying it or, in an extreme case, cancelling the contest and drawing the material prizes among registered teams (2027 rules, arts. 4, 10 and 12).
  • Registration: opening was announced for Monday 10 August 2026 at 08:00, and on Tuesday 11 August at 11:00 the system was “enabled again” (opening notice; notice). It is through the official registration website: each team has two or three anglers (at least two must be entered), with each one’s tax ID (CUIT or CUIL) and sizes for the official clothing, and you pay in full or in instalments (notice; arts. 6 and 7).
  • Registration fee (per team, according to art. 7): ARS 1,350,000 in instalments, due on the 15th of each month; paying in full, ARS 1,100,000 if paid in August, rising monthly to ARS 1,350,000 in April 2027 (Co.Mu.Pe. notice).
  • Cap: 1,400 teams. Co.Mu.Pe. explains it by the capacity of the fishing zones and the closed park, safety, logistics, officiating and the capacity of the hall for the prize dinner: about 4,200 anglers, around 5,000 people with officials, logistics and authorities (official notice). The rules reserve the right to raise or lower the cap for exceptional reasons (art. 7).
  • What happened with the cap: 1,413 teams registered (4,239 anglers) and the cap filled in two hours; about 300 teams could not register and went on a waiting list. If Co.Mu.Pe. decides to raise the cap, it will do so with a draw among those on the list, before a notary and streamed live. The fishing ground stays at 14 zones (official notice).

Prizes for 2027

Co.Mu.Pe. presents the prize list as “unprecedented”, with a total purse “that will reach 1,000 million pesos” (notice); the banner of the official site says “$1.000.000.000 in prizes, raffles and trophies”. Elonce (10/8/2026) called it the highest figure in the event’s history. The list Co.Mu.Pe. published (an image inside the registration-opening notice, read on 16/11/2026) says, in Argentine pesos:

  • Contest (by points), 1st to 20th place: ARS 100,000,000 for 1st, ARS 50,000,000 for 2nd, ARS 25,000,000 for 3rd, ARS 20,000,000 for 4th and ARS 18,000,000 for 5th, going down to ARS 3,000,000 for 19th and 20th.
  • Biggest fish (by length), 1st to 15th place: ARS 50,000,000 for 1st, ARS 25,000,000 for 2nd and ARS 15,000,000 for 3rd, going down to ARS 3,000,000 for 14th and 15th.
  • Zones, 1st to 5th in each zone: ARS 5,000,000, ARS 3,300,000, ARS 2,100,000, ARS 1,800,000 and ARS 1,500,000.
  • Also: best-placed woman, best-placed minor, trophies and raffles.

We do not add up the list: the announced total also includes raffles and trophies.

How the contest works (2027 rules)

  • It is boat fishing on the Paraná river, left bank and tributaries, inside the fishing ground set by the Chief Official; zones are drawn by group according to engine power (groups A to N and a group V for large boats) (art. 10).
  • Teams have two or three anglers; minors under 18 go with an adult and written authorisation (arts. 6 and 7).
  • You fish from the boat anchored or tied up inside the assigned zone, without going ashore or moving the boat to land a fish. Each angler uses one rod with a single hook; a landing loop or net may be used to take fish out, and gaffs and the “bogagrip” are banned (arts. 8 and 14).
  • A fish counts from 50 cm; surubí under 50 cm are released at once. Each fish is measured with the organisers’ equipment and photographed on the “pescadómetro” and when it is released alive; a fish that cannot be released alive does not count, unless the official confirms causes beyond the team’s control (arts. 15 and 16).
  • Points by length of each surubí: 50 to under 60 cm, 1 point per 10 cm of length plus the fraction; 60 to under 80 cm, 5 points plus the same; 80 to under 100 cm, 10 points plus the same; 100 cm and over, 15 points plus the same. The rules’ own example: a 125 cm fish scores 27.5 points (art. 19).
  • Co.Mu.Pe. publishes the provisional score on goyasurubi.com at 13:00 on the day the contest ends (it can be challenged until 16:00) and the final score at 17:00 (art. 19).

In Goya every fish is released alive, which fits the Corrientes rule: surubí is catch and release only (Disposición 853/22).

What happened in 2026

The 49th edition ran from 27 April to 3 May 2026 with the Costa Surubí grounds as its centre, with more than 1,000 boats announced (Canal 26, 15/4/2026). According to Perfil (4/5/2026), about 1,400 boats took part; a team from Santa Fe won on accumulated points, with 42.40 points and 3 fish, and the biggest fish was a 119 cm surubí, officiated in Zone 11. We did not read the official results: the festival’s site links them to another site.

If you want to go

To compete in 2027 you must be on a registered team: the cap is full, there is a waiting list and only Co.Mu.Pe. decides whether to raise it (official notice); news is on the official site. If you want to go to Goya without competing, the festival’s description on the official site says the programme includes a commercial and craft show, a shore fishing contest and a palometa contest, sports competitions and free song and music festivals (that is the general description, not the 2027 programme). What the fish is called

In Argentina it is surubí. In Brazil, according to Embrapa Pesca e Aquicultura (December 2015), the species of the genus Pseudoplatystoma are popularly known as “surubim” or “pintado”, and the same name can refer to different species depending on the region. More on the surubí page.

National Surubí Festival: frequently asked questions

When is the National Surubí Festival 2027?

From 28 April to 2 May 2027, in Goya, Corrientes: it is the 50th edition, according to Co.Mu.Pe. (official site, read on 16/11/2026). The fishing contest runs from Saturday 1 May (from 17:00) to Sunday 2 May (until 08:00), according to the 2027 rules.

How many teams take part?

The cap is 1,400 teams. 1,413 registered (4,239 anglers) and the cap filled in two hours; about 300 teams went on a waiting list (Co.Mu.Pe., official notice). Each team has two or three anglers.

How do I enter and how much does it cost?

Through the official registration website, by team, with at least two anglers, paying in full or in instalments. The fee is ARS 1,350,000 in instalments; paying in full it was ARS 1,100,000 in August 2026 and rises to ARS 1,350,000 in April 2027 (Co.Mu.Pe.). The 2027 cap is full and there is a waiting list: ask Co.Mu.Pe. about an increase.

How much is the prize money?

The official site’s banner announces “$1.000.000.000 in prizes, raffles and trophies”. In Co.Mu.Pe.’s list, 1st in the contest gets ARS 100,000,000 and 1st for the biggest fish ARS 50,000,000.

Do you keep the surubí you catch in the contest?

No: under the 2027 rules a fish counts from 50 cm, is measured, photographed and released alive, and in Corrientes surubí is catch and release only (Disposición 853/22).

How big was the biggest fish of 2026?

A 119 cm surubí, officiated in Zone 11, according to Perfil (4/5/2026).
