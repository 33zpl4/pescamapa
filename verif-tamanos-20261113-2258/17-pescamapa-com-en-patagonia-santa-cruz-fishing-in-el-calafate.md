El Calafate is the base for fishing Lago Argentino, the Santa Cruz River, the La Leona River, the Calafate stream and the Bote River. This page gathers the 2026/27 rules read from the Santa Cruz regulation, the permit, what a guided day costs and what we have not read yet.
The 2026/27 season and general rules
According to the General Sport Fishing Regulation of Santa Cruz, the general season runs from 1 October 2026 to 30 September 2027, with specific dates for each water in Annex I. Fishing is with one artificial lure with one single or double hook, barbed or barbless. The fishing day runs from sunrise to sunset.
Disinfecting your gear. Article 18 of the General Regulation requires every angler entering the province to use new or previously disinfected equipment, and to disinfect waders, boots, gear, float tubes and vehicles when changing basin; felt-soled boots are only allowed if you comply. The portal’s procedure: remove vegetation and mud, soak in bleach or water above 60 °C, dry in the sun and wait 48 hours (Disposición N° 241/2026, read 13 November 2026).
Rules by water
Lago Argentino: open all year; two fish per angler per day; no fishing within 200 m of river inlets and outlets; trolling allowed, meeting the Coast Guard requirements.
Santa Cruz River: open all year; no natural bait (with exceptions) and no lead weights; a basin affected by Didymo, with mandatory disinfection before moving to another water; daily limit of two fish (one perch and one brown trout); one steelhead per season, with mandatory release from 15 March to 30 September 2027.
La Leona River: from 1 October 2026 to 3 May 2027; one chinook salmon per angler per day; all other species must be released; gear must be disinfected and washed at the end of the day.
Calafate stream: from 1 October 2026 to 3 May 2027; mandatory catch and release.
Bote River: same dates; mandatory catch and release, with a single hook.
The Santa Cruz River in detail
What Annex I says about the Santa Cruz River, on top of the above (Disposición 241/2026, Santa Cruz Official Gazette no. 6164 of 8/10/2026 and no. 6166 of 15/10/2026):
Hook: one single or double hook, barbed or barbless, with exceptions.
Authorised shore fishing zone: from the river access at the Héroes de Malvinas monument, near Bella Vista street, to the crossing of the Provincial Interconnected power line, on both banks (north and south); it includes Isla Pavón, on its bank from the Route 3 bridge to the area locally known as “La Noria”, which will be marked with signs.
Chinook salmon: a short season from 1 August to 30 November 2027, with a free lure with one single or double hook, barbed or barbless; daily limit of one fish per angler, and every other species caught must be released. Shore zone: from the urban access at Bella Vista street to the crossing of the Provincial Interconnected power line.
Róbalo (Eleginops maclovinus): all year, with spinning, fly or bottom line with up to three hooks and free bait; no lead. Up to ten per angler per day, minimum size 30 cm total length; it is the only species that may be kept and all others are released. Zone: from the river access at the Héroes de Malvinas monument, near Bella Vista street, to the perimeter fence of Estancia La Esperanza (north bank).
Trolling: only for fishing guides registered in the R.P.A.T., with approved motorised floating units that meet Article 16, on two stretches: from the Route 3 bridge to the Puerto Santa Cruz water intake (lower part) and from the Chacra de Teira (Tapera Los Frutales) to the Interconnected power line crossing (upper part).
Total closure at the hydroelectric works: the stretch from Estancia El Rincón (“Los Laberintos” area) to “La Barrancosa”, bounded by the Cóndor Cliff work camps (70° 07′ W) and the point fixed at 70° 10′ W.
The permit and what it costs
You need the Santa Cruz permit, bought online on the official portal. For a non-resident foreigner in 2026/27: $ 90,000 for one day, $ 300,000 for a week and $ 450,000 for the season (about US$ 58, US$ 194 and US$ 291 at US$ 1 = $ 1,545, as of 8 November 2026). What a guided day costs
An El Calafate agency lists $ 440,000 for a half day (5 hours) and $ 720,000 for a full day (9 hours) per angler, as of 5 November 2026. An operator lists the fishing day on a booking platform at US$ 340 per adult (11 November 2026), with the permit, gear, insurance and hotel pickup included. According to that operator the fishing is wading only, never from a boat, the season runs from November to April and typical catches weigh between 500 grams and 2 kilos.

How to choose a guide: what to ask before booking
Style: does the guide fish bait, spinning/baitcasting or fly? Pick one who specialises in what you want to do.
Boat and gear: what boat, how many anglers it takes, which engine (horsepower, bow electric motor)? Casting platform, fish finder? Is tackle provided?
What is included: bait, meals, fuel, lodging, transfers. Get it in writing, with the price.
Timing rules: the month decides the fishing. Ask what works on your dates, at what river level and water temperature.
The place: where the lodging is, boat ramp, distance to the fishing areas and the overall setting.
References: photos of recent trips and anglers who have been. If you can, talk to the owner or operator.
