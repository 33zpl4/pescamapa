Only golden dorado is fished, catch and release, from authorised boats, Monday to Thursday in daylight, and you must book with the permit holder each country authorises. The golden dorado closed season on the Uruguay River runs from 1 September to 31 December and we found no text that exempts the zone.
What La Zona is and where it starts
“La Zona” is what anglers call the Safety Zone downstream of the Salto Grande Dam on the Uruguay River, between Concordia (Argentina) and Salto (Uruguay). The regulated sport-fishing area runs from an imaginary line parallel to the dam axis, 500 metres downstream, to the end of the safety zone (CARU Catch-and-Release Fishing Regulation, Resolution 27/2004 as amended, updated text read on 13 November 2026). The line has changed twice:
500 m since CARU Resolution 46/16 (22 September 2016).
200 m since CARU Resolution 01/2023 (5 January 2023; published in the Argentine Boletín Oficial on 7 March 2023).
500 m again since CARU Resolution 63/2026, signed on 21 September 2026 and published in the Boletín Oficial on 28 September 2026, at the request of the Salto Grande Joint Technical Commission, which cites the safety of the dam, of navigation and of people.
We do not state a total length for the zone: no CARU text we read gives one.
Who regulates it
CARU, the Uruguay River Administrative Commission (binational), issues the regulation. The resolution that approved the regulation leaves permits, awards and enforcement to each State, and each State may authorise one permit holder (art. 4.2 a). The enforcing authorities are, in Argentina, the Entre Ríos Dirección de Recursos Naturales (now the Dirección de Recursos Naturales y Fiscalización) and, in Uruguay, the Dirección Nacional de Recursos Acuáticos (DINARA), together with the Naval Prefectures of both countries (art. 2.1).
The rules in the regulation
These are the articles of the CARU regulation, in its updated text:
Golden dorado only, and catch and release only (arts. 5.1 and 10.3). Fishing contests, which the authority authorises, are the exception to the species rule.
Only from authorised boats: no fishing from the shore and no landing, except in an emergency (art. 3.1).
Artificial lure or fly (fly casting, spinning and bait casting), with a barbless hook or one with the barb crushed or filled; no bait and no trolling (arts. 7.1 and 8.1). One rod per angler (art. 9.1).
No more than two minutes out of the water for each fish (art. 10.3).
Up to four boats at a time, no more than two per country per shift; up to four people on board, guide and skipper included; boats of 4.50 to 6.50 m in length and 15 km/h at most (art. 11).
Monday to Thursday, daylight only, since 1 June 2023 (CARU Resolution 37/2023, Boletín Oficial of 11 April 2023). Earlier texts (the 2018 CARU Digesto and a 2020 Uruguayan call, among them) say Friday to Monday.
Clients: book with the permit holder and carry documents that prove that link and their identity; the permit holder notifies the Prefectura of its clients’ identities at least 48 hours ahead (art. 4.2 b and c).
The golden dorado closed season: how it applies here
CARU sets the golden dorado closed season on the Uruguay River from 1 September to 31 December every year, for commercial and sport fishing (CARU Resolution 111/2023, of 1 August 2023; CARU notice of 7 October 2026). The La Zona regulation says its season “will follow what CARU establishes on the Uruguay River” (our translation). We found no text that exempts the zone from the closed season, and none that confirms it applies inside: it stays an open question (below). An agency site that sells trips to La Zona Lodge lists September to November as “mid-season” and calls La Zona a year-round fishery; ask the operator, in writing, how the closed season applies to your dates. The Concordia municipality tourism page gives different dates (16 October to 14 January, with release); the CARU resolution is the norm, not that page.
Who can take you: what is unresolved
To fish La Zona you must book with the permit holder of one of the two countries. Argentine side: the 2023 call was declared failed by Entre Ríos Decree 3874/25 (20 December 2025; Boletín Oficial of 28 January 2026), because CARU Resolution 207/2023 banned tagging fish and that changed the terms of the call. A new tender document then appears, Concurso Público de Méritos y Antecedentes N° 01-2026 (ten-year permit, up to two boats, fishing Monday to Thursday). As of 13 November 2026 we found no published award.
What the market offers. La Zona Lodge (Concordia) offers, according to its own site, fishing from boats below the Salto Grande dam. On the Uruguayan side, River Plate Dorados says on its own site that in 2013 it acquired at a public auction the exclusive right to use the only two boats; that it is on the Uruguayan side comes from an agency site. We could not verify from an official text which permit covers each of them in 2026, and we do not say either is missing one. On the Uruguayan side there was also a call in 2020 (Resolution 795/2020 of the Ministry of Livestock, Agriculture and Fisheries, 12 August 2020) for one permit holder for five years; we did not read its outcome.

What to ask before you pay
Which permit covers the trip on your dates? Ask for the resolution or decree number.
Which days will you fish? The regulation says Monday to Thursday.
How many anglers per boat? Four people fit on board, guide and skipper included.
How does the 1 September to 31 December closed season apply to your dates? Ask in writing.
Will the permit holder notify the Prefectura of your details 48 hours ahead?
Getting there and how the river is
Concordia is about 430 km from Buenos Aires, according to El Cronista (article read on 13 November 2026), a little over five hours by road. Dam releases change the river. The Salto Grande Joint Technical Commission publishes a daily report of flows and levels (PDF)
. Example, reading at 09:00 on 13 November 2026: Concordia level 8.57 m; total discharge from Salto Grande, daily mean, 11,787 m³/s. It is that day’s figure, not a forecast. We do not have a river-level page for Concordia yet.
The record
The IGFA all-tackle world record for the golden dorado is 25.28 kg (55 lb 11 oz), caught on 11/1/2006 by Andre L. S. de Botton in the Uruguay River at Concordia, Argentina, according to the IGFA record page (read 13 November 2026). El País (Uruguay, 13 January 2011) places the catch at the Salto Grande dam: the press describes the place differently. More on the golden dorado species page
and in our golden dorado fishing guide

Frequently asked questions
What is La Zona in Argentina?
It is what anglers call the Safety Zone downstream of the Salto Grande Dam on the Uruguay River, between Concordia (Argentina) and Salto (Uruguay). The CARU regulation allows catch-and-release fishing for golden dorado from a line 500 m from the dam to the end of that zone (Resolution 27/2004 as amended; the 500 m applies since Resolution 63/2026).
Can anyone fish La Zona?
No. You fish only from authorised boats and by booking with the permit holder each country authorises, one per State; shore fishing is not allowed. Clients carry documents proving their link with the permit holder and their identity, and the permit holder notifies the Prefectura at least 48 hours ahead. As of 13 November 2026 we found no published record of who holds the Argentine-side permit.
Which days can you fish La Zona?
Monday to Thursday and in daylight only, since 1 June 2023 (CARU Resolution 37/2023). Earlier texts, such as the 2018 CARU Digesto, say Friday to Monday.
When is the golden dorado closed season on the Uruguay River?
1 September to 31 December every year, for commercial and sport fishing (CARU Resolution 111/2023; CARU notice of 7 October 2026). We found no text that exempts La Zona, and the regulation says its season follows what CARU establishes: ask the operator, in writing, how it applies to your dates.
