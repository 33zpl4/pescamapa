Many people come to Patagonia just to fish. Indeed, multitudes of lakes and rivers with clear waters are the great delight of fans of this sport.
Angling or fly fishing, there’s no shortage of different options. Many estancias also offer fishing-related activities. You’ll then be in the heart of thousands of hectares just for you, with your guide. You’ll also have the privilege of fishing in Tierra del Fuego, on the Río Grande, where the largest trout in the world await you. Here are some examples of fishing trips that I offer you, knowing that many other fishing trips are possible and in other regions than those presented below.
Exclusive fishing trip
Moreno and Nahuel Huapi lakes
Pictures
Fishing and discovery tour
Exclusive and little-known excursion. Departing from Bariloche, we start with Lago Moreno Este, Lago Moreno Oeste, Lago Morenito, Isla Conejo, Bahia López, Lago Nahuel Huapi and Brazo Tristeza. For lunch, a typical meal is organized at the end of the “Brazo Tristeza”, which is a wild area untouched by any human presence.
Then, a walk among the imposing trees native to the region, to the Cascada Frey. It’s also possible, as an option, to go to Frey Lake, camp there and fish there. The scenery between the cliffs and the flight of the condors is grandiose… Unforgettable!!!
Fly fishing in Patagonia
Río Limay
Pictures
The enchanted valley
The Limay River is one of the best known for its splendor and its qualities for fly fishing. For this trip, we sail on the Limay River “El Río Limay” to fish at different points of the river, very close to Bariloche in Valle Encantado.
I offer you the best conditions for this angling trip with an exclusive service. On a large private portion of the Limay River, you’ll be accompanied by a guide who will advise you. You’ll also have the opportunity to spend the night in a very close Estancia to stay immersed in this pleasant natural environment. An exclusive Borispatagonia activity!

The lakes and rivers of Bariloche
In the San Carlos de Bariloche
area there are a multitude of heavenly places for fishing lovers. There’s something for everyone and the programs can be tailor-made, according to your wishes.
Fishing in the lakes and rivers around Bariloche is unforgettable and will satisfy all enthusiasts.
Fishing and panoramas
Lago Fonck
Pictures
Andean lake of glacial origin
Undoubtedly a paradise for fishing fanatics: Lake Fonck… access is earned because you need a 4×4 or good legs to complete the last 5 kilometers. Once at Lake Fonck, there’s only fishing as an activity, possibly a boat trip…
In addition to being a great place to practice salmon sport fishing, there are very beautiful views of the Tronador volcano
, so it’s the best of the best – for those who want the best in fishing!
Fishing in Tierra del Fuego
Tierra del Fuego
Pictures
From river to river
Tierra del Fuego
is a unique destination, the land of Ushuaia
, where the light will surprise you from sunrise to sunset, where people will seduce you with their simplicity, their work and their festivals, where nature can enchant you, so much is different from anything you know.
Experience the most beautiful fishing of your life, the one you dream of… Share the passion for trout fishing – Camp de río en río in individual tents – Exchange, in the evening, around a wood fire – Adapt to nature, to encounters – Travel this land at the end of the world in 4×4 – Discover unique landscapes with a Fuegian guide.
