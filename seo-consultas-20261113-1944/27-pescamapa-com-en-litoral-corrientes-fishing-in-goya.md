Why Goya
Goya sits in southern Corrientes province on the Paraná and is known as the home of the surubí. The islands and side channels facing the town are this catfish’s habitat, which gave rise to the National Surubí Festival: in 2026 it drew 1,400 teams, and it has been catch and release since 2004.
But Goya is more than the tournament. The province describes it as a mosaic of channels, side channels, sandbanks, lagoons and wetlands, with half-day, full-day and multi-day trips. The Paraná–Isoró system adds appeal for longer trips and non-fishing companions: capybaras, caimans, waterbirds and black howler monkeys are often seen along the way.
The water: Paraná, riacho and Isoró
The riacho Goya is an arm of the Paraná right next to town, and its flow depends on the river level. Further out are the deep holes and drop-offs of the Paraná. Inland, the Isoró is a protected wetland and fish breeding area: official information promotes it mainly for boating and wildlife watching. Check permits and zones before fishing there.
There is no official map of permitted fishing spots. That is why Pescamapa shows areas, not exact fishing marks, and never presents a spot as permitted without confirmation.
Species and techniques
The surubí is the signature species. Golden dorado and mixed river species round out the fishing, and provincial promotion also mentions pacú, manguruyú and armado. For Corrientes as a whole, the province mentions spinning, trolling and fly fishing. The right technique in Goya depends on the day, the species and the guide: confirm it with your outfitter.
Golden dorado is a Provincial Natural Monument in Corrientes (Law 6730): it is always catch and release.
When to go: read the river before the calendar
The level and trend of the Paraná change everything. Before you travel, check the INA river alert system
. For example, on 18 October 2026 Goya read 5.28 m and rising, above the alert level (5.20 m). That reading is only a snapshot of that day.
How the river level changes the fishing. When the river drops, the side channels drain into the main channel and fish gather at creek mouths and in the deep holes of the Paraná: that is when lures and trolling work best. When it rises, water floods the islands and marshes, fish spread out to feed in the flooded forest, and it pays to look for them along the banks and in deep side channels.
By species (it shifts every year with the river; check with your guide): golden dorado fishes best from October to April in warmer water, in rapids, lagoon mouths and sandbanks; surubí is mostly targeted from March to August in deep holes and drop-offs, with live bait, anchored or drifting; pacú and boga show up in summer, December to March, near floating vegetation and tree-lined banks.
Choosing a guide
Ask before you book:
Which water and species they suggest for your dates, and how the current river level affects it.
How long the trip lasts, where you launch and what the price includes: fuel, tackle, bait, food and transfers.
Whether the guide is licensed, whether they help with your licence and what happens if the weather turns.
What safety gear the boat carries (a life jacket for each person) and whether fishing is catch and release.
Surubí week
The 50th National Surubí Festival is announced for 28 April to 2 May 2027. If you plan to compete, read the rules and follow COMUPE’s announcements. If you’re going for the festival, book lodging and transport early and check cancellation policies: it’s the busiest week of the year.
Getting there
Goya is reached via national route RN 12 and provincial route RP 27, about 220 km from the city of Corrientes and about 715 km from Buenos Aires. Check roadworks and road conditions before you set out.

Licence, closures and regulations
Fishing in Corrientes requires a sport fishing licence, issued online through the provincial Natural Resources portal, with categories for bank and boat fishing and by residence (Corrientes, rest of Argentina, foreign visitors). Carry the receipt and your ID. Residents of Santa Fe, Entre Ríos or Chaco with a valid licence can fish in Corrientes under the regional mutual-recognition agreement, following local rules; check its scope before you travel. Golden dorado is a Provincial Natural Monument (Law 6730) and is catch and release. In the Ysoró creek, harvesting fish is banned: catch and release only (Law 6655). For surubí, the national reference minimum size is 85 cm; check the current Corrientes rule. The province sets spawning closures and, when needed, extraordinary closures on stretches of the Paraná (the 2026 ones were upstream, in the Alto Paraná, far from Goya). Always check the current ones for the stretch you plan to fish.

How to choose a guide: what to ask before booking
Style: does the guide fish bait, spinning/baitcasting or fly? Pick one who specialises in what you want to do.
Boat and gear: what boat, how many anglers it takes, which engine (horsepower, bow electric motor)? Casting platform, fish finder? Is tackle provided?
What is included: bait, meals, fuel, lodging, transfers. Get it in writing, with the price.
Timing rules: the month decides the fishing. Ask what works on your dates, at what river level and water temperature.
The place: where the lodging is, boat ramp, distance to the fishing areas and the overall setting.
References: photos of recent trips and anglers who have been. If you can, talk to the owner or operator.

Frequently asked questions
Do I need to hire a guide to fish in Goya?Not for bank fishing: according to the municipality it isn’t mandatory. You still need a licence and must respect local restrictions, such as the waterfront ban. For boat fishing, and especially in the Isoró, the municipality recommends licensed guides.
What species can you catch in Goya?The province’s official promotion lists surubí, golden dorado, pacú, manguruyú, armado and mixed river species. It is a list of signature species, not a catch guarantee: your guide confirms what to target on each date and with which technique.
