El Viracho Pesca

Fishing Outings

Mons. Tavella 1306, Concordia, Entre Ríos

Phone
Mobile +54 9 345 5281035 Phone +54 345-4216047

In Concordia, Entre Ríos, 430 kilometers away from Buenos Aires, there lies the best fishing environment to catch dorados, bogas, tarariras and varied.

Amidst falls, rocks and streams, each corner of the river hides a secret we invite you to discover with us and our twenty years in the market.

EL Viracho Pesca offers an outing into this astonishing world where anglers can experience maximum sensations.


Spinning, bait-casting, fly-casting.
Dorados, bogas, tarariras and varied.
5.20-meter-long boats with 4-cycle engines.
Accommodation and other services.
