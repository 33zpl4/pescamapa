The finest fly fishing guide service in Bariloche, Patagonia Argentina.

We are based in San Carlos de Bariloche, located in the heart of the Nahuel Huapi National Park and near many of the most famous Patagonia fly fishing rivers. 

Available options range from Daily Fishing Trips to Overnight Float Trips and full service All-Inclusive Packages… all in private, with all-inclusive and first-class lodging.

Customized trips. We've been guiding fly fishers of all levels of experience in Patagonia for more than 20 years... let our experience lead you to the best fly fishing in Argentina. Whether you're inclined to fish a day or a week, remote streams or floating and camping expeditions, we can customize and create the perfect fishing experience.

Get access to more water than any other outfitter in the area. We partner with several of the best ranches in the region, which provide first-class lodging and unparalleled access to the best Fly Fishing in Bariloche Patagonia Argentina. We tailor our trips to your needs and experience and pick the best waters based on the timing and length of your stay.

The advantage of traveling with us. We know that rivers change during the seasons, and so do the behaviors of trout. That's why we're constantly on the move looking for the best fishing. If the river is too high, we'll move to another one. We'll take you to rarely fished waters, far from civilization and only accesible by floating or multi-day camping trips. With us you're not just getting a fishing trip... you will experience an adventure!

Non-Anglers. We offer a variety of activities to choose from, such as hiking, rafting, horseback riding, etc. So you can find the perfect one for your interests and needs. Send us an email for further information.
