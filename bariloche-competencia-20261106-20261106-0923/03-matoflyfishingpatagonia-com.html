Fly Fishing Trips in Bariloche, Patagonia Argentina

Experience world-class fly fishing in Argentine Patagonia with Mato Fly Fishing Patagonia, your local Bariloche fly fishing guide. Based in the heart of Nahuel Huapi National Park, we offer fully guided fly fishing trips on pristine lakes and rivers, home to wild Rainbow Trout and Brown Trout.

Whether you prefer fishing from a drift boat or wading crystal-clear rivers, every trip is customized to the best seasonal conditions, ensuring an unforgettable day on the water surrounded by the breathtaking Andes Mountains. During the late season, anglers can pursue legendary migratory Brown Trout—one of the most exciting and sought-after fly fishing experiences in Argentina.

Join us for an authentic Patagonia fly fishing adventure and discover why Bariloche is considered one of South America's premier destinations for wild trout fishing.

Why Fish With Mato Fly Fishing Patagonia?

Every trip is tailored to current river and lake conditions, seasonal hatches, and your personal fishing goals. As a local Bariloche fly fishing guide, I spend much of the season on these waters, allowing me to choose the best destination each day and give every guest the best possible opportunity to experience Patagonia's exceptional trout fishing.
