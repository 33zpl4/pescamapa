Fly fishing in Bariloche

Where passion and good company meet

Because we are as passionate about fishing as you are, we want to host your next fly fishing trip and share our experience on the rivers and lakes around Bariloche, in the heart of Patagonia, Argentina.

Every day begins with one practical question: where will the conditions create the best experience? We evaluate the water, recommend the right destination and shape the day around you.

From Bariloche, explore a full-day fly fishing trip on the Limay or a full-day trip on the Manso. We help you choose around the season, river conditions and your preferences.

Experience built over time

Local knowledge
Thoughtful service

We are not just anglers showing you around Bariloche. For nearly 20 years, we have been running guided fly fishing trips full-time in Patagonia, combining local knowledge with thoughtful, personal service.

Before every trip, we evaluate river and lake conditions and recommend the water offering the best experience that day. The final choice is always yours, and we provide the guidance, equipment and support needed from start to finish.

Whether you are learning your first cast or searching for your next memorable trout, we make the day safe, exciting and genuinely enjoyable—with good fishing, friendship and outstanding food along the way.
