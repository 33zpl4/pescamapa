La Paz is the seat of La Paz department, in Entre Ríos, on the bank of the Paraná River. It is a different province from Corrientes: the rules and the licence are those of Entre Ríos. The most sought-after species are the golden dorado and the surubí.
Fishing licence in Entre Ríos
You need a sport fishing licence to fish in Entre Ríos. According to the permits and licences table of the Entre Ríos Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate (consulted on 22/10/2026), the fees are:
Annual licence for Entre Ríos residents: $15,444.44 (Argentine pesos).
Annual licence for residents of other provinces: $24,251.60.
4-day temporary permit for residents of other provinces: $5,488.52.
Permit for foreigners (5 days): $24,251.60.
Fees are adjusted, and according to the press the province last updated them on 29 May 2026: confirm the amount when you apply for the licence through the Government of Entre Ríos procedure
. There is also a regional agreement for reciprocal recognition of licences in the Litoral that includes Entre Ríos (Resolution 0336/25 of Santa Fe): if you hold a licence from another province in the region, confirm the conditions with the Directorate before you travel.
Sizes, bag limits and closed seasons
We could not confirm online the current resolution on minimum sizes, bag limits and closed seasons in Entre Ríos, so we do not publish them. Before you go, ask the Entre Ríos Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate or your guide, who knows what applies that year. Spawning closures and extraordinary closures change every year.
Festivals and tournaments
The Fiesta Provincial del Dorado Entrerriano (Entre Ríos Provincial Dorado Festival) stands out: in 2026 it was its 33rd edition, on 25 and 26 September, with the 12th lure fishing meet (on the 25th) and the 33rd bait fishing meet (on the 26th). The 2026 entry fee was $550,000 per modality if paid by 31/08 (in up to 3 instalments) and $600,000 after that date ($800,000 for both modalities), according to the official poster. La Paz also has other festivals and tournaments. The dates of the next edition have not been published yet: they are announced by the Municipality of La Paz
and Turismo La Paz

Getting there
La Paz is on national route 12, north of Paraná. To go out in your own boat you need the nautical permit and clearance from the Prefectura. The river level is checked in the INA alert system.

How to choose a guide: what to ask before booking
Style: does the guide fish bait, spinning/baitcasting or fly? Pick one who specialises in what you want to do.
Boat and gear: what boat, how many anglers it takes, which engine (horsepower, bow electric motor)? Casting platform, fish finder? Is tackle provided?
What is included: bait, meals, fuel, lodging, transfers. Get it in writing, with the price.
Timing rules: the month decides the fishing. Ask what works on your dates, at what river level and water temperature.
The place: where the lodging is, boat ramp, distance to the fishing areas and the overall setting.
References: photos of recent trips and anglers who have been. If you can, talk to the owner or operator.

Frequently asked questions
Which licence do I need to fish in La Paz, Entre Ríos?The sport fishing licence of Entre Ríos. You get it through the procedures portal of the Government of Entre Ríos, and the fees are set by the Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate.
How much does the licence cost for an Argentine tourist?According to the table of the Entre Ríos Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate (consulted on 22/10/2026): annual licence for residents of other provinces, $24,251.60; 4-day temporary permit, $5,488.52. Foreigners: 5-day permit, $24,251.60. Fees are adjusted: confirm them when you apply.
Is a licence from another province valid?There is a regional agreement for reciprocal recognition of licences in the Litoral that includes Entre Ríos (Resolution 0336/25 of Santa Fe). Confirm the exact conditions with the Entre Ríos Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate before you travel.
What size and bag limits apply in La Paz?We could not confirm online the current resolution on sizes, bag limits and closed seasons in Entre Ríos, so we do not publish them. Ask the Entre Ríos Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate or your guide before you go.
Are there closed seasons?Yes, there can be spawning closures and extraordinary closures. The dates change every year and we could not confirm them online: ask the Entre Ríos Natural Resources and Enforcement Directorate.
When is the fishing festival in La Paz?The Fiesta Provincial del Dorado Entrerriano stands out. In 2026 it was its 33rd edition, on 25 and 26 September: on the 25th the 12th lure fishing meet, and on the 26th the 33rd bait fishing meet, with prizes and draws worth more than $40,000,000 according to the official poster. 2026 entry fee: $550,000 per modality until 31/08 (in up to 3 instalments) and $600,000 after that ($800,000 for both modalities). The date of the next edition has not been published yet, and La Paz also has other festivals and tournaments.
How do I get to La Paz?By national route 12, north of the city of Paraná.
